## Part C

12. ***O**n **M**onday **I** visit my aunt in **C**airo. **S**he speaks **A**rabic and **F**rench.*
    - **O**n — first word of a sentence.
    - **M**onday — day.
    - **I** — always capital.
    - **C**airo — city.
    - **S**he — first word after a full stop.
    - **A**rabic, **F**rench — languages.
    - Review [L-004](../../00-foundations/02-letters-and-sounds/lesson-004-capital-and-small-letters.md).
13. **winter** — seasons usually take small letters. (*July, Friday, Mexico* need capitals.) — L-004.

## Part E

18. — L-009
    a) ❌ — no verb. Fix: *The old man **is** in the garden.*
    b) ✅ — who (*the old man*) + verb (*is*).
    c) ✅ — an instruction; the hidden "who" is *you*.
    d) ❌ — no who and no verb. Fix: *I **am** very tired today.*
19. a) **Exclamation** · b) **Question** · c) **Instruction** · d) **Statement**. — L-009.
20. ***M**y name is **O**mar**.** **I** am twelve years old**.** **W**here do you live**?***
    - 3 ideas → 3 sentences.
    - The last one is a question → **?**
    - **O**mar is a name → capital.
    - Review [L-010](../../00-foundations/04-what-is-a-sentence/lesson-010-capital-letter-and-full-stop.md).
21.
    a) *I **am** ten **years old**.* — English uses *am/is/are* for age. — L-010.
    b) *Where **is the station**?* — in a question, **is** comes before the thing. — L-009.
    c) *I am hungry**. I** want to eat.* — a comma cannot join two complete sentences. Also correct: *I am hungry, **so** I want to eat.* — L-010.

## Part F — Example answer

> *My name is Lina. I am fifteen years old. I live in Amman. I speak Arabic.*

Check yourself:
- 4 sentences, each with a **capital letter** at the start and a **full stop** at the end.
- Names (*Lina, Amman, Arabic*) have capitals.
- Each sentence has a **verb**: *is, am, live, speak*.
- Age: **I am** ___ **years old** (not *I have*).
