## Part A — Irregular plurals (L-025)

### A1. Write the plural.

1. man 2. woman 3. child 4. person 5. foot 6. tooth 7. mouse 8. sheep 9. fish 10. goose 11. deer 12. policeman

### A2. Choose the correct word.

1. Three (childs / children) are playing.
2. (People / Peoples) are very friendly here.
3. My (foots / feet) are cold.
4. There are ten (sheep / sheeps) in the field.
5. The (women / womens) are doctors.
6. Many people (live / lives) in this building.

### A3. Make plural. Change all the partners.

1. This child is my son.
2. A woman is waiting outside.
3. My tooth hurts.
4. That person is very kind.

### A4. Error correction.

1. The childrens are at school.
2. I have two foots.
3. People is happy today.
4. Where are the mans?

### A5. Pronunciation 🗣️ — Say aloud: *woman / women* · *child / children* · *foot / feet*. Which pair changes its **first** vowel sound most?

## Part B — Countable and uncountable (L-026)

### B1. C (countable) or U (uncountable)?

1. chair 2. furniture 3. bag 4. luggage 5. job 6. work 7. coin 8. money 9. information 10. fact 11. bread 12. sandwich

### B2. *a / an* or *some*?

1. ___ egg 2. ___ milk 3. ___ apple 4. ___ advice 5. ___ cheese 6. ___ banana

### B3. *is* or *are*?

1. The information ___ correct.
2. The chairs ___ new.
3. The news ___ bad.
4. My luggage ___ heavy.
5. The eggs ___ in the fridge.
6. The furniture ___ old.

### B4. *How much* or *How many*?

1. ___ water do you drink?
2. ___ brothers do you have?
3. ___ money do you need?
4. ___ homework do you have?
5. ___ people are coming?

### B5. Units — complete with: *glass · piece · slice · loaf · bottle · cup*

1. a ___ of bread (one whole bread)
2. a ___ of water (from a shop)
3. a ___ of advice
4. a ___ of tea
5. a ___ of cake
6. a ___ of milk (to drink now)

### B6. Error correction.

1. Can you give me an information?
2. I have many homeworks.
3. We bought new furnitures.
4. How was your travel?
5. I have a work in a bank.
6. There are some milk in the fridge.

### B7. Same word, two meanings — explain the difference.

1. *I like **chicken**.* / *We have three **chickens**.*
2. *I don't have **time**.* / *I called you three **times**.*
