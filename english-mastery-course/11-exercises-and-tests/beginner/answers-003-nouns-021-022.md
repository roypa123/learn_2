## Part A — L-021

### A1.
1. **cat, sofa** — *cat* = subject; *sofa* after *on* (P).
2. **father, bus, city** — S, O, P.
3. **Happiness** — abstract noun (idea) as subject. *important* is an adjective.
4. **children, fruit, breakfast** — S, O, P (after *for*).
5. **advice** — after *your* → noun. (*Thank you* is a phrase; *you* is a pronoun.)
- Rule: [L-021 §7.2–7.3](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-021-what-is-a-noun.md#72-four-tests-to-find-a-noun).

### A2.
1. **place** 2. **idea** (feeling) 3. **person** 4. **thing** 5. **animal** 6. **idea** (event/time)
- Why: groups 1–4 can be seen/touched; group 5 (ideas) cannot.

### A3.
1. **S** (before *need*) 2. **O** (*like what?*) 3. **B** (after *is*) 4. **P** (after *on*)

### A4.
1. **noun** — after *a*.
2. **verb** — instruction: *Please* + verb.
3. **noun** — after *a nice*.
4. **verb** — after *We*.
5. **noun** — after *some*.
- Rule: after *a/the/my/some* → noun; after *I/we/they/please* → verb ([L-021 §7.5](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-021-what-is-a-noun.md#75-same-word-noun-or-verb)).
- How to avoid the mistake: always look at the word **just before**.

### A5.
1. head **box** → **is**. (*toys* is not the head.)
2. head **children** → **are**.
3. head **bag** → **is**.
- Rule: the verb agrees with the **head noun** ([L-021 §7.4](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-021-what-is-a-noun.md#74-noun-phrases)).

### A6.
1. *She is **a** teacher.* — jobs need *a/an*.
2. *I have **a** car.* — one countable noun needs *a/the/my*.
3. ***Love** is beautiful.* — general ideas: no *the*.
4. *Thanks for your **help**.* — use the noun.

### A7. (Example answers — many are possible.)
1. *The doctor works in a hospital.*
2. *The dog is in the garden.*
3. *That's a good idea!*
- Check: subject + verb ✅, *a/the* before countable singular nouns ✅.

## Part B — L-022

### B1.
1. **C** 2. **P** 3. **P** 4. **C** (season) 5. **C** 6. **P** 7. **P** 8. **C**

### B2.
1. ***M**y brother lives in **N**ew **Y**ork.* — first word; both words of the city name.
2. ***W**e have a holiday in **A**ugust.* — month; *holiday* stays small.
3. ***M**rs **G**reen teaches maths and **S**panish.* — title + name; *maths* small; *Spanish* (language) capital.
4. ***I** love **J**apanese food.* — *I*; nationality adjective.
5. ***N**ext **F**riday we are going to the **L**ouvre in **P**aris.* — day; famous building (*the* stays small); city.
- Rule: [L-022 §7.1–7.2](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-022-common-and-proper-nouns.md#71-kinds-of-proper-nouns).

### B3.
1. **the** Nile (river) 2. **–** Japan 3. **the** USA (*States*) 4. **–** Mount Everest (one mountain) 5. **the** Alps (range) 6. **–** London 7. **the** Pacific (ocean) 8. **–** Lake Como (lake)
- Rule: [L-022 §7.4](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-022-common-and-proper-nouns.md#74-proper-nouns-and-the). Tip: learn each name with its article.

### B4.
1. **Mum** — used as her name.
2. **dad** — after *my* → common noun.
3. **Riverside School** — a name.
4. **school** — general word, after *a big*.

### B5.
1. *We visited **Italy** last **s**ummer.* — no *the* with *Italy*; seasons small.
2. *My **f**riend Ali is from **E**gypt.* — *friend* common; *Egypt* proper.
3. *The **Smiths** live next door.* — the whole family → plural *Smiths*.
4. *I study English, French and **h**istory.* — only languages get capitals among subjects.

### B6.
1. *china* (small) = plates/cups of fine white clay; *China* (capital) = the country.
2. The second: **May** = the month (proper noun). The first *May* is a helping verb; its capital is only because it's the first word.

## Part C — Mixed

### C1.
- (a) Proper: **Nadia, Karim, Tunis** (3). ⚠️ *summer* is **not** proper (a season), and *sea* has no name here → common.
- (b) Common: **summer, brother, grandmother, house, sea, books, fish** (any 5).

### C2. Example answers
1. *My friend Tom works at Sunrise Hotel in Leeds.*
2. *My mother speaks Arabic and French.*
3. *Our neighbour Mrs Diaz has a dog called Max.*
4. *My sister studies at Oxford University in England.*
- Check: capitals for every proper noun; small letters for common nouns; no *the* before city/country names (except *the UK/USA*…).
