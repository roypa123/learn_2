# Exercise 001 — Mixed Practice: Lessons 001–010

**ID:** E-001 · **Answers:** [answers-001](answers-001-foundations-lessons-001-010.md) · **Time:** about 30 minutes

> ✏️ Do **all** the exercises before you look at the answers. Mistakes are okay — they show you what to review.

---

## Part A — Ideas (Lessons 001, 002, 007)

1. What are the three parts of a language?
2. Name the four skills.
3. Do these two sentences mean the same? *The girl helps the boy.* / *The boy helps the girl.* Why / why not?
4. How many words are in this sentence? *My little brother doesn't like milk.*
5. Write the full form of: **it's**, **don't**, **I'm**.

## Part B — Letters and sounds (Lessons 003, 005, 006)

6. Write the letter names: **G**, **J**, **E**, **I**, **W**.
7. Spell this word out loud using letter names, then write the names: **queen**.
8. **a** or **an**?
   a) ___ umbrella b) ___ table c) ___ hour d) ___ egg e) ___ yellow bag
9. Which **c** sounds like /s/? **c**at / **c**ircle / **c**up / **c**ar
10. Find the silent letter: **knock**, **write**, **lamb**, **listen**.
11. Choose the correct word: *Please (right / write) your name here.*

## Part C — Capital letters (Lesson 004)

12. Rewrite with correct capital letters:
    *on monday i visit my aunt in cairo. she speaks arabic and french.*
13. Which word does **not** need a capital? **july** / **winter** / **friday** / **mexico**

## Part D — Kinds of words (Lesson 008)

14. Thing word (T), action word (A) or describing word (D)?
    a) kitchen b) sing c) tall d) happy e) doctor f) write
15. Put in order: *bag / a / blue / small*
16. Thing word or action word? *I **drink** tea.* / *Can I have a **drink**?*
17. Correct: *She has two bigs dogs.*

## Part E — Sentences (Lessons 009, 010)

18. Complete sentence ✅ or not ❌?
    a) The old man in the garden.
    b) The old man is in the garden.
    c) Sit down, please.
    d) Very tired today.
19. Which kind of sentence? Statement / Question / Instruction / Exclamation
    a) What a great film! b) Is it cold? c) Open the window. d) The shop is closed.
20. Rewrite with capital letters and end marks:
    *my name is omar i am twelve years old where do you live*
21. Correct the mistakes:
    a) I have ten years.
    b) Where the station is?
    c) I am hungry, I want to eat.

## Part F — Write about you (all lessons)

22. Write **4 sentences** about yourself:
    - your name
    - your age
    - your city
    - a language you speak

    Check: capital letters ✅ · end marks ✅ · a verb in every sentence ✅

## Part G — Say it 🗣️

23. Read your 4 sentences aloud. Stop briefly at each full stop.
24. Say these minimal pairs: *ship / sheep*, *three / tree*, *light / right*.
