## Part A — Subject pronouns (L-027)

### A1. Replace the bold words with a subject pronoun.

1. **My mother** is a teacher.
2. **The bus** is late.
3. **My brother and I** like tennis.
4. **The children** are in the garden.
5. **You and Sara** are early.
6. **Mr Ali** works in a bank.

### A2. Choose the correct verb.

1. She (work / works) in a hospital.
2. They (is / are) my friends.
3. I (am / is) hungry.
4. It (rain / rains) a lot here.
5. We (has / have) two cats.

### A3. Error correction.

1. My father he is a pilot.
2. Is very hot today.
3. i am from Spain.
4. This is my new phone. He is very fast.
5. Someone left a bag. It must be worried.

### A4. *it* or *he/she*?

1. Look at that bird! ___ is beautiful.
2. This is my dog, Bella. ___ is three years old.
3. Who's at the door? — ___'s Tom.
4. ___'s 8 o'clock. Let's go.

## Part B — Object pronouns (L-028)

### B1. Replace the bold words with an object pronoun.

1. I know **your sister**.
2. Can you help **my friend and me**?
3. I don't like **this music**.
4. Call **your parents**.
5. This is for **Tom**.

### B2. Subject or object? Choose.

1. (She / Her) loves (he / him).
2. Can you come with (we / us)?
3. (They / Them) invited (I / me) to the party.
4. Let (I / me) help you.
5. I'm hungry. — (I / Me) too!

### B3. Two people — choose *I* or *me*.

1. My sister and ___ are students.
2. She invited my sister and ___.
3. This is between you and ___.
4. Tom and ___ went to the cinema.
5. The teacher gave Tom and ___ a book.

### B4. Word order — put the words in the correct order.

1. it / me / give / to
2. off / it / turn
3. them / up / pick
4. her / I / very much / like

### B5. Error correction.

1. Give to me the book.
2. Turn off it, please.
3. She is taller than I. *(make it natural)*
4. This gift is for she.
5. Me and Ali play football. *(careful English)*
