# Test 001 — Level 0 (Lessons 001–019)

**ID:** E-002 (test) · **Answers:** [answers-test-001](answers-test-001-level-0.md) · **Time:** about 45 minutes · **Total:** 60 points

> Don't look at the lessons. Write your answers on paper. Then check with the answer key.

---

## Section A — Letters, sounds and spelling (8 points)

1. Write the letter names: **H**, **Y**, **G**, **E**. *(4 points)*
2. **a** or **an**? *(4 points)*
   a) ___ uncle b) ___ house c) ___ hour d) ___ university

## Section B — Capital letters and end marks (6 points)

3. Rewrite correctly. *(6 points: 1 for each correct sentence)*
   *hello my name is pablo i am from mexico i speak spanish where are you from my teacher is mrs jones*

## Section C — Kinds of words (6 points)

4. Noun (N), verb (V) or adjective (A)? *(6 points)*
   a) beautiful b) kitchen c) understand d) cold (*It is cold.*) e) cook (*She is a good cook.*) f) cook (*I cook dinner.*)

## Section D — Subject, verb, object (8 points)

5. Write the **subject**, **verb** and **object** (or "no object"). *(2 points each)*
   a) My little sister loves chocolate.
   b) The students in my class speak five languages.
   c) The baby sleeps in the afternoon.
   d) Tom helps me every day.

## Section E — Word order (6 points)

6. Put the words in order. *(2 points each)*
   a) football / the boys / in the park / play / after school
   b) a / car / have / red / I
   c) every morning / coffee / drinks / my mother

## Section F — am / is / are (6 points)

7. Fill in the gap. *(1 point each)*
   a) I ___ a student.
   b) My parents ___ at work.
   c) The book on the table ___ new.
   d) Ana and I ___ friends.
   e) It ___ 5 o'clock.
   f) You ___ very kind.

## Section G — Questions and short answers (8 points)

8. Make questions. *(1 point each)*
   a) She is a doctor. → ___?
   b) You can swim. → ___?
   c) They live in Rome. → ___?
   d) He likes music. → ___?
9. Short answers. *(1 point each)*
   a) Are you tired? — Yes, ___.
   b) Does she speak French? — No, ___.
   c) Can they come? — Yes, ___.
   d) Is it cold? — No, ___.

## Section H — this / that / these / those, numbers (6 points)

10. Choose. *(1 point each)*
    a) (This / These) are my shoes.
    b) What's (that / those)? — It's a plane.
    c) (That / Those) books are old.
11. Write in words. *(1 point each)*
    a) 15 b) 40 c) 68

## Section I — Find and correct the mistake (6 points)

12. Each sentence has **one** mistake. *(1 point each)*
    a) I am live in Cairo.
    b) Is very hot today.
    c) What means "nervous"?
    d) My brother has twenty years.
    e) Does your sister likes tea?
    f) I am tired, I want to sleep.

## Section J — Everyday English (bonus: not counted)

13. What do you say?
    a) Someone says "Thank you!"
    b) You arrive at a party at 8 pm. (formal)
    c) You don't understand the teacher.
    d) You introduce your friend Ali to your mother.
