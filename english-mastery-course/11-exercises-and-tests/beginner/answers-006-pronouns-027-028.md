## Part A — L-027

### A1.
1. **She** 2. **It** 3. **We** 4. **They** 5. **You** (plural) 6. **He**

### A2.
1. **works** — *she* + -s.
2. **are**
3. **am**
4. **rains** — *it* + -s (empty *it* for weather).
5. **have**
- Rule: [L-027 §7.3](../../01-beginner-grammar/pronouns/lesson-027-subject-pronouns.md#73-pronoun--verb-agreement).

### A3.
1. *My father is a pilot.* / *He is a pilot.* — don't repeat the subject.
2. ***It** is very hot today.* — English needs a subject.
3. ***I** am from Spain.* — capital I.
4. *…**It** is very fast.* — things → *it*.
5. *…**They** must be worried.* — people → *they* (singular *they* for an unknown person).
- Rule: [L-027 §7.5](../../01-beginner-grammar/pronouns/lesson-027-subject-pronouns.md#75-three-big-mistakes).

### A4.
1. **It** — an unknown animal.
2. **She** — a pet with a name.
3. **It** — identifying someone at the door.
4. **It** — time.

## Part B — L-028

### B1.
1. I know **her**. 2. Can you help **us**? 3. I don't like **it**. 4. Call **them**. 5. This is for **him**.

### B2.
1. **She** loves **him**. 2. with **us** 3. **They** invited **me** 4. Let **me** 5. **Me** too!
- Rule: before the verb → subject; after a verb/preposition or alone → object ([L-028 §7](../../01-beginner-grammar/pronouns/lesson-028-object-pronouns.md#7-detailed-explanation)).

### B3.
1. **I** (subject) 2. **me** (object) 3. **me** (after *between*) 4. **I** (subject) 5. **me** (object)
- Test: remove the other person. *She invited **me*** ✅ → *my sister and **me***.

### B4.
1. ***Give it to me.*** 2. ***Turn it off.*** 3. ***Pick them up.*** 4. ***I like her very much.***
- 1: thing pronoun + *to* + person ([§7.6](../../01-beginner-grammar/pronouns/lesson-028-object-pronouns.md#76-two-objects-give-me-the-book--give-it-to-me)). 2–3: pronoun in the middle of phrasal verbs ([§7.7](../../01-beginner-grammar/pronouns/lesson-028-object-pronouns.md#77-phrasal-verbs-turn-it-off-not-turn-off-it)). 4: don't split verb and object.

### B5.
1. ***Give me the book.*** / *Give the book to me.*
2. ***Turn it off, please.***
3. ***She is taller than me.*** / *…than I am.*
4. *This gift is for **her**.*
5. ***Ali and I** play football.*
