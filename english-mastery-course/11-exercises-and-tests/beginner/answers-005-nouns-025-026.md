## Part A — L-025

### A1.
1. men 2. women 3. children 4. people 5. feet 6. teeth 7. mice 8. sheep 9. fish 10. geese 11. deer 12. policemen (neutral: *police officers*)
- Review the 5 types: [L-025 §7](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-025-irregular-plurals.md#7-the-five-types-of-irregular-plurals).

### A2.
1. **children** — never *childs*.
2. **People** — *peoples* = nations; *people* is the everyday plural.
3. **feet**
4. **sheep** — same form.
5. **women** — never add -s to an irregular plural.
6. **live** — *people* is plural → no -s.

### A3.
1. ***These children are my sons.*** — *This → These*, *child → children*, *is → are*, *son → sons*.
2. ***Women are waiting outside.*** (or *Some women…*) — *a* disappears.
3. ***My teeth hurt.*** — no -s on the verb now.
4. ***Those people are very kind.***

### A4.
1. *The **children** are…* — no -s after *children*.
2. *two **feet***
3. *People **are** happy today.*
4. *Where are the **men**?*

### A5. **woman / women** — "WUM-un" → "WIM-in". (*child/children*: long i → short i; *foot/feet*: short → long — also big changes!)

## Part B — L-026

### B1.
1. C 2. **U** 3. C 4. **U** 5. C 6. **U** 7. C 8. **U** 9. **U** 10. C 11. **U** 12. C
- Notice the **pairs**: chair/furniture, bag/luggage, job/work, coin/money, fact/information, sandwich/bread ([L-026 §7.5](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-026-countable-and-uncountable-nouns.md#75-countable--uncountable-pairs-with-similar-meanings)).

### B2.
1. **an** egg 2. **some** milk 3. **an** apple 4. **some** advice 5. **some** cheese 6. **a** banana
- U nouns never take *a/an*. Also review *a/an* by sound ([L-005](../../00-foundations/02-letters-and-sounds/lesson-005-vowels-and-consonants.md)).

### B3.
1. **is** (U) 2. **are** (C plural) 3. **is** (*news* = U) 4. **is** (U) 5. **are** 6. **is** (U)

### B4.
1. **How much** (U) 2. **How many** (C) 3. **How much** 4. **How much** 5. **How many** (*people* = plural)

### B5.
1. **loaf** 2. **bottle** 3. **piece** 4. **cup** 5. **slice** (or *piece*) 6. **glass**

### B6.
1. *Can you give me **some information**?* (or *a piece of information*)
2. *I have **a lot of homework**.*
3. *We bought new **furniture**.*
4. *How was your **trip**?*
5. *I have **a job** in a bank.* / *I work in a bank.*
6. *There **is** some milk in the fridge.*

### B7.
1. *chicken* = the meat (U) / *chickens* = the birds (C).
2. *time* = minutes/hours in general (U) / *times* = occasions (C).
- Rule: [L-026 §7.4](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-026-countable-and-uncountable-nouns.md#74-nouns-that-can-be-both--with-different-meanings).

## Part C — Review

### C1.
> *Last summer, my **family** visited **Italy**. The **weather was** great and the **people** were friendly. We ate **a lot of bread** and two **pizzas** every day. My **children** loved it!*

| Mistake | Correction | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| familys | family (one family) — and the plural would be *families* | [L-024](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-024-plural-spelling-rules.md) |
| the italy | Italy (capital, no *the*) | [L-022](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-022-common-and-proper-nouns.md) |
| weathers | weather (U) | L-026 |
| were (weather) | was | L-026 |
| peoples | people | L-025 |
| many breads | a lot of bread / many loaves of bread | L-026 |
| two pizza | two pizzas | [L-023](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-023-singular-and-plural-nouns.md) |
| childrens | children | L-025 |

### C2. Example answer
*There are six eggs in the fridge. There is some milk too. We have a lot of rice but not much sugar. There are three apples on the table. There isn't any bread.*
