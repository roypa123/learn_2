## Section B (6)

3. ***Hello. My name is Pablo. I am from Mexico. I speak Spanish. Where are you from? My teacher is Mrs Jones.***
   - 6 sentences → 6 capitals at the start, 5 full stops + 1 question mark.
   - Names: **P**ablo, **M**exico, **S**panish, **M**rs **J**ones. **I** is always capital.
   - *Hello!* (with !) is also correct.
   - Review [L-004](../../00-foundations/02-letters-and-sounds/lesson-004-capital-and-small-letters.md), [L-010](../../00-foundations/04-what-is-a-sentence/lesson-010-capital-letter-and-full-stop.md).
