## Part A — What is a noun? (L-021)

### A1. Recognition — find all the nouns.

1. The cat sleeps on the sofa.
2. My father drives a bus in the city.
3. Happiness is important.
4. The children eat fruit for breakfast.
5. Thank you for your advice.

### A2. Which group? (person / animal / place / thing / idea)

1. kitchen 2. fear 3. nurse 4. umbrella 5. horse 6. holiday

### A3. Position — subject (S), object (O), after *be* (B) or after a preposition (P)?

1. **Students** need books. 2. I like **music**. 3. She is a **dentist**. 4. The cup is on the **shelf**.

### A4. Noun or verb?

1. I have a **drink** every afternoon.
2. Please **call** me tomorrow.
3. That was a nice **walk**.
4. We **work** from 9 to 5.
5. I need some **help**.

### A5. Find the head noun of the subject. Then choose the verb.

1. The box of old toys (is / are) in the garage.
2. The children in the garden (is / are) loud.
3. A bag with three apples (is / are) on the table.

### A6. Error correction.

1. She is teacher.
2. I have car.
3. The love is beautiful.
4. Thanks for your helping.

### A7. Sentence building — make a sentence with these nouns.

1. doctor / hospital
2. dog / garden
3. idea / good

## Part B — Common and proper nouns (L-022)

### B1. Common (C) or proper (P)?

1. river 2. Thames 3. Tuesday 4. summer 5. language 6. Arabic 7. Google 8. company

### B2. Capital letters — rewrite correctly.

1. my brother lives in new york.
2. we have a holiday in august.
3. mrs green teaches maths and spanish.
4. i love japanese food.
5. next friday we are going to the louvre in paris.

### B3. *the* or no *the* (–)?

1. ___ Nile 2. ___ Japan 3. ___ USA 4. ___ Mount Everest 5. ___ Alps 6. ___ London 7. ___ Pacific 8. ___ Lake Como

### B4. Capital or small?

1. Can I have some juice, (Mum / mum)?
2. My (Dad / dad) is a pilot.
3. I go to (Riverside School / riverside school).
4. I go to a big (School / school).

### B5. Error correction.

1. We visited the Italy last Summer.
2. My Friend Ali is from egypt.
3. The Smith live next door.
4. I study English, French and History.

### B6. Common or proper? Same word, different meaning.

1. I bought new **china** / I visited **China**. — explain the difference.
2. **May** I sit here? / I was born in **May**. — which one is a proper noun?

## Part C — Mixed (L-021 + L-022)

### C1. In this text, find (a) **all** the proper nouns and (b) 5 common nouns.

> *Every summer, Nadia and her brother Karim visit their grandmother in Tunis. Her house is near the sea. They swim, read books and eat fresh fish.*

### C2. Write 4 sentences about your family or friends. Use at least 2 proper nouns and 2 common nouns in each sentence.

Example: *My cousin Lina lives in Amman with her husband.*

### C3. Speaking 🗣️

Say aloud: your name, your city, your country, your language, your favourite month, and a shop you like. Example: *I'm Leo. I live in Lyon, in France. I speak French. My favourite month is June. I like Zara.*
