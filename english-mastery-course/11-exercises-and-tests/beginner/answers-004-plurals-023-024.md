## Part A — L-023

### A1.
1. ***These apples are red.*** — *This → These*, *apple → apples*, *is → are*. *red* doesn't change.
2. ***Those girls are my friends.*** — *That → Those*; the noun after *be* also becomes plural (*friends*).
3. ***The dogs bark every morning.*** — the -s moves from the verb to the noun. *every morning* stays singular.
4. ***They are good ideas.*** — *It → They*, *a* disappears.
5. ***Are the windows open? — Yes, they are.*** — *Is → Are*, *it → they*.
- Rule: the 5 partners ([L-023 §7.2](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-023-singular-and-plural-nouns.md#72-agreement-make-everything-match)).

### A2.
1. **students** — number 2 → plural.
2. **some** — *a* never goes with plurals.
3. **These** — *shoes* is plural.
4. **live** — plural subject → no -s.
5. **needs** — *every* + singular → verb -s.
6. **is** — *one of* + plural noun, but the head is *one* → singular verb.
7. **are** — *jeans* is always plural.
8. **is** — *news* is singular.

### A3.
1. *three **brothers***.
2. *two **pieces of information*** / *some information* — uncountable.
3. *These are **nice photos**.* — no *a* with plurals.
4. *My glasses **are** on the table.* — always plural.
5. ***Every student has** a book.* — every + singular.
6. *I like **cats**.* — general → plural, no *the*.

### A4.
1. **G** 2. **S** (specific cats) 3. **G** 4. **S** (the children in my class)
- Rule: [L-023 §7.4](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-023-singular-and-plural-nouns.md#74-plurals-for-things-in-general).

### B2.
1. **R2** (-ss) 2. **R3** (consonant t + y) 3. **R3** (vowel e + y → +s) 4. **R4** 5. **R4 exception** 6. **R5** (+es)

### B3.
1. **/s/** (t voiceless) 2. **/z/** (g voiced) 3. **/ɪz/** (hissing s) 4. **/s/** (k) 5. **/z/** (vowel) 6. **/ɪz/** (/dʒ/ sound) 7. **/s/** (f) 8. **/ɪz/** (hissing s)
- Rule: [§7.6](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-024-plural-spelling-rules.md#76-how-to-pronounce-plural--s-and--es). Tip: the **sound**, not the letter, decides (*page* ends in letter *e*, but sound /dʒ/).

### B4.
1. 1 → **2** 2. 1 → **1** 3. 1 → **2** 4. 2 → **2**
- Only the /ɪz/ plurals add a syllable.

### B5.
1. **watches** 2. **carries** 3. **plays** 4. **goes** 5. **fixes**
- Same rules as nouns ([§7.7](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-024-plural-spelling-rules.md#77-same-rules-for-verbs-he--she--it)).

### B6.
> *My aunt has two **babies** and three **boxes** of **toys**. On sunny **days**, the **children** play in the garden.*
- *babys → babies* (consonant + y), *boxs → boxes* (-x), *toies → toys* (vowel + y), *daies → days* (vowel + y), *childs → children* (irregular — [L-025](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-025-irregular-plurals.md), next lesson!).
