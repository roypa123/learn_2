## Part A — Singular and plural, agreement (L-023)

### A1. Make the sentences plural. Change **all** the partners.

1. This apple is red.
2. That girl is my friend.
3. The dog barks every morning.
4. It is a good idea.
5. Is the window open? — Yes, it is.

### A2. Choose the correct word.

1. Two (student / students) are late.
2. I need (a / some) books.
3. (This / These) shoes are new.
4. My parents (live / lives) in Rome.
5. Every child (need / needs) love.
6. One of my friends (is / are) a doctor.
7. My jeans (is / are) dirty.
8. The news (is / are) very good.

### A3. Error correction — one mistake in each.

1. I have three brother.
2. She gave me two informations.
3. These are a nice photos.
4. My glasses is on the table.
5. Every students have a book.
6. I like the cats. *(you mean cats in general)*

### A4. General or specific? Write **G** (in general) or **S** (specific).

1. Cats sleep a lot.
2. The cats are in the garden.
3. Children love games.
4. The children in my class love games.

## Part B — Spelling and pronunciation (L-024)

### B1. Write the plural.

1. bus 2. city 3. day 4. knife 5. box 6. photo 7. potato 8. watch 9. roof 10. baby 11. key 12. leaf 13. dish 14. piano 15. quiz 16. chef

### B2. Which rule? (R1 +s · R2 +es · R3 y→ies / y+s · R4 f→ves / exception · R5 -o)

1. glasses 2. parties 3. monkeys 4. wolves 5. cliffs 6. heroes

### B3. Pronunciation — /s/, /z/ or /ɪz/?

1. cats 2. dogs 3. buses 4. books 5. trees 6. pages 7. roofs 8. houses

### B4. Count the syllables.

1. box → boxes 2. cat → cats 3. page → pages 4. city → cities

### B5. Same rules for verbs — write the *he/she/it* form.

1. watch → she ___ 2. carry → he ___ 3. play → she ___ 4. go → he ___ 5. fix → she ___

### B6. Find the 5 spelling mistakes in this text and correct them.

> *My aunt has two babys and three boxs of toies. On sunny daies, the childs play in the garden.*
