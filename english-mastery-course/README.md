# English Mastery Course

Welcome! 👋

This is a big English course. It starts at **zero**. You do not need to know any English grammar before you start.

## Who is this course for?

- People who are just starting English.
- People who know some English words but cannot make sentences.
- People who studied grammar before but forgot it.
- People who want to speak English with more confidence.

## How is this course different?

In many books, you read a rule, then you see a sentence, and that is all.

In this course, **every important example sentence is explained slowly, piece by piece**:

1. What the sentence means (in very easy words).
2. When people say it.
3. Which word is the subject (who or what the sentence is about).
4. Which word is the verb (the action or state).
5. Why every word is in its place.
6. A small reminder of old rules you might have forgotten.
7. Wrong versions, and why they are wrong.
8. How people say it in real life.
9. A pattern you can use to make your own sentences.

## How to use this course

1. **Go in order.** Start with [00-foundations](00-foundations/). Each lesson uses ideas from the lessons before it.
2. **Read slowly.** It is okay to read a lesson two or three times.
3. **Say the sentences out loud.** Speaking helps you remember.
4. **Do the exercises before you look at the answers.**
5. **Review.** Use [REVISION_SCHEDULE.md](REVISION_SCHEDULE.md). We all forget things. Reviewing is normal, not a sign of failure.
