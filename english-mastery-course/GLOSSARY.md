# Glossary — Grammar Words Made Simple

Every grammar word used in this course is explained here in easy English. Words are in **A–Z order**.

"First taught" shows the lesson where the word is first explained.

| Word | Simple meaning | Example | First taught |
|---|---|---|---|
| **accent** | the way a person's speech sounds, often because of where they are from | British accent, Indian accent | [L-002](00-foundations/01-what-is-language/lesson-002-what-is-english-used-for.md) |
| **alphabet** | all the letters of a language, in a fixed order | A, B, C … Z | [L-003](00-foundations/02-letters-and-sounds/lesson-003-the-english-alphabet.md) |
| **apostrophe** | the small mark **'** ; it can show a missing letter | *What's* = What **i**s | L-003 |
| **capital letter** (uppercase) | the big form of a letter | **A**, **B**, **C** | [L-004](00-foundations/02-letters-and-sounds/lesson-004-capital-and-small-letters.md) |
| **comma** | the mark **,** ; a small pause in writing | *Today is Monday, and…* | L-004 (first look) |
| **consonant** | a letter or sound where lips, tongue or teeth block the air a little | b, t, s | [L-005](00-foundations/02-letters-and-sounds/lesson-005-vowels-and-consonants.md) |
| **first language** | the language you learned first, as a child | | L-002 |
| **foreign language** | a language of another country that you learn later | | L-002 |
| **full stop** (US: **period**) | the mark **.** at the end of a sentence | *I am Sam**.*** | [L-001](00-foundations/01-what-is-language/lesson-001-what-is-a-language.md) (first look) |
| **grammar** | the rules for putting words together | | L-001 |
| **greeting** | a word we say when we meet someone | Hello, Hi | L-001 |
| **helping word** (helping verb) | a word that helps the main action word | *I **am** learning.* | L-002 (first look) |
| **language** | a way to share ideas, feelings and information using words | English, Arabic | L-001 |
| **letter** | a small written sign used to write words | a, b, c | L-003 |
| **meaning** | what something tells us; the idea inside it | | L-001 |
| **question mark** | the mark **?** at the end of a question | *What's your name**?*** | L-003 |
| **question word** | a word that starts a question to ask for information | what, how | L-003 (first look) |
| **sentence** | a group of words that gives a complete idea | *Tom eats an apple.* | L-001 (first look) |
| **small letter** (lowercase) | the small form of a letter | a, b, c | L-004 |
| **sound** | something you hear | | L-001 |
| **spell** | to say or write the letters of a word in order | *c-a-t* | L-003 |
| **syllable** | one "beat" of a word; each has a vowel sound | *ap·ple* = 2 | L-005 |
| **vowel** | a letter (a, e, i, o, u) or sound made with an open mouth | a, e, i, o, u | L-005 |
| **word** | a small piece of language with a meaning | apple, eat | L-001 |

*New words are added after each batch of lessons.*
