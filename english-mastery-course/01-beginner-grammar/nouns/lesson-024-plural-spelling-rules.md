# Lesson 024 — Plural Spelling Rules (-s, -es, -ies, -ves, -os/-oes) and How to Say Them

## 1. Learning objectives

1. Spell plurals correctly using **5 rules**: **-s**, **-es**, **-ies**, **-ves**, and nouns ending in **-o**.
2. Know the common **exceptions** (*roofs, photos, days*).
3. **Pronounce** the plural ending correctly: **/s/**, **/z/** or **/ɪz/**.
4. See that the **same rules** work for verbs with *he/she/it* (*watches, carries*).

## 2. Why this topic matters

- **Writing:** *citys*, *boxs*, *knifes* are some of the most common spelling mistakes.
- **Speaking:** if you say *boxes* without the extra "iz" sound, people may not hear the plural.
- **Later lessons:** exactly the same rules come back for *he works / she watches / it carries* ([L-043](../verbs/lesson-043-present-simple-he-she-it-s.md)). Learn once, use twice!

## 3. Remember from before 🔁

- **Vowel letters:** a, e, i, o, u. All others are **consonants** ([L-005](../../00-foundations/02-letters-and-sounds/lesson-005-vowels-and-consonants.md)).
- **Voiceless** sounds: no throat shaking (/p/, /t/, /k/, /f/, /s/…). **Voiced**: throat shakes (/b/, /d/, /g/, /v/, /z/, and all vowels) ([sounds-001](../../08-pronunciation-and-listening/sounds/sounds-001-english-vowel-and-consonant-sounds.md)).
- Most plurals = noun + **-s** ([L-023](lesson-023-singular-and-plural-nouns.md)).
- In [L-021](lesson-021-what-is-a-noun.md) you met *babies* (y → ies), and in [L-023](lesson-023-singular-and-plural-nouns.md) *boxes, cities, scarves*. Now you learn **why**.

## 4. Simple introduction

Try saying *box* + *s* = "boxs". It's hard! Two hissing sounds come together. So English adds an **e** to make an extra small beat: *box**es*** ("BOK-siz").

Most of the spelling rules exist for a reason like this: to make the word **easier to say**, or to keep an old spelling.

## 5. Definition

> **Plural spelling rules** tell you which letters to add (or change) at the end of a noun to make it plural.

## 6. Key words

| Word | Simple meaning |
|---|---|
| **ending** | the last letter(s) of a word |
| **consonant + y** | a consonant letter just before **y**: *ci**ty***, *ba**by*** |
| **vowel + y** | a vowel letter just before **y**: *d**ay***, *b**oy*** |
| **hissing sounds** (sibilants) | /s/, /z/, /ʃ/ ("sh"), /tʃ/ ("ch"), /dʒ/ ("j"), /ʒ/ |
| **syllable** | a beat in a word ([L-005](../../00-foundations/02-letters-and-sounds/lesson-005-vowels-and-consonants.md)) |

## 7. The rules

### 7.1 Rule 1 — Most nouns: add **-s**

> book → book**s** · car → car**s** · apple → apple**s** · house → house**s** · idea → idea**s**

### 7.2 Rule 2 — After **-s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -z**: add **-es**

| Ending | Singular → Plural |
|---|---|
| -s | bus → bus**es** |
| -ss | glass → glass**es**, class → class**es** |
| -sh | dish → dish**es**, brush → brush**es** |
| -ch | watch → watch**es**, church → church**es** |
| -x | box → box**es**, fox → fox**es** |
| -z | quiz → qui**zzes** (double z!), buzz → buzz**es** |

**Why?** These endings are "hissing sounds". We need the extra beat **-es** to say the plural.

⚠️ **-ch that sounds like /k/** just adds **-s**: *stomach → stomachs*, *monarch → monarchs* (rare words).

### 7.3 Rule 3 — Consonant + **y**: change **y → ies**. Vowel + **y**: just add **-s**

| Consonant + y → **-ies** | Vowel + y → **-s** |
|---|---|
| city → cit**ies** | day → day**s** |
| baby → bab**ies** | boy → boy**s** |
| country → countr**ies** | key → key**s** |
| party → part**ies** | toy → toy**s** |
| story → stor**ies** | monkey → monkey**s** |
| family → famil**ies** | holiday → holiday**s** |

**How to check:** look at the letter **before** the *y*.
- *ci**t**y* → **t** = consonant → *cities*
- *d**a**y* → **a** = vowel → *days*

⚠️ **Names** just add -s: *two Marys*, *the Kennedys*.

### 7.4 Rule 4 — Many nouns in **-f / -fe**: change to **-ves**

| -f / -fe → **-ves** |
|---|
| leaf → lea**ves** 🍂 |
| knife → kni**ves** 🔪 |
| wife → wi**ves** |
| life → li**ves** |
| half → hal**ves** |
| shelf → shel**ves** |
| wolf → wol**ves** 🐺 |
| thief → thie**ves** |
| loaf → loa**ves** 🍞 |

**But some just add -s** (learn these as exceptions):
> roof → roof**s** · chef → chef**s** · cliff → cliff**s** · belief → belief**s** · chief → chief**s** · safe → safe**s**

Some have **both**: *scarf → scarfs / scarves*, *dwarf → dwarfs / dwarves* (both correct).

### 7.5 Rule 5 — Nouns in **-o**

| + **-es** (learn these common ones) | + **-s** (most others) |
|---|---|
| potato → potato**es** 🥔 | photo → photo**s** 📷 |
| tomato → tomato**es** 🍅 | piano → piano**s** 🎹 |
| hero → hero**es** | radio → radio**s** |
| echo → echo**es** | video → video**s** |
| | zoo → zoo**s** |
| | kilo → kilo**s** |

**Helpful pattern:**
- **vowel + o** → always **-s**: *radio, video, zoo, studio*.
- Short forms and music words → **-s**: *photo* (= photograph), *piano, kilo* (= kilogram), *auto*.
- **Both** are correct for some: *volcano(e)s, mosquito(e)s, zero(e)s, mango(e)s*.

> 💡 **Memory sentence:** "*My **heroes** eat **tomatoes** and **potatoes**.*" — the three most common -oes words.

### 7.6 How to pronounce plural -s and -es

The ending is spelled **-s** or **-es**, but it has **three sounds**. The sound depends on the **last sound** (not letter!) of the singular word:

| Last sound of the singular | Plural sounds like | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **hissing sound**: /s/ /z/ /ʃ/ /tʃ/ /dʒ/ /ʒ/ | **/ɪz/** — an extra syllable "iz" | bus**es**, box**es**, dish**es**, watch**es**, page**s**, hous**es**, rose**s** |
| **voiceless** (/p/ /t/ /k/ /f/ /θ/) | **/s/** | cup**s**, cat**s**, book**s**, roof**s**, month**s** |
| **voiced** (all other consonants + **all vowels**) | **/z/** | dog**s**, car**s**, bag**s**, day**s**, tree**s**, knive**s**, citi**es** |

**Count the syllables:**
- *box* (1) → *box-es* (**2**)
- *house* (1) → *hous-es* (**2**) — ⚠️ also *house* /s/ becomes /z/: "HOW-ziz"
- *page* (1) → *pag-es* (**2**) — the spelling just adds **-s**, but the sound adds a syllable!
- *cat* (1) → *cats* (1) — no new syllable

**Throat test:** say the last sound of the word with your hand on your throat ([sounds-001](../../08-pronunciation-and-listening/sounds/sounds-001-english-vowel-and-consonant-sounds.md)). Shaking → /z/. No shaking → /s/.

### 7.7 Same rules for verbs (he / she / it)

| Noun plural | Verb (he/she/it) |
|---|---|
| bus → bus**es** | pass → *she* pass**es** |
| watch → watch**es** | watch → *he* watch**es** TV |
| city → cit**ies** | carry → *she* carr**ies** |
| day → day**s** | play → *he* play**s** |
| (-o) hero → hero**es** | go → *she* go**es**, do → *he* do**es** |

Same spelling, same three sounds. Full lesson: [L-043](../verbs/lesson-043-present-simple-he-she-it-s.md).

## 8. Quick decision chart

```
Does the noun end in -s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -z?  ──yes──►  + es    (boxes)
            │ no
Does it end in consonant + y?  ──yes──►  y → ies   (cities)
            │ no
Does it end in -f / -fe?  ──yes──►  usually f → ves (knives) — check exceptions (roofs, chefs)
            │ no
Does it end in -o?  ──yes──►  usually + s (photos) — except potatoes, tomatoes, heroes, echoes
            │ no
  + s   (books)
```

And remember: some nouns are **irregular** (*man → men, child → children*) — [L-025](lesson-025-irregular-plurals.md).

## 9. Rules and exceptions (summary)

| Rule | Main exceptions |
|---|---|
| + s | — |
| hissing ending + es | *-ch* said /k/ → +s (*stomachs*) |
| consonant + y → ies | names (*Marys*) |
| vowel + y → s | — |
| f/fe → ves | *roofs, chefs, cliffs, beliefs, chiefs, safes*; both: *scarfs/scarves* |
| -o → s | *potatoes, tomatoes, heroes, echoes*; both: *volcanoes/volcanos, zeroes/zeros* |

## 10. Examples (fully explained)

### EX-024-001 — "The buses are full."

- **Meaning:** All the buses have no free seats. 🚌🚌
- **Situation:** Rush hour in the city.
- **Main lesson:** Rule 2: **-s → -es**.
- **Spelling:** *bus* ends in **-s** → add **-es** → *buses*. (❌ *buss*, ❌ *busses* — *busses* is very rare/old.)
- **Sound:** /ɪz/ → "BUS-iz" (2 syllables).
- **Partners:** plural → **are** ([L-023](lesson-023-singular-and-plural-nouns.md)).
- **Wrong:** ❌ *The bus are full.* · ❌ *The buss are full.*
- **Pattern:** **noun ending in -s + es**.
- **Quick check:** Plural of *glass*? How many syllables?

---

### EX-024-002 — "Please wash the dishes."

- **Meaning:** Clean the plates and bowls with water, please. 🍽️
- **Situation:** After dinner at home.
- **Main lesson:** Rule 2: **-sh → -shes**.
- **Spelling:** *dish* + **es** → *dishes*.
- **Sound:** /ɪz/ → "DISH-iz".
- **Also:** *wash* is a verb with the same rule: *She wash**es** the dishes.* ([§7.7](#77-same-rules-for-verbs-he--she--it))
- **Wrong:** ❌ *dishs*
- **Quick check:** Plural of *brush*?

---

### EX-024-003 — "I have two watches, but they are both broken."

- **Meaning:** I own two watches (clocks for the wrist). Neither of them works. ⌚⌚
- **Situation:** You explain why you don't know the time.
- **Main lesson:** Rule 2: **-ch → -ches**.
- **Spelling:** *watch* + **es**. *Sound:* /ɪz/ "WOTCH-iz".
- **both** = the two of them. **broken** = not working.
- **Partners:** *two watches → they are*.
- **Wrong:** ❌ *two watchs* · ❌ *two watch*
- **Quick check:** Plural of *beach*?

---

### EX-024-004 — "Put the books in those boxes."

- **Meaning:** Place the books inside those boxes (over there). 📦
- **Situation:** Moving house.
- **Main lesson:** Rule 1 (*books*) and Rule 2 (*boxes*) side by side.
- **Spelling:** *book + s* · *box + es* (ends in **-x**).
- **Sound:** *books* /s/ (k is voiceless, 1 syllable) · *boxes* /ɪz/ (2 syllables).
- **Partners:** *those* + plural ([L-019](../../00-foundations/06-first-words/lesson-019-this-is-and-that-is.md)).
- **Wrong:** ❌ *those boxs*
- **Quick check:** Plural of *fox*? Sound?

---

### EX-024-005 — "London and Paris are big cities."

- **Meaning:** London and Paris are both large cities. 🏙️
- **Situation:** Talking about European capitals.
- **Main lesson:** Rule 3: **consonant + y → ies**.
- **Spelling:** *ci**t**y* → letter before *y* is **t** (consonant) → *cities*.
- **Sound:** /z/ → "SIT-eez".
- **Partners:** two names joined by *and* → plural → **are**; *big cities* (adjective unchanged).
- **Wrong:** ❌ *big citys* · ❌ *big city*
- **Quick check:** Plural of *country*?

---

### EX-024-006 — "We go on holiday for two weeks, and we stay with friends for three days."

- **Meaning:** Our holiday is two weeks; three of those days we sleep at our friends' house.
- **Situation:** Planning a summer trip.
- **Main lesson:** Rule 3 the other way: **vowel + y → just -s**.
- **Spelling:** *d**a**y* → letter before *y* is **a** (vowel) → *days*. (❌ *daies*)
- **Also:** *holiday → holidays* (same rule).
- **Sound:** *days* /z/ · *weeks* /s/ · *friends* /z/.
- **Wrong:** ❌ *three daies*
- **Quick check:** Plural of *key* and *baby*.

---

### EX-024-007 — "The leaves are yellow and red in autumn."

- **Meaning:** In autumn, the leaves on the trees change colour. 🍂🍁
- **Situation:** Walking in a park in October.
- **Main lesson:** Rule 4: **-f → -ves**.
- **Spelling:** *lea**f*** → *lea**ves***. (❌ *leafs* — though *Leafs* is the name of a hockey team! Names keep their spelling.)
- **Sound:** *leaves* /z/ (v is voiced) → "leevz".
- **Partners:** plural → *are*.
- **Remember 🔁:** seasons → small letter: *autumn* ([L-022](lesson-022-common-and-proper-nouns.md)).
- **Wrong:** ❌ *The leafs are yellow.*
- **Quick check:** Plural of *shelf*?

---

### EX-024-008 — "Be careful — the knives are sharp."

- **Meaning:** Take care. The knives can cut you easily. 🔪
- **Situation:** In a kitchen with a child.
- **Main lesson:** Rule 4: **-fe → -ves**.
- **Spelling:** *kni**fe*** → *kni**ves***. Remember the silent **k** ([L-006](../../00-foundations/02-letters-and-sounds/lesson-006-letters-and-sounds.md)): "nyvz".
- **Partners:** plural → *are*.
- **— (dash):** shows a short pause before the reason.
- **Wrong:** ❌ *knifes* (as a noun)
- **Quick check:** Plural of *wife* and *life*?

---

### EX-024-009 — "Many houses in this street have flat roofs."

- **Meaning:** A lot of houses on this street have tops that are flat (not sloped). 🏠
- **Situation:** Describing houses in a hot country.
- **Main lesson:** A **-f exception**: *roof → roofs* (not *rooves*).
- **Spelling:** *roof + s*. Other exceptions: *chefs, cliffs, beliefs*.
- **Sound:** *roofs* /s/ (f is voiceless). *houses* /ɪz/ — and the *s* in *house* changes to a /z/ sound: "HOW-ziz".
- **Partners:** *Many houses* → *have* (no -s).
- **Wrong:** ❌ *flat rooves* (old, rare) · ❌ *houses has*
- **Quick check:** Plural of *chef*?

---

### EX-024-010 — "I need two kilos of potatoes and some tomatoes."

- **Meaning:** I want to buy 2 kg of potatoes and a few tomatoes. 🥔🍅
- **Situation:** At a market.
- **Main lesson:** Rule 5: **-o** words — *potatoes, tomatoes* (+es) and *kilos* (+s).
- **Spelling:**
  - *potato → potato**es***, *tomato → tomato**es*** (the common -oes words).
  - *kilo → kilo**s*** (a short form of *kilogram* → just -s).
- **Sound:** all /z/ (vowel endings are voiced).
- **some** + plural ([L-069](../articles-and-determiners/lesson-069-some-and-any.md)).
- **Wrong:** ❌ *potatos* · ❌ *kiloes*
- **Memory:** "*My heroes eat tomatoes and potatoes.*"
- **Quick check:** Plural of *photo* and *hero*?

---

### EX-024-011 — "Do you have any photos of the zoos you visited?"

- **Meaning:** Did you take pictures at the zoos you went to? 📷🦒
- **Situation:** A friend just came back from a trip.
- **Main lesson:** Rule 5: most **-o** words → **-s**.
- **Spelling:** *photo + s* (short form of *photograph*) · *zoo + s* (vowel + o).
- **any** + plural in questions ([L-069](../articles-and-determiners/lesson-069-some-and-any.md)).
- **the zoos you visited** — *you visited* tells which zoos (relative clause, much later — [L-171](../../02-intermediate-grammar/clauses/lesson-171-relative-clauses-who-which-that.md)).
- **Wrong:** ❌ *photoes* · ❌ *zooes*
- **Quick check:** Plural of *piano* and *radio*?

---

### EX-024-012 — "She watches videos and carries her phone everywhere."

- **Meaning:** She looks at videos, and she always has her phone with her. 📱
- **Situation:** A parent talks about their teenage daughter.
- **Main lesson:** The **same spelling rules** for **verbs** with *she* ([§7.7](#77-same-rules-for-verbs-he--she--it)).
- **Spelling:**
  - *watch → she watch**es*** (Rule 2, -ch).
  - *carry → she carr**ies*** (Rule 3, consonant + y).
  - *video → video**s*** (noun, Rule 5).
- **Sound:** *watches* /ɪz/ · *carries* /z/ · *videos* /z/.
- **Wrong:** ❌ *she watchs* · ❌ *she carrys*
- **Pattern:** noun and verb endings follow the **same** rules.
- **Quick check:** *He (fix) computers. She (study) at night.*

## 11. Common mistakes

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Correct | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| boxs, buss, dishs, watchs | box**es**, bus**es**, dish**es**, watch**es** | 2 |
| citys, babys, partys | cit**ies**, bab**ies**, part**ies** | 3 |
| daies, boies, keies | day**s**, boy**s**, key**s** | 3 (vowel + y) |
| knifes, leafs, wifes | kni**ves**, lea**ves**, wi**ves** | 4 |
| rooves, chefves | roof**s**, chef**s** | 4 exceptions |
| potatos, tomatos | potato**es**, tomato**es** | 5 |
| photoes, pianoes | photo**s**, piano**s** | 5 |
| "boxs" (1 syllable) | "box-iz" (2 syllables) | 7.6 |
| quizes | qui**zz**es | 2 (double z) |

## 13. Grammar combinations

The *consonant + y* rule returns in **-ed** (*carried*), comparatives (*happier*) and **-ly** adverbs (*happily*). Watch for it!

## 14. Everyday conversation

> **A:** How many **classes** do you have today?
> **B:** Three. And two **quizzes**!
> **A:** Oh no. Do you have your **notes**?
> **B:** Yes, in one of my **boxes**… somewhere!

- *classes* (-ss + es), *quizzes* (double z), *notes* (+s), *boxes* (-x + es).

## 15. Story-based example

> *On Saturdays, my grandmother bakes three **loaves** of bread and makes two **pies** with fresh **cherries** from her **trees**. The **children** from the neighbours' **houses** always come to taste them.*

- *loaves* (f → ves), *pies* (pie + s — ends in **e**, not consonant + y!), *cherries* (consonant + y → ies), *trees* (+s), *houses* (+s, sound /ɪz/).
- *children* — irregular ([L-025](lesson-025-irregular-plurals.md)).

## 16. Pronunciation and listening 🗣️👂

**Practice in three groups** (say them, clap the syllables):

| /s/ | /z/ | /ɪz/ |
|---|---|---|
| cats, books, cups, roofs, months | dogs, cars, days, trees, knives | buses, boxes, dishes, watches, pages, houses |

**Listening tip:** /ɪz/ plurals are **easy to hear** (an extra beat). /s/ and /z/ are short — listen for *these, are, they* to confirm a plural.

Make plural, step by step: **party**
1. Ends in -s/-sh/-ch/-x/-z? No.

✅ **parties** — sound: /z/ (2 syllables: PAR-teez).

Now: **monkey** → *mon**k**e**y*** → the letter before *y* is **e** (a vowel) → **monkeys**.

## 20. Quick revision sheet

1. Most → **+s** (books)
2. -s/-ss/-sh/-ch/-x/-z → **+es** (buses, boxes, quizzes)
3. consonant + y → **-ies** (cities); vowel + y → **+s** (days)
4. -f/-fe → **-ves** (knives) — but *roofs, chefs, cliffs, beliefs*
5. -o → **+s** (photos, pianos) — but *potatoes, tomatoes, heroes, echoes*
- **Sounds:** /ɪz/ after hissing sounds · /s/ after voiceless · /z/ after voiced.
- Same rules for *he/she/it* verbs.
