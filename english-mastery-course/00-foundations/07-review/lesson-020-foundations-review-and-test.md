## 4. The 15 most important rules of Level 0

1. **I** is always a capital letter.
2. Every sentence starts with a **capital** and ends with **. ? !**
3. Every sentence needs a **subject** and a **verb**.
4. English needs a subject even for weather/time: ***It** is cold.*
5. Statement order: **Subject – Verb – Object – Place – Time**.
6. Don't put anything between the **verb and its object**.
7. **Describing word before the noun**: *a red car*. Describing words never take -s.
8. **a** + consonant sound · **an** + vowel sound.
9. **I am · he/she/it is · you/we/they are.**
10. No *be* before a normal verb: ❌ *I am live* → ✅ *I live*.
11. One person (he/she/it) + normal verb → **-s**: *She speaks*.
12. Questions: *Are you…? · Can you…? · Do you…? · Does she…?*
13. After **does**, no -s on the main verb: *Does she **like**…?*
14. Age: ***I am** 20 (years old)* — not *I have 20 years*.
15. Short positive answers aren't contracted: *Yes, I **am**.*
