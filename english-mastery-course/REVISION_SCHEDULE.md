# Revision Schedule

## Why review?

Everybody forgets. Your brain forgets new things quickly at first. Each time you **remember something by yourself** (without looking), the memory gets stronger and lasts longer.

So in this course you do not just re-read. You **test yourself**: cover the answer, try to remember, then check.

## The review plan for every lesson

| When | What to do | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Right after the lesson | Do the "Quick check" questions without looking. | 5 min |
| Next day | Read the "Quick revision sheet". Say 5 example sentences out loud. | 5–10 min |
| 3 days later | Do the lesson's flashcards. Make 3 new sentences of your own. | 10 min |
| 7 days later | Do a short mixed quiz (this lesson + 2 older lessons). | 10 min |
| 14 days later | Flashcards again. Fix any you got wrong. | 5–10 min |
| 30 days later | Cumulative review (the whole unit). | 15–20 min |
| After that | Level tests and mixed tests. | — |

These times are a **starting point**. If a lesson felt hard, review it more often. If it felt very easy, you can wait longer.

## How to know if you remember

After each review, give yourself a mark:

- 😀 **Easy** — I remembered everything. → Next review can be later.
- 🙂 **Okay** — I remembered most things. → Keep the plan.
- 😟 **Hard** — I forgot a lot. → Review again tomorrow, and read the lesson again.
