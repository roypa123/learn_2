## L-021 — What is a noun?

| # | Front | Back |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is a noun? | A naming word: person, animal, place, thing or idea. |
| 2 | The 5 groups of nouns? | people · animals · places · things · ideas |
| 3 | Two quick noun tests? | *a/the/my* before it · it can be plural (-s) |
| 4 | 4 noun positions? | subject · object · after *be* · after a preposition |
| 5 | Head noun of *the old man with the black dog*? | man |
| 6 | *Thanks for your help* — noun or verb? | noun (after *your*) |
| 7 | Correct: *She is doctor.* | She is **a** doctor. |
| 8 | Correct: *The life is beautiful.* | Life is beautiful. (general idea → no *the*) |
