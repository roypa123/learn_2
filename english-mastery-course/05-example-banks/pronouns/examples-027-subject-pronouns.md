# Example Bank 027 — Subject Pronouns (EX-027-013 to EX-027-100)

**For lesson:** [L-027 — Subject Pronouns](../../01-beginner-grammar/pronouns/lesson-027-subject-pronouns.md) · EX-027-001 to 012 are in the lesson.

## How to use this bank

- **Cover the notes.** For each **bold** pronoun: (1) what does it **refer to**? (2) is the **verb** correct for it?
- **→** shows what the pronoun refers to.

> 🔁 *I am · you/we/they are · he/she/it is* · he/she/it + verb-**s** · never repeat or drop the subject ([L-027 §7](../../01-beginner-grammar/pronouns/lesson-027-subject-pronouns.md#7-detailed-explanation)).

---

## Step 1 — One pronoun, short sentences (EX-027-013 to 022)

**EX-027-013** — ***I** am hungry.* — *I* → the speaker · *am*
**EX-027-014** — ***You** are late.* — *you* → the listener · *are*
**EX-027-015** — ***He** is tall.* — a man/boy · *is*
**EX-027-016** — ***She** is a doctor.* — a woman/girl · *is*
**EX-027-017** — ***It** is broken.* — a thing · *is*
**EX-027-018** — ***We** are friends.* — I + others · *are*
**EX-027-019** — ***They** are tired.* — other people · *are*
**EX-027-020** — ***She** sings.* — *sings* (-s)
**EX-027-021** — ***They** sing.* — no -s
**EX-027-022** — ***It** works.* — *works* (-s) · a machine

---

## Step 2 — Noun first, then pronoun (EX-027-023 to 032)

**EX-027-023** — *My grandfather is 90. **He** walks every day.* — → my grandfather
**EX-027-024** — *Ms Diaz is our new teacher. **She** is from Chile.* — → Ms Diaz
**EX-027-025** — *This book is great. **It** has beautiful pictures.* — → this book
**EX-027-026** — *Our neighbours are very quiet. **They** never make noise.* — → our neighbours
**EX-027-027** — *Paris is beautiful. **It** has many museums.* — → Paris (a city → *it*)
**EX-027-028** — *Ali and I are cousins. **We** see each other every week.* — → Ali and I
**EX-027-029** — *The children are outside. **They** are playing football.* — → the children
**EX-027-030** — *My boss is very kind. **She** always helps me.* — → my boss (a woman)
**EX-027-031** — *The new shoes are comfortable. **They** don't hurt my feet.* — → the shoes
**EX-027-032** — *Music is important to me. **It** makes me happy.* — → music (idea → *it*)

---

## Step 3 — Different pronouns for different nouns (EX-027-033 to 042)

**EX-027-033** — *Lucas has a cat. **It** is black.* — unknown/general animal → *it* (Lucas might say *she*).
**EX-027-034** — *My cat Luna is old. **She** sleeps all day.* — pet with a name → *she*.
**EX-027-035** — *My son and daughter are at school. **They** are in the same class.* — → my son and daughter
**EX-027-036** — *You and Tom are early! **You** can help me.* — *you* (plural) → you and Tom
**EX-027-037** — *A doctor is coming. **They** will be here in 10 minutes.* — singular *they* (we don't know who)
**EX-027-038** — *The government has a new plan. **They** want more trains.* — organisation → *they* (UK; US often *it*)
**EX-027-039** — *The team is ready. **They** are on the field.* — UK English often uses *they* for a team; US: *It is ready*.
**EX-027-040** — *The baby is crying. **She** is hungry.* — you know it's a girl → *she*
**EX-027-041** — *That restaurant is expensive, but **it** is very good.* — → the restaurant
**EX-027-042** — *Water is cheap here, but **it** isn't clean.* — U noun → *it* ([L-026](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-026-countable-and-uncountable-nouns.md))

---

## Step 4 — Questions and negatives (EX-027-043 to 052)

**EX-027-043** — *Is **she** your sister?* — *she* after *is* in a question
**EX-027-044** — *Are **they** coming tonight?* — *are* + *they*
**EX-027-045** — *Do **you** like jazz?* — *do* + *you*
**EX-027-046** — *Does **he** speak French?* — *does* + *he* (no -s on *speak*)
**EX-027-047** — ***I**'m not ready.* — *I'm not* (no *amn't*)
**EX-027-048** — ***It** isn't my fault.* — *fault* = the reason something bad happened (my mistake)
**EX-027-049** — ***We** don't have a car.* — *we* → *don't*
**EX-027-050** — ***She** doesn't eat meat.* — *she* → *doesn't* + *eat*
**EX-027-051** — *Where are **you** from?* — wh-question
**EX-027-052** — *Why is **he** angry?* — wh-question with *be*

---

## Step 5 — Different times (EX-027-053 to 060)

**EX-027-053** — ***I** was tired yesterday.* — *I was* (past)
**EX-027-054** — ***They** were at the beach last week.* — *they were*
**EX-027-055** — ***She** will call you tomorrow.* — *will* (same for all pronouns)
**EX-027-056** — ***We** visited Rome last year.* — *-ed* past (same for all pronouns)
**EX-027-057** — ***It** was a great party!* — *it* → the party
**EX-027-058** — ***You** were right.* — *you were* (singular or plural)
**EX-027-059** — ***He** is going to study medicine.* — future plan ([L-076](../../01-beginner-grammar/verbs/lesson-076-future-be-going-to.md))
**EX-027-060** — ***It** will be sunny tomorrow.* — empty *it* (weather)

---

## Step 6 — Pronouns + adjectives / descriptions (EX-027-061 to 070)

**EX-027-061** — *Meet my brother. **He** is funny and very smart.*
**EX-027-062** — *I love this city. **It** is old and beautiful.*
**EX-027-063** — *These apples are from my garden. **They** are sweet.*
**EX-027-064** — *My grandmother is 85, but **she** is very active.* — *active* = does a lot, moves a lot
**EX-027-065** — *We are hungry and tired.* — two adjectives
**EX-027-066** — *Look at that dog! **It** is huge!* — unknown dog → *it*; *huge* = very big
**EX-027-067** — *I'm sorry. **I** am not very good at maths.* — *good at* + noun ([L-089](../../01-beginner-grammar/prepositions/lesson-089-objects-of-prepositions.md))
**EX-027-068** — *You are so kind!* — *so* = very (strong feeling)
**EX-027-069** — *The test was easy. **It** was only ten questions.*
**EX-027-070** — *My friends are very different. **They** have different hobbies.*

---

## Step 7 — Empty *it* and *It's + person* (EX-027-071 to 078)

**EX-027-071** — ***It**'s 6 o'clock.* — time
**EX-027-072** — ***It**'s Friday!* — day
**EX-027-073** — ***It**'s windy today.* — weather
**EX-027-074** — ***It**'s far from here.* — distance
**EX-027-075** — ***It**'s nice to see you.* — *it* points forward to *to see you* (the situation). Learn it as a fixed phrase.
**EX-027-076** — *"Who is it?" — "**It**'s me, Sam."* — identifying a person
**EX-027-077** — ***It** takes 20 minutes to walk to school.* — *it takes* + time = how long something needs
**EX-027-078** — ***It**'s your turn.* — *turn* = the time when it's your chance (in a game)

---

## Step 8 — Longer sentences and clear reference (EX-027-079 to 086)

**EX-027-079** — *My brother and his wife live in Canada, and **they** have two children.* — → my brother and his wife
**EX-027-080** — *When **I** was a child, **I** lived in a small village near the sea.* — two *I*s are fine; same person.
**EX-027-081** — *Tom called Ali because **Tom** needed help.* — ⚠️ we **repeat Tom**: *he* could mean Ali → unclear ([§7.5](../../01-beginner-grammar/pronouns/lesson-027-subject-pronouns.md#75-three-big-mistakes)).
**EX-027-082** — *Maria told her sister that **she** was late.* — ⚠️ unclear! Who was late? Better: *Maria said to her sister, "I'm late."* or *"You're late."*
**EX-027-083** — *The phone and the charger are in the bag, but **they** aren't new.* — → both things
**EX-027-084** — *Every student must bring a pencil, and **they** must write their name on the paper.* — singular *they* after *every student*
**EX-027-085** — ***They** say that the new museum is fantastic.* — *they* = people in general
**EX-027-086** — *My cat is very lazy: **she** sleeps sixteen hours a day, and **she** only wakes up for food.*

## Step 9 — Conversations (EX-027-087 to 090)

**EX-027-087**
> **A:** Is Leo coming?
> **B:** No, **he** isn't. **He**'s sick.
- *Leo* → *he*.

**EX-027-088**
> **A:** Where are your keys?
> **B:** **They**'re in my bag. Oh no — **they** aren't!
- *keys* → *they*.

**EX-027-089**
> **A:** How's your new job?
> **B:** **It**'s great! **I** love **it**.
- *job* → *it* (subject), *it* (object).

**EX-027-090**
> **A:** Are **you** and Ana free on Saturday?
> **B:** Yes, **we** are. Why?
- *you* (plural: you + Ana) → answer *we*.

---

## Step 10 — Short story (EX-027-091 to 093)

> *(1) Ben and Kate are twins. (2) **They** are ten years old, and **they** go to the same school. (3) Ben loves football, but Kate doesn't — **she** prefers painting.*

**EX-027-091** — (1) *twins* = two children born at the same time to the same mother.
**EX-027-092** — (2) *They* → Ben and Kate · *are, go* (no -s).
**EX-027-093** — (3) *she* → Kate (not Ben — clear because *Kate* is the last name, and *she* is female). *prefers* = likes more.

---

## Step 11 — Real writing: a short email (EX-027-094 to 096)

> *Hi Jo,*
> ***I**'m in Madrid with my parents. **It**'s very hot! **We** visit a museum every day, and **they** love the food. See you soon! Mia*

**EX-027-094** — *I'm in Madrid… It's very hot!* — *I* (the writer) · *it* (weather).
**EX-027-095** — *We visit a museum every day* — *we* = Mia + her parents.
**EX-027-096** — *they love the food* — *they* = her parents (*we* = all three, *they* = only the parents).

---

## Step 12 — Telling similar cases apart (EX-027-097 to 100)

**EX-027-097** — ***It**'s my brother. / **He**'s my brother.*
- *It's my brother* — identifying (e.g., in a photo, on the phone, at the door). *He's my brother* — describing someone already in view: *This is Tom. He's my brother.* Both are possible in many situations.

**EX-027-098** — ***They** are coming. (several people) / Someone is coming. **They** are near.*
- plural *they* vs singular *they* — verb is **are** in both.

**EX-027-099** — *The dog is barking. **It**'s hungry. / Max is barking. **He**'s hungry.*
- unknown/general animal → *it*; pet with a name → *he/she*.

**EX-027-100** — ***You** are my best friend. / **You** are all welcome here.*
- *you* singular (one friend) / *you* plural (*all* makes it clear).
