# Example Bank 028 — Object Pronouns (EX-028-013 to EX-028-100)

**For lesson:** [L-028 — Object Pronouns](../../01-beginner-grammar/pronouns/lesson-028-object-pronouns.md) · EX-028-001 to 012 are in the lesson.

## How to use this bank

- **Cover the notes.** For each **bold** pronoun: (1) subject or object form? (2) why — **after a verb**, **after a preposition**, or **alone**? (3) what does it refer to?
- **Key:** **V** = after a verb · **P** = after a preposition · **A** = alone / short answer

> 🔁 *me, you, him, her, it, us, them* — after verbs, after prepositions, alone. Subject position → *I, he, she, we, they* ([L-028 §7](../../01-beginner-grammar/pronouns/lesson-028-object-pronouns.md#7-detailed-explanation)).

## Step 1 — One pronoun, after a verb (EX-028-013 to 022)

**EX-028-013** — *Help **me**!* — V
**EX-028-014** — *I know **you**.* — V
**EX-028-015** — *We like **him**.* — V
**EX-028-016** — *Call **her**.* — V
**EX-028-017** — *I need **it**.* — V
**EX-028-018** — *They visit **us** often.* — V
**EX-028-019** — *I see **them**.* — V
**EX-028-020** — *She hates **it**.* — V · *hate* = strongly not like
**EX-028-021** — *Please wait for **me**.* — P (*for*)
**EX-028-022** — *Don't forget **us**!* — V

## Step 2 — Noun first, then object pronoun (EX-028-023 to 032)

**EX-028-023** — *I met your brother. I like **him**.* — → your brother
**EX-028-024** — *Where is my bag? I can't find **it**.* — → my bag
**EX-028-025** — *Your parents are lovely. Say hello to **them**.* — P · → your parents
**EX-028-026** — *This soup is cold. Can you heat **it**?* — → the soup
**EX-028-027** — *Mrs Lee is my neighbour. I help **her** with shopping.* — → Mrs Lee
**EX-028-028** — *These shoes are too small. I can't wear **them**.* — → the shoes
**EX-028-029** — *My friends and I are lost. Can you help **us**?* — → my friends and I
**EX-028-030** — *I love this song. Play **it** again!* — → the song
**EX-028-031** — *The baby is crying. Can you hold **him**?* — → the baby (a boy)
**EX-028-032** — *Someone is at the door. Can you let **them** in?* — singular *them* · *let in* = allow to enter

## Step 3 — After prepositions (EX-028-033 to 042)

**EX-028-033** — *This present is **for you**.* — P
**EX-028-034** — *Sit **next to me**.* — P
**EX-028-035** — *I'm angry **with him**.* — P · *angry with* + person
**EX-028-036** — *Look **at them**!* — P
**EX-028-037** — *We talked **about her** all evening.* — P
**EX-028-038** — *Can I come **with you**?* — P
**EX-028-039** — *I can't do it **without them**.* — P
**EX-028-040** — *There's a letter **from us** in the box.* — P
**EX-028-041** — *The cat is sleeping **behind it**.* — P · *it* = the sofa (for example)
**EX-028-042** — *Everyone is here **except him**.* — P · *except* = but not

## Step 4 — Questions and negatives (EX-028-043 to 052)

**EX-028-043** — *Do you know **her**?* — V
**EX-028-044** — *Can you hear **me**?* — V
**EX-028-045** — *I don't understand **you**.* — V
**EX-028-046** — *She doesn't like **them**.* — V
**EX-028-047** — *Why are you looking at **me**?* — P
**EX-028-048** — *Who is this for? Is it for **us**?* — P
**EX-028-049** — *Don't touch **it**!* — V · negative instruction
**EX-028-050** — *Does he know **you**?* — V
**EX-028-051** — *What do you think of **him**?* — P · *think of* = have an opinion about
**EX-028-052** — *Are you talking to **me**?* — P

## Step 5 — Different times (EX-028-053 to 060)

**EX-028-053** — *I saw **her** yesterday.* — V · *saw* = past of *see*
**EX-028-054** — *They called **us** last night.* — V
**EX-028-055** — *I will help **you** tomorrow.* — V
**EX-028-056** — *We visited **them** in July.* — V
**EX-028-057** — *He gave **me** a book for my birthday.* — person + thing ([§7.6](../../01-beginner-grammar/pronouns/lesson-028-object-pronouns.md#76-two-objects-give-me-the-book--give-it-to-me))
**EX-028-058** — *I bought **it** last week.* — V
**EX-028-059** — *She is going to call **him** later.* — V
**EX-028-060** — *My grandmother taught **me** to cook.* — V · *taught* = past of *teach*

## Step 6 — Subject vs object in the same sentence (EX-028-061 to 070)

**EX-028-061** — ***I** love **her**, and **she** loves **me**.* — S/O/S/O
**EX-028-062** — ***We** invited **them**, but **they** didn't come.*
**EX-028-063** — ***He** knows **us**, but **we** don't know **him**.*
**EX-028-064** — ***They** told **me** the news.* — person + thing
**EX-028-065** — ***She** sent **them** a photo.* — person + thing
**EX-028-066** — ***You** can trust **me**.* — *trust* = believe someone is honest
**EX-028-067** — ***It** helps **me** sleep.* — *it* = the music (for example)
**EX-028-068** — ***I** saw **you** with **him**.* — V, P
**EX-028-069** — ***We** need **you** more than **they** do.* — *they do* = they need you
**EX-028-070** — ***He** gave **it** to **her**.* — thing pronoun + *to* + person

## Step 7 — Two people together (EX-028-071 to 078)

> 🔁 Test: remove the other person ([§7.5](../../01-beginner-grammar/pronouns/lesson-028-object-pronouns.md#75-two-people-together-you-and-me-or-you-and-i)).

**EX-028-071** — *Ana and **I** are going to Rome.* — subject → *I*
**EX-028-072** — *Come to Rome with Ana and **me**!* — after *with* → *me*
**EX-028-073** — *This is a secret between you and **me**.* — after *between* → *me*
**EX-028-074** — *My parents gave my sister and **me** a puppy.* — object → *me*
**EX-028-075** — *He and **I** work in the same office.* — subject → *He and I* (formal); informal speech often says *Me and him* ❌ in writing
**EX-028-076** — *The teacher wants to see you and **him** after class.* — object → *him*
**EX-028-077** — *A photo of my wife and **me** is on my desk.* — after *of* → *me*
**EX-028-078** — *She and her brother look very similar.* — subject → *She* (❌ *Her and her brother look…*)

## Step 8 — Two objects and phrasal verbs (EX-028-079 to 086)

**EX-028-079** — *Can you show **me** the way to the station?* — person + thing
**EX-028-080** — *Here are the keys. Give **them** to your mother.* — thing pronoun + *to* + person
**EX-028-081** — *I bought **her** some flowers.* — person + thing · or: *I bought some flowers **for her**.*
**EX-028-082** — *Your coat is on the floor. Pick **it** up, please.* — phrasal verb · ❌ *Pick up it*
**EX-028-083** — *These are old magazines. Throw **them** away.* — *throw away* = put in the rubbish
**EX-028-084** — *It's cold. Put your jacket on. / Put **it** on.* — noun: both orders; pronoun: middle only
**EX-028-085** — *The baby is asleep. Don't wake **her** up!* — *wake up* · pronoun in the middle
**EX-028-086** — *I lent **him** my bike, but he never gave **it** back to **me**.* — *lend* = give for a short time · *give back* = return

## Step 9 — Conversations (EX-028-087 to 090)

**EX-028-087**
> **A:** I love this film.
> **B:** **Me** too!
- A — alone → *me*.

**EX-028-088**
> **A:** Who ate my chocolate?
> **B:** Not **me**!
- A — alone → *me* (formal: *Not I* — very rare).

**EX-028-089**
> **A:** Where's Lisa?
> **B:** I don't know. I can't see **her**.
- V · → Lisa.

**EX-028-090**
> **A:** Can I borrow your pen?
> **B:** Sure. Here you are. Give **it** back to **me** later.
- *borrow* = take for a short time · *it* = the pen.

## Step 10 — Short story (EX-028-091 to 093)

> *(1) Lina has a little brother, Omar. (2) Every morning, she takes **him** to school and picks **him** up at three. (3) Omar loves **her**, and he always makes **her** a drawing.*

**EX-028-091** — (1) no pronouns — names first.
**EX-028-092** — (2) *takes him* (V) · *picks him up* (phrasal verb, pronoun in the middle).
**EX-028-093** — (3) *loves her* (V) · *makes her a drawing* (person + thing).

---

## Step 11 — Real writing: a thank-you message (EX-028-094 to 096)

> *Dear Aunt Rosa,*
> *Thank **you** for the beautiful scarf. I wear **it** every day! Mum says hello to **you**. We miss **you** — please visit **us** soon!*
> *Love, Leo*

**EX-028-094** — *Thank you for…* — V · *you* = Aunt Rosa.
**EX-028-095** — *I wear it every day* — V · *it* = the scarf.
**EX-028-096** — *hello to you · miss you · visit us* — P, V, V · *miss* = feel sad because someone is not with you.

---

## Step 12 — Telling similar forms apart (EX-028-097 to 100)

**EX-028-097** — *I like **her**. / I like **her** dress.*
- 1st *her* = object pronoun (alone after the verb). 2nd *her* = possessive (+ noun *dress*) ([L-036](../../01-beginner-grammar/pronouns/lesson-036-possessive-determiners.md)).

**EX-028-098** — *He is taller than **me**. / He is taller than **I am**.*
- both correct; *than me* is more common in speech; *than I am* is good in writing.

**EX-028-099** — ***Let's** go. / **Let us** go.*
- *Let's* = a suggestion (we go together). *Let us go* can also mean "allow us to go" (e.g., *Please let us go home early!*).

**EX-028-100** — *Turn off the light. / Turn the light off. / Turn **it** off.*
- with a noun → two orders · with a pronoun → only the middle.
