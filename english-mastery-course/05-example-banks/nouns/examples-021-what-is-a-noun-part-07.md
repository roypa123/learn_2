**Short reminders:** **Noun** = naming word (👩 person · 🐾 animal · 📍 place · 📦 thing · 💭 idea). **Positions:** **S** subject · **O** object · **B** after *be* · **P** after a preposition. **Head noun** decides the verb ([L-021 §7.4](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-021-what-is-a-noun.md#74-noun-phrases)).

**Work words in this part:** office · boss · meeting · salary · factory · customer · engineer · colleague · manager · strike · interview · employee · shift · company · receptionist · accountant · patient · waiter · experience.

> 🔁 **Important pair:** *work* (U — no *a*, no -s: *I have a lot of work*) vs *job* (C — *a job, two jobs*) ([L-026 §7.5](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-026-countable-and-uncountable-nouns.md#75-countable--uncountable-pairs-with-similar-meanings)).

## Step A — One noun, short sentences

### EX-021-301 — "The office is open."

1. **Meaning:** The office is not closed — people can come in now. 🏢
2. **Situation:** A visitor arrives at 9 a.m. and asks if they can come in.
3. **Main lesson:** A **workplace** noun as the subject.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + *is* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The office** (S) → **is** → **open**.
6. **Subject:** *The office*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *open* — adjective.
10. **Word by word:** *office* 📍 — a room/building where people do desk work · *open* — not closed.
11. **How the rules work together:** singular noun → *is*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The shop isn't open today* (EX-021-044).
13. **Why this is correct:** S + *be* + adjective.
14. **Other versions:** *The offices are open.* · *Is the office open on Saturday?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Office is open.* → ✅ *The office* · ❌ *The office is opened.* (for the state now) → ✅ *open*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *We're open!* (a sign or what staff say)
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "OF-is".
19. **Pattern:** **The + workplace + is + open/closed.**
20. **More examples:** *The factory is closed. · The bank is open.*
21. **Quick check:** Opposite of *open*? → **Answer:** *closed*.

### EX-021-302 — "Nurses work at night."

1. **Meaning:** Nurses often have to work during the night. 🌙👩‍⚕️
2. **Situation:** Explaining why hospitals are busy 24 hours.
3. **Main lesson:** A plural **job** noun (general) as the subject.
4. **Grammar used:** plural noun + verb + *at night*.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Nurses** (S) → **work** (V) → **at night** (time).
6. **Subject:** *Nurses* (in general).
7. **Verb:** *work* — plural → no -s.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *at night* — fixed time phrase (no *the*).
10. **Word by word:** *Nurses* 👩 · *work* (verb here) · *at night*.
11. **How the rules work together:** general plural; *at night*, but *in the morning / in the evening*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Teachers work hard* (EX-021-021).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct general form and fixed phrase.
14. **Other versions:** *My sister is a nurse. She works at night.* · *Do nurses work at night?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Nurses work in the night.* (less natural) → ✅ *at night* · ❌ *Nurses works…* → ✅ *work*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Nurses do night shifts.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "NUR-siz" (2 syllables).
19. **Pattern:** **Plural job noun + work + at night / in the morning.**
20. **More examples:** *Bakers work early in the morning. · Security guards work at night.*
21. **Quick check:** *at night* or *in the night*? → **Answer:** *at night*.

3. **Main lesson:** A work-person noun with *my*.
4. **Grammar used:** *my* + noun + *is* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** **My boss** (S) → **is** → **kind**.
6. **Subject:** *My boss*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *kind*.
10. **Word by word:** *boss* 👩 — the person who tells you what to do at work (plural *bosses* — -ss + es) · *kind*.
11. **How the rules work together:** S + *be* + adjective.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** -ss + es ([L-024](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-024-plural-spelling-rules.md)).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct agreement.
14. **Other versions:** *My bosses are kind.* · *Is your boss kind?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *My boss kind.* → ✅ *is kind* · ❌ *My bosss…* → plural ✅ *bosses*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *My boss is really nice.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "BOS"; plural "BOS-iz".
19. **Pattern:** **My + noun + is + adjective.**
20. **More examples:** *My manager is strict. · My colleague is funny.*
21. **Quick check:** Plural of *boss*? → **Answer:** *bosses*.

### EX-021-304 — "The meeting starts at nine."

1. **Meaning:** The work meeting begins at 9:00. 🗓️
2. **Situation:** A colleague reminds you in the morning.
3. **Main lesson:** An event noun + timetable present.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + verb-s + *at* + time.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The meeting** (S) → **starts** → **at nine**.
6. **Subject:** *The meeting*.
7. **Verb:** *starts*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *at nine*.
10. **Word by word:** *meeting* 💭 — people coming together to talk about work · *starts*.
11. **How the rules work together:** fixed schedules → present simple.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The exam starts at ten* (EX-021-209).
13. **Why this is correct:** timetable tense.
14. **Other versions:** *The meetings start at nine.* · *When does the meeting start?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The meeting start…* → ✅ *starts* · ❌ *…in nine.* → ✅ *at nine*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *The meeting's at nine.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "MEE-ting".
19. **Pattern:** **The + event + starts at + time.**
20. **More examples:** *The training starts at ten. · The lunch break starts at one.*
21. **Quick check:** Question: *When ___ the meeting start?* → **Answer:** *does*.

### EX-021-305 — "My salary is low."

1. **Meaning:** The money I get for my job every month is not much. 💸
2. **Situation:** Explaining why you want a new job.
3. **Main lesson:** A money noun as subject; *low/high* for amounts.
4. **Grammar used:** *my* + noun + *is* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** **My salary** (S) → **is** → **low**.
6. **Subject:** *My salary*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *low*.
10. **Word by word:** *salary* 💭 — fixed money you get for a job (C: *salaries*) · *low* — small (for amounts/prices).
11. **How the rules work together:** for *salary, price, cost* → *high/low* (not *cheap/expensive*).
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The price … is very low* (EX-021-086).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct adjective for an amount.
14. **Other versions:** *My salary is high.* · *Is your salary good?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *My salary is cheap.* → ✅ *low* · ❌ *My salary is little.* → ✅ *low / small*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *I don't earn much.* (*earn* = get money for work)
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "SAL-uh-ree".
19. **Pattern:** **My + money noun + is + high/low.**
20. **More examples:** *The rent is high. · The cost is low.*
21. **Quick check:** *cheap* or *low* for a salary? → **Answer:** *low*.

### EX-021-306 — "The factory makes cars."

1. **Meaning:** In this factory, they produce cars. 🏭🚗
2. **Situation:** Driving past a big building, a child asks what it is.
3. **Main lesson:** A **place** noun as the subject doing an action (meaning the people/machines in it).
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + verb-s + plural noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The factory** (S) → **makes** (V) → **cars** (O).
6. **Subject:** *The factory*.
7. **Verb:** *makes*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *cars* — general plural.
10. **Word by word:** *factory* 📍 — a building where things are made (plural *factories*) · *makes* · *cars*.
11. **How the rules work together:** singular place noun → *makes*; general plural object.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Bees make honey* (EX-021-029).
13. **Why this is correct:** S-V-O.
14. **Other versions:** *The factories make cars.* · *What does the factory make?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The factory make cars.* → ✅ *makes* · ❌ *…makes the cars.* (general) → ✅ *cars*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *They make cars there.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "FAK-tuh-ree".
19. **Pattern:** **The + place + makes + plural noun.**
20. **More examples:** *The bakery makes bread. · The company makes phones.*
21. **Quick check:** Plural of *factory*? → **Answer:** *factories*.

### EX-021-307 — "Work starts early."

1. **Meaning:** My working day begins early in the morning.
2. **Situation:** Explaining why you go to bed early.
3. **Main lesson:** *work* as an **uncountable noun** (subject).
4. **Grammar used:** U noun + verb-s + adverb.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Work** (S) → **starts** (V) → **early**.
6. **Subject:** *Work* — the activity/time of working; no article.
7. **Verb:** *starts* (U = singular).
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *early* — adverb.
10. **Word by word:** *Work* 💭 (U here — like *school*) · *starts* · *early*.
11. **How the rules work together:** U → singular verb, no article.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *at work* (EX-021-156).
13. **Why this is correct:** *work* as U noun.
14. **Other versions:** *My work starts at seven.* · *When does work start?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The work starts early.* (possible for a specific job/task) · ❌ *A work starts early.* → ✅ no *a*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *I start work early.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "WURK STARTS UR-lee".
19. **Pattern:** **Work + starts/finishes + time.**
20. **More examples:** *School starts at eight. · Work finishes at five.*
21. **Quick check:** *a work* — correct? → **Answer:** No (U) — say *a job*.

### EX-021-308 — "The customer is always right."

1. **Meaning:** A famous business saying: always try to make customers happy.
2. **Situation:** A shop manager trains new staff.
3. **Main lesson:** *the* + singular noun for a **whole group** (all customers).
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + *is always* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The customer** (S) → **is** → **always** → **right**.
6. **Subject:** *The customer* — here = every customer (a type).
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *always right*.
10. **Word by word:** *customer* 👩 — a person who buys · *always* — after *be* · *right* — correct.
11. **How the rules work together:** *the* + singular can mean a whole type (more formal than plural *Customers are…*).
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Dogs are loyal* / *The dog is a loyal animal* ([L-023](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-023-singular-and-plural-nouns.md) EX-023-005).
13. **Why this is correct:** fixed saying; correct adverb position.
14. **Other versions:** *Customers are always right.* (same idea, plural)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The customer always is right.* → ✅ *is always* · ❌ *Customer is always right.* → ✅ *The customer*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Remember — the customer's always right!*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "KUS-tuh-mer".
19. **Pattern:** **The + type noun + is always + adjective.**
20. **More examples:** *The teacher is always busy. · The boss is always late.*
21. **Quick check:** Where does *always* go with *is*? → **Answer:** after *is*.

12. **Remember from before 🔁:** -s after /dʒ/ sounds /ɪz/ ([L-024](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-024-plural-spelling-rules.md#76-how-to-pronounce-plural--s-and--es)).

17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "en-ji-NEERZ"; "BRIJ-iz".
19. **Pattern:** **Plural job noun + verb + plural noun.**

## Step B — Work nouns with pronouns

### EX-021-311 — "My colleague Ana is very helpful. She answers all my questions."

1. **Meaning:** Ana, who works with me, helps a lot — she answers every question I ask.
2. **Situation:** Your first week at a new job.
3. **Main lesson:** Common + proper noun → *She*; plural object noun.
4. **Grammar used:** *my* + noun + name + *is very* + adj · *She* + verb-s + *all my* + plural.
5. **Sentence structure:** **My colleague Ana** (S) → **is** → **very helpful** · **She** (S) → **answers** → **all my questions** (O).
6. **Subject:** *My colleague Ana* · *She*.
7. **Verb:** *is* · *answers*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *very helpful* · *all my questions*.
10. **Word by word:** *colleague* 👩 — a person you work with · *helpful* · *all my questions* — every question I have.
11. **How the rules work together:** noun first → pronoun; *all* + *my* + plural.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *My friend Hana…* (EX-021-214).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct reference and agreement.
14. **Other versions:** *My colleagues are very helpful. They answer all my questions.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *She answers all my question.* → ✅ *questions* · ❌ *My colleague Ana she is…* → ✅ no *she*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Ana's so helpful — she answers all my questions.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** ⚠️ *colleague* = "KOL-eeg" (the *-ue* is silent).
19. **Pattern:** **My colleague + Name + is very + adj. She/He + verb-s + all my + plural.**
20. **More examples:** *My neighbour Tom is very kind. He waters my plants.*
21. **Quick check:** How do you say *colleague*? → **Answer:** "KOL-eeg".

### EX-021-312 — "Our manager has a new plan. He explains it to us."

1. **Meaning:** Our manager made a new plan, and he explains the plan to us.
2. **Situation:** A team meeting.
3. **Main lesson:** *a new plan* → *it*; *to us* after the verb.
4. **Grammar used:** noun + *has a* + adj + noun · *He explains it to us*.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Our manager** (S) → **has** → **a new plan** (O) · **He** (S) → **explains** → **it** (O) → **to us**.
6. **Subject:** *Our manager* · *He*.
7. **Verb:** *has* · *explains*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *a new plan* · *it* (= the plan) · *to us*.
10. **Word by word:** *manager* 👩 · *plan* 💭 · *explains* — makes clear · *it* · *to us*.
11. **How the rules work together:** *explain* + thing + *to* + person (❌ *explain us*).
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Give it to me* ([L-028 §7.6](../../01-beginner-grammar/pronouns/lesson-028-object-pronouns.md#76-two-objects-give-me-the-book--give-it-to-me)).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct *explain* pattern.
14. **Other versions:** *She explains the plan to the team.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *He explains us the plan.* → ✅ *He explains the plan to us.* (very common mistake!) · ❌ *He explains to us it.* → ✅ *explains it to us*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *The manager's got a new plan — he's explaining it now.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** "ik-SPLAYNZ".
19. **Pattern:** **He/She explains + it/the + noun + to + us/me/them.**
20. **More examples:** *The teacher explains the rules to the students.*
21. **Quick check:** Correct: *Can you explain me the problem?* → **Answer:** *Can you explain the problem to me?*

### EX-021-313 — "The workers are on strike. They want more money."

1. **Meaning:** The workers have stopped working to protest; they want higher pay. ✊
2. **Situation:** News report.
3. **Main lesson:** Plural people noun → *They*; *on strike* (fixed phrase); U noun.
4. **Grammar used:** plural + *are on* + noun · *They want more* + U noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The workers** (S) → **are** → **on strike** · **They** (S) → **want** → **more money** (O).
6. **Subject:** *The workers* · *They*.
7. **Verb:** *are* · *want*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *on strike* · *more money*.
10. **Word by word:** *workers* 👩 · *strike* 💭 — refusing to work as a protest · *on strike* — no article · *more* · *money* (U).
11. **How the rules work together:** fixed phrase *on strike*; *more* + U noun.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Money isn't everything* (EX-021-310).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct fixed phrase and U noun.
14. **Other versions:** *The workers went on strike yesterday.* · *Are the workers on strike?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *…are in strike.* → ✅ *on strike* · ❌ *They want more moneys.* → ✅ *money*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *The workers are striking for better pay.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** "STRYK".
19. **Pattern:** **The + plural noun + are on strike. They want more + U noun.**
20. **More examples:** *The drivers are on strike. They want better hours.*
21. **Quick check:** Preposition with *strike*? → **Answer:** *on*.

### EX-021-314 — "I have an interview tomorrow. I'm nervous about it."

1. **Meaning:** Tomorrow I have a job interview, and I feel nervous about it. 😬
2. **Situation:** Telling a friend.
3. **Main lesson:** *an interview* → *it* (after the preposition *about*).
4. **Grammar used:** *I have an* + noun + time · *I'm* + adj + *about it*.
5. **Sentence structure:** **I** → **have** → **an interview** (O) → **tomorrow** · **I** → **'m** → **nervous** → **about it**.
6. **Subject:** *I*.
7. **Verb:** *have* · *'m* (am).
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *an interview* · *nervous about it*.
10. **Word by word:** *an* — vowel sound · *interview* 💭 — a formal meeting to get a job · *nervous about* + noun/pronoun · *it* = the interview.
11. **How the rules work together:** first mention *an*; then *it* after a preposition.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *nervous* (S-001); object pronouns after prepositions ([L-028](../../01-beginner-grammar/pronouns/lesson-028-object-pronouns.md)).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct article and pronoun.
14. **Other versions:** *I had an interview yesterday. It went well.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *I have a interview.* → ✅ *an interview* · ❌ *I'm nervous for it.* (less common) → ✅ *about it*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *I've got a job interview tomorrow — I'm so nervous!*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** "IN-ter-vyoo".
19. **Pattern:** **I have a/an + noun + time. I'm nervous/excited about it.**
20. **More examples:** *I have an exam on Friday. I'm worried about it.*
21. **Quick check:** *nervous about* or *nervous of*? → **Answer:** *about* (for an event).

### EX-021-315 — "The new computers are fast. We love them."

1. **Meaning:** The new computers at work work quickly; we really like them. 💻⚡
2. **Situation:** Office workers after an upgrade.
3. **Main lesson:** Plural noun → *them*.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + adj + plural + *are* + adj · *We love them*.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The new computers** (S) → **are** → **fast** · **We** → **love** → **them** (O).
6. **Subject:** *The new computers* · *We*.
7. **Verb:** *are* · *love*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *fast* · *them*.
10. **Word by word:** *computers* · *fast* — adjective · *them* = the computers.
11. **How the rules work together:** plural → *them*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *These exercises are difficult. I don't understand them.* (EX-021-215).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct pronoun.
14. **Other versions:** *The new computer is fast. We love it.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *We love it.* (for plural) → ✅ *them* · ❌ *The new computers is fast.* → ✅ *are*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *The new computers are so quick — we love them!*

### EX-021-348 — "Her patients love her because she is kind and patient."

1. **Meaning:** The people she cares for in hospital love her, because she is nice and calm.
2. **Situation:** The story ends.
3. **Main lesson:** *patient* as a **noun** (person) and an **adjective** (calm) in one sentence!
4. **Grammar used:** *Her* + plural noun + verb + *her* + *because* + clause.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Her patients** (S) → **love** (V) → **her** (O) → **because she is kind and patient** (reason).
6. **Subject:** *Her patients*.
7. **Verb:** *love*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *her* (O) · reason clause.
10. **Word by word:** *patients* — noun (sick people) · *her* (O = Marta) · *because* · *kind* · *patient* — adjective (calm, doesn't get angry).
11. **How the rules work together:** the same word in two jobs — position shows which (after *her* + plural = noun; after *is* = adjective).
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *My tutor is very patient* (EX-021-263).
13. **Why this is correct:** each word in its position.
14. **Other versions:** *The patient likes her nurse.* (noun) · *Be patient!* (adjective)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Her patient love her…* → ✅ *patients love* · ❌ *…she is kind and patience.* → ✅ *patient* (adjective; *patience* = noun)
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Her patients adore her — she's so kind and patient.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** no comma before *because*.
18. **Pronunciation:** "PAY-shunts" / "PAY-shunt".
19. **Pattern:** **Her + plural noun + love her because she is + adj and + adj.**
20. **More examples:** *His students like him because he is funny and clever.*
21. **Quick check:** Noun form of the adjective *patient*? → **Answer:** *patience*.

## Step J — Real writing: a job advert

### EX-021-349 — "We are looking for a friendly waiter for our restaurant."

1. **Meaning:** Our restaurant wants to employ a nice waiter. 🍽️
2. **Situation:** A sign in a restaurant window / an online advert.
3. **Main lesson:** Job noun (O) with an adjective + noun after *for*.
4. **Grammar used:** *We are looking for a* + adj + noun + *for our* + noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **We** (S) → **are looking for** (V) → **a friendly waiter** (O) → **for our restaurant**.
6. **Subject:** *We*.
7. **Verb:** *are looking for* — searching for.
8. **Helping verbs:** *are*.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *a friendly waiter* · *for our restaurant*.
10. **Word by word:** *looking for* — trying to find · *friendly* · *waiter* 👩 — serves food (*wait* + *-er*; neutral: *server*) · *restaurant*.
11. **How the rules work together:** *look for* + noun; second *for* = purpose/place.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *We need an experienced driver* (EX-021-331).
13. **Why this is correct:** standard advert phrase.
14. **Other versions:** *Waiter wanted.* (short sign) · *We are hiring!*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *We are looking a waiter.* → ✅ *looking for* · ❌ *…a waiter friendly…* → ✅ *a friendly waiter*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *We're looking for a new waiter.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** in adverts, often short: *Friendly waiter wanted.*
18. **Pronunciation:** "WAY-ter"; "RES-tuh-ront".
19. **Pattern:** **We are looking for a/an + adj + job + for our + place.**
20. **More examples:** *We are looking for an experienced cook for our café.*
21. **Quick check:** *look* + which preposition = search? → **Answer:** *for*.

## Step K — Noun or verb?

### EX-021-350 — "We plan every project carefully. / The plan is ready."

1. **Meaning:** (1) We think about and organise every project carefully. (2) The plan is finished.
2. **Situation:** A project manager talks to a client.
3. **Main lesson:** *plan* as a verb (1) and a noun (2).
4. **Grammar used:** *We* + verb + *every* + noun + adverb · *The* + noun + *is* + adj.
5. **Sentence structure:** (1) **We** → **plan** (V) → **every project** (O) → **carefully** · (2) **The plan** (S) → **is** → **ready**.
6. **Subject:** *We* · *The plan*.
7. **Verb:** (1) *plan* · (2) *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** (1) *every project* · *carefully* · (2) *ready*.
10. **Word by word:** (1) *plan* after *We* → verb · *every* + singular · *carefully* — adverb · (2) *plan* after *The* → noun.
11. **How the rules work together:** position decides.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Our manager has a new plan* (EX-021-312).
13. **Why this is correct:** each word in its position.
14. **Other versions:** *She plans her week on Sundays. · We need a new plan.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *We plan every projects…* → ✅ *every project* · ❌ *The plan are ready.* → ✅ *is*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *The plan's ready — let's go!*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** "PLAN".
19. **Pattern:** **We + verb + every + noun + adverb / The + noun + is + adjective.**
20. **More examples:** *We design websites. / I love the design.*
21. **Quick check:** In *a new plan*, noun or verb? → **Answer:** noun.
