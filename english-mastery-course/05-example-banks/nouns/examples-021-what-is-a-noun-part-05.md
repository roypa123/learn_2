**Short reminders:** **Noun** = naming word (👩 person · 🐾 animal · 📍 place · 📦 thing · 💭 idea). **Positions:** **S** subject · **O** object · **B** after *be* · **P** after a preposition. **Head noun** decides the verb ([L-021 §7.4](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-021-what-is-a-noun.md#74-noun-phrases)).

**School words in this part:** classroom · board · subject · bell · exam · playground · dictionary · exercise · pencil · calculator · term · quiz · trip · headteacher · lab · equipment · locker · corridor · canteen · gym · noticeboard · ruler.

## Step A — One noun, short sentences

### EX-021-201 — "The classroom is bright."

1. **Meaning:** There is a lot of light in the classroom. ☀️🏫
2. **Situation:** A new student describes their school to their parents.
3. **Main lesson:** A **compound** place noun (*class* + *room*) as the subject.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + *is* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The classroom** (S) → **is** → **bright**.
6. **Subject:** *The classroom*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *bright* — full of light.
10. **Word by word:** *classroom* 📍 — one word: a room where lessons happen · *bright* — adjective.
11. **How the rules work together:** singular noun → *is*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** compound nouns written as one word: *bedroom, bathroom* (EX-021-118).
13. **Why this is correct:** S + *be* + adjective.
14. **Other versions:** *The classrooms are bright.* · *Is the classroom bright?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The class room is bright.* → ✅ *classroom* (one word) · ❌ *Classroom is bright.* → ✅ *The classroom*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Our classroom's really bright.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** one word.
18. **Pronunciation:** ***KLAHS**-room* (UK) / ***KLAS**-room* (US) — stress the first part.
19. **Pattern:** **The + place + is + adjective.**
20. **More examples:** *The library is quiet. · The hall is big.*
21. **Quick check:** One word or two: *class room*? → **Answer:** one: *classroom*.

### EX-021-202 — "Students need books."

1. **Meaning:** People who study must have books.
2. **Situation:** A head teacher asks for money for new books.
3. **Main lesson:** Two plural nouns in a general statement.
4. **Grammar used:** plural noun + verb + plural noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Students** (S) → **need** (V) → **books** (O).
6. **Subject:** *Students* (in general).
7. **Verb:** *need* (no -s — plural subject).
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *books* — object (in general).
10. **Word by word:** *Students* 👩 · *need* · *books* 📦.
11. **How the rules work together:** general statement → plurals with no article.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Bees make honey* (EX-021-029).
13. **Why this is correct:** S-V-O, general plurals.
14. **Other versions:** *A student needs books.* (one → *needs*) · *Do students need books?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Students needs books.* → ✅ *need* · ❌ *The students need the books.* (sounds specific) → ✅ *Students need books.*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Every student needs books.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "STOO-dents NEED BUKS".
19. **Pattern:** **Plural noun + need + plural noun.**
20. **More examples:** *Children need sleep. · Plants need water.*
21. **Quick check:** Singular version? → **Answer:** *A student needs books.*

### EX-021-203 — "The teacher writes on the board."

1. **Meaning:** The teacher puts words on the big board at the front of the class. 👩‍🏫✍️
2. **Situation:** Describing a normal lesson.
3. **Main lesson:** Person noun (S) + thing noun after *on*.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + verb-s + *on the* + noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The teacher** (S) → **writes** (V) → **on the board** (place).
6. **Subject:** *The teacher*.
7. **Verb:** *writes* — no object here (*write* can stand alone).
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *on the board* — place.
10. **Word by word:** *teacher* 👩 · *writes* (silent **w**) · *board* 📦 — whiteboard/blackboard.
11. **How the rules work together:** singular → *writes*; place phrase after the verb.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** silent **w** in *write* ([L-006](../../00-foundations/02-letters-and-sounds/lesson-006-letters-and-sounds.md)).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct agreement and preposition.
14. **Other versions:** *The teacher writes the date on the board.* (+ object) · *Does the teacher write on the board?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *…writes in the board.* → ✅ *on the board* · ❌ *The teacher write…* → ✅ *writes*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Miss is writing on the board.* (UK children call a female teacher *Miss*)
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "RYTS" / "BORD".
19. **Pattern:** **The + person + writes on the + noun.**
20. **More examples:** *The student writes in her notebook. · The doctor writes on a form.*
21. **Quick check:** *on* or *in* the board? → **Answer:** *on*.

### EX-021-204 — "My school has a big library."

1. **Meaning:** There is a large library in my school. 📚
2. **Situation:** Telling a new friend about your school.
3. **Main lesson:** Place noun (S) + place noun (O).
4. **Grammar used:** *my* + noun + *has a* + adj + noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **My school** (S) → **has** → **a big library** (O).
6. **Subject:** *My school*.
7. **Verb:** *has*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *a big library*.
10. **Word by word:** *school* 📍 · *big* · *library* 📍 (plural *libraries*).
11. **How the rules work together:** singular → *has*; *a* + adjective + noun.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Our flat has a balcony* (EX-021-154).
13. **Why this is correct:** S-V-O with correct article.
14. **Other versions:** *Our school doesn't have a library.* · *Does your school have a library?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *My school have a big library.* → ✅ *has* · ❌ *…a library big.* → ✅ *a big library*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Our school's got a huge library.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "LY-bruh-ree".
19. **Pattern:** **My + place + has a + adjective + place.**
20. **More examples:** *My school has a small gym. · Our town has a new hospital.*
21. **Quick check:** Plural of *library*? → **Answer:** *libraries*.

### EX-021-205 — "Homework is important."

1. **Meaning:** The school work you do at home matters a lot. 📝
2. **Situation:** A teacher talks to parents.
3. **Main lesson:** An **uncountable** noun as the subject.
4. **Grammar used:** U noun + *is* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Homework** (S) → **is** → **important**.
6. **Subject:** *Homework*.
7. **Verb:** *is* (U → singular).
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *important*.
10. **Word by word:** *Homework* 💭 — U (no *a*, no -s) · *important*.
11. **How the rules work together:** U noun → singular verb, no article.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *I have a lot of homework* ([L-026](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-026-countable-and-uncountable-nouns.md)).
13. **Why this is correct:** U noun + *is*.
14. **Other versions:** *My homework is easy today.* · *Is homework important?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Homeworks are important.* → ✅ *Homework is important.* · ❌ *A homework is important.* → ✅ no *a*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Homework really matters.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** one word.
18. **Pronunciation:** "HOHM-wurk".
19. **Pattern:** **U noun + is + adjective.**
20. **More examples:** *Practice is important. · Education is important.*
21. **Quick check:** Can you say *three homeworks*? → **Answer:** No → *three pieces of homework / three exercises*.

### EX-021-206 — "The bell rings at nine."

1. **Meaning:** At 9:00 the school bell makes its sound — lessons start. 🔔
2. **Situation:** Explaining the school timetable.
3. **Main lesson:** Thing noun (S) + time.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + verb-s + *at* + time.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The bell** (S) → **rings** (V) → **at nine** (time).
6. **Subject:** *The bell*.
7. **Verb:** *rings*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *at nine*.
10. **Word by word:** *bell* 📦 · *rings* — makes a bell sound · *at nine* — clock time.
11. **How the rules work together:** present simple for timetables; *at* + time.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The phone is ringing* (now) vs *The bell rings at nine* (every day) (EX-021-015).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct tense for routines.
14. **Other versions:** *The bell rang late today.* (past) · *When does the bell ring?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The bell ring at nine.* → ✅ *rings* · ❌ *…in nine.* → ✅ *at nine*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *The bell goes at nine.* (UK: *go* = ring)
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "BEL RINGZ".
19. **Pattern:** **The + noun + verb-s + at + time.**
20. **More examples:** *The bus leaves at eight. · The shop opens at ten.*
21. **Quick check:** Past of *ring*? → **Answer:** *rang*.

### EX-021-207 — "Maths is my favourite subject."

1. **Meaning:** Of all school subjects, I like maths the most. ➗➕
2. **Situation:** Talking about school with a new friend.
3. **Main lesson:** A noun that **ends in -s but is singular** (*maths*).
4. **Grammar used:** U noun + *is* + *my* + adj + noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Maths** (S) → **is** → **my favourite subject**.
6. **Subject:** *Maths* (UK; US *math*).
7. **Verb:** *is* — singular! (*maths* = one subject).
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *my favourite subject*.
10. **Word by word:** *Maths* 💭 — short for *mathematics*; looks plural, is singular · *favourite* · *subject* 💭 — a school area (maths, history, art).
11. **How the rules work together:** subjects ending in -s (*maths, physics, economics*) take *is*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The news is good* ([L-023](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-023-singular-and-plural-nouns.md)); school subjects lowercase except languages ([L-022](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-022-common-and-proper-nouns.md)).
13. **Why this is correct:** singular verb for a subject name.
14. **Other versions:** *Physics is difficult. · History is interesting.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Maths are my favourite subject.* → ✅ *is* · ❌ *…my favourite subjects.* (one subject) → ✅ *subject*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *I love maths!*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** small **m** in the middle of a sentence (*I like maths*).
18. **Pronunciation:** "MATHS" (/θs/ — difficult! tongue between teeth then **s**).
19. **Pattern:** **Subject name + is + my favourite subject.**
20. **More examples:** *Science is my favourite subject. · Art is her favourite subject.*
21. **Quick check:** *Physics (is / are) hard.* → **Answer:** *is*.

8. **Helping verbs:** none.

20. **More examples:** *My laptop is light. · My shoes are wet.*
21. **Quick check:** Opposite of *heavy*? → **Answer:** *light*.

## Step B — School nouns and pronouns

### EX-021-211 — "Our teacher is from Ireland. She teaches English."

1. **Meaning:** Our teacher comes from Ireland, and she teaches the English language.
2. **Situation:** Describing your teacher to a parent.
3. **Main lesson:** Noun → *She*; proper nouns (country, language).
4. **Grammar used:** noun + *is from* + country · *She* + verb-es + language.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Our teacher** (S) → **is** → **from Ireland** · **She** (S) → **teaches** → **English** (O).
6. **Subject:** *Our teacher* · *She*.
7. **Verb:** *is* · *teaches* (-ch + es).
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *from Ireland* · *English* (object).
10. **Word by word:** *teacher* · *Ireland* (proper, no *the*) · *She* · *teaches* · *English* (language → capital).
11. **How the rules work together:** noun first, pronoun after; -ch → -es.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *She watches videos* ([L-024](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-024-plural-spelling-rules.md#77-same-rules-for-verbs-he--she--it)).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct spellings and reference.
14. **Other versions:** *Our teacher is from Wales. He teaches music.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *She teachs English.* → ✅ *teaches* · ❌ *She teaches english.* → ✅ *English* · ❌ *…from the Ireland.* → ✅ *from Ireland*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Our teacher's Irish — she teaches English.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** capitals for *Ireland, English*.
18. **Pronunciation:** "TEE-chiz" (2 syllables).
19. **Pattern:** **Noun + is from + country. He/She teaches + subject.**
20. **More examples:** *Our coach is from Brazil. He teaches football.*
21. **Quick check:** *teachs* or *teaches*? → **Answer:** *teaches*.

### EX-021-212 — "I have a new dictionary. It has 50,000 words."

1. **Meaning:** I got a new book of word meanings; it contains fifty thousand words. 📖
2. **Situation:** Showing a friend your new study tool.
3. **Main lesson:** Thing noun → *It*; number + plural noun.
4. **Grammar used:** *I have a* + adj + noun · *It has* + number + plural.
5. **Sentence structure:** **I** → **have** → **a new dictionary** (O) · **It** (S) → **has** → **50,000 words** (O).
6. **Subject:** *I* · *It* (= the dictionary).
7. **Verb:** *have* · *has*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *a new dictionary* · *50,000 words*.
10. **Word by word:** *dictionary* 📦 (plural *dictionaries*) · *It* · *50,000* — "fifty thousand" · *words*.
11. **How the rules work together:** first mention *a*, then *it*; number + plural.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *five thousand* (no -s on *thousand* after a number) (EX-021-028).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct article, pronoun and number.
14. **Other versions:** *My dictionaries are old. They have small letters.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *It has 50,000 word.* → ✅ *words* · ❌ *It has fifty thousands words.* → ✅ *fifty thousand*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *I've got a new dictionary — fifty thousand words!*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** comma in 50,000 (English style).
18. **Pronunciation:** "DIK-shun-ree" (UK) / "DIK-shun-air-ee" (US).
19. **Pattern:** **I have a + noun. It has + number + plural noun.**
20. **More examples:** *I have a new phone. It has 128 gigabytes.*
21. **Quick check:** How do you say 50,000? → **Answer:** *fifty thousand*.

### EX-021-213 — "The students are in the hall. They are taking an exam."

1. **Meaning:** The students are in the big school room; they are doing a test now. 📝
2. **Situation:** A parent arrives and asks where the students are.
3. **Main lesson:** Plural noun → *They*; *take an exam*.
4. **Grammar used:** plural noun + *are in the* + noun · *They are* + verb-ing + *an* + noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The students** (S) → **are** → **in the hall** · **They** (S) → **are taking** (V) → **an exam** (O).
6. **Subject:** *The students* · *They*.
7. **Verb:** *are* · *are taking*.
8. **Helping verbs:** *are* (in *are taking*).
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *in the hall* · *an exam*.
10. **Word by word:** *hall* 📍 — a large room for meetings/exams · *taking* — doing (an exam) · *an exam* — vowel sound → *an*.
11. **How the rules work together:** plural → *they*; collocation *take an exam*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *an* before vowel sounds ([L-005](../../00-foundations/02-letters-and-sounds/lesson-005-vowels-and-consonants.md)).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct pronoun and collocation.
14. **Other versions:** *The student is in the hall. She is taking an exam.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *They are making an exam.* → ✅ *taking* (UK *sitting*) · ❌ *…a exam.* → ✅ *an exam*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *They're doing an exam in the hall.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** *an exam* → "a-nig-ZAM".
19. **Pattern:** **Plural noun + are in the + place. They are taking a/an + noun.**
20. **More examples:** *The children are in the gym. They are playing basketball.*
21. **Quick check:** *make* or *take* an exam? → **Answer:** *take*.

### EX-021-214 — "My friend Hana sits next to me. I help her with maths."

1. **Meaning:** My friend Hana's seat is beside mine; I help her with maths.
2. **Situation:** Describing your classroom friends.
3. **Main lesson:** Common noun + proper noun; pronouns *me* and *her*.
4. **Grammar used:** noun + name + verb-s + *next to me* · *I help her with* + subject.
5. **Sentence structure:** **My friend Hana** (S) → **sits** → **next to me** · **I** → **help** → **her** (O) → **with maths**.
6. **Subject:** *My friend Hana* · *I*.
7. **Verb:** *sits* · *help*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *next to me* · *her* (= Hana) · *with maths*.
10. **Word by word:** *friend* (common) · *Hana* (proper) · *next to* · *me* · *her* · *maths*.
11. **How the rules work together:** *help someone with something*; object pronouns after prepositions/verbs.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Can you help me with the dishes?* (EX-021-139).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct pronouns and pattern.
14. **Other versions:** *She helps me with English.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *…next to I.* → ✅ *me* · ❌ *I help she…* → ✅ *her*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Hana sits next to me — I help her with maths.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** *help her* → "HEL-per".
19. **Pattern:** **My friend + Name + sits next to me. I help him/her with + subject.**
20. **More examples:** *My friend Ali sits behind me. He helps me with science.*
21. **Quick check:** What does *her* refer to? → **Answer:** Hana.

### EX-021-215 — "These exercises are difficult. I don't understand them."

1. **Meaning:** These practice tasks are hard; I can't understand them.
2. **Situation:** A student asks a teacher for help.
3. **Main lesson:** Plural noun with *these* → *them*.
4. **Grammar used:** *These* + plural + *are* + adj · *I don't understand* + *them*.
5. **Sentence structure:** **These exercises** (S) → **are** → **difficult** · **I** → **don't understand** → **them** (O).
6. **Subject:** *These exercises* · *I*.
7. **Verb:** *are* · *understand*.
8. **Helping verbs:** *don't*.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *difficult* · *them* (= the exercises).
10. **Word by word:** *These* · *exercises* 💭 — practice tasks (C: *exercise* + **s**; the sound is /ɪz/ because *exercise* ends in a /z/ sound) · *difficult* · *them*.
11. **How the rules work together:** plural noun → *these, are, them*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *homework* (U) vs *exercises* (C) ([L-026](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-026-countable-and-uncountable-nouns.md#75-countable--uncountable-pairs-with-similar-meanings)).

19. **Pattern:** **Every + time, + name + takes the + transport + to + school/work.**
20. **More examples:** *Every day, my dad takes the train to work.*
21. **Quick check:** *by the bus* or *by bus*? → **Answer:** *by bus*.

### EX-021-247 — "Her first lesson is biology, in the science lab."

1. **Meaning:** Nadia's first class of the day is biology; it happens in the science lab. 🧬
2. **Situation:** The story continues.
3. **Main lesson:** A school subject noun (no article, lowercase) + place.
4. **Grammar used:** *her* + adj + noun + *is* + U noun + *, in the* + noun + noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Her first lesson** (S) → **is** → **biology** → **, in the science lab** (place).
6. **Subject:** *Her first lesson*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *biology* (complement) · place.
10. **Word by word:** *Her* — Nadia's · *first* — number one · *biology* — the study of living things (lowercase).
11. **How the rules work together:** subjects: no article, small letter.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Maths is my favourite subject* (EX-021-207).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct article and capitalisation.
14. **Other versions:** *Her first lesson is French.* (languages → capital)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *…is the biology…* → ✅ *biology* · ❌ *…is Biology* (in the middle of a sentence — usually small)
16. **Natural spoken English:** *She's got biology first.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** comma before the extra place information.
18. **Pronunciation:** "by-OL-uh-jee".
19. **Pattern:** **Her/His first lesson is + subject, in the + place.**
20. **More examples:** *My last lesson is art, in Room 5.*
21. **Quick check:** Capital or small: *biology*? *French*? → **Answer:** *biology* small, *French* capital.

### EX-021-248 — "At lunch, she eats a sandwich with her friends in the canteen."

1. **Meaning:** At lunchtime, Nadia eats a sandwich with her friends in the school dining room. 🥪
2. **Situation:** The story ends.
3. **Main lesson:** Many nouns in one sentence: meal, food, people, place.
4. **Grammar used:** *At* + meal + *she* + verb-s + *a* + noun + *with her* + plural + *in the* + noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **At lunch,** → **she** (S) → **eats** (V) → **a sandwich** (O) → **with her friends** → **in the canteen**.
6. **Subject:** *she* (= Nadia).
7. **Verb:** *eats*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *a sandwich* · *with her friends* · *in the canteen*.
10. **Word by word:** *lunch* — meal (no article; *at lunch* = at lunchtime) · *sandwich* (C, *a*) · *friends* · *canteen*.
11. **How the rules work together:** S-V-O-with whom-where.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The canteen is next to the gym* (EX-021-238).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct articles and order.
14. **Other versions:** *At lunch, they eat sandwiches in the canteen.* (plural)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *At the lunch…* → ✅ *At lunch* · ❌ *…eats sandwich…* → ✅ *a sandwich*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *She has lunch with her mates in the canteen.* (*mates* = friends, UK informal)
17. **Writing & punctuation:** comma after *At lunch*.
18. **Pronunciation:** "SAN-wich" (the **d** is usually silent).
19. **Pattern:** **At + meal, she eats a + noun + with her + plural + in the + place.**
20. **More examples:** *At breakfast, he drinks a coffee with his dad in the kitchen.*
21. **Quick check:** Silent letter in *sandwich*? → **Answer:** *d*.

## Step J — School nouns in real writing: an email to a teacher

> *Dear Mr Patel,*
> *I am sorry, but I can't come to **class** tomorrow. I have a **doctor's appointment** at ten. Could you send me the **homework**, please?*
> *Thank you, Leo*

### EX-021-249 — "I have a doctor's appointment at ten."

1. **Meaning:** I must go to see a doctor at 10:00 (that's why I can't come to class). 🩺
2. **Situation:** A polite email to a teacher.
3. **Main lesson:** Possessive used to describe a type of noun (*a doctor's appointment*).
4. **Grammar used:** *I have a* + possessive noun + noun + *at* + time.
5. **Sentence structure:** **I** (S) → **have** (V) → **a doctor's appointment** (O) → **at ten** (time).
6. **Subject:** *I*.
7. **Verb:** *have*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *a doctor's appointment* · *at ten*.
10. **Word by word:** *a* — goes with *appointment* (not *doctor*) · *doctor's* — type of appointment (with a doctor) · *appointment* 💭 — a planned meeting at a fixed time.
11. **How the rules work together:** in *a doctor's appointment*, *a* belongs to the head noun *appointment*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** possessive *'s* ([L-037](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-037-possessive-s.md)); *at* + time.
13. **Why this is correct:** natural phrase and preposition.
14. **Other versions:** *I have a dentist's appointment.* · *I have an appointment with the doctor.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *I have a doctor appointment.* (common in US, less in UK) · ❌ *I have an appointment of the doctor.* → ✅ *with the doctor* / *a doctor's appointment*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *I've got a doctor's appointment.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** in emails, full sentences and polite forms (*Dear…*, *Could you… please?*).
18. **Pronunciation:** "a-POYNT-ment".
19. **Pattern:** **I have a + person's + appointment + at + time.**
20. **More examples:** *She has a hairdresser's appointment on Friday.*
21. **Quick check:** Which noun does *a* go with? → **Answer:** *appointment*.

## Step K — Noun or verb? School words

### EX-021-250 — "We test the students every month. / The test is on Monday."

1. **Meaning:** (1) We give the students a test each month. (2) The test will happen on Monday.
2. **Situation:** A teacher explaining the school system to parents.
3. **Main lesson:** *test* as a verb (1) and a noun (2).
4. **Grammar used:** *We* + verb + *the* + plural + time · *the* + noun + *is on* + day.
5. **Sentence structure:** (1) **We** → **test** (V) → **the students** (O) → **every month** · (2) **The test** (S) → **is** → **on Monday**.
6. **Subject:** *We* · *The test*.
7. **Verb:** (1) *test* · (2) *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** (1) *the students* · (2) *on Monday*.
10. **Word by word:** (1) *test* after *We* → verb · (2) *test* after *The* → noun 💭.
11. **How the rules work together:** position decides the job.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** [L-021 §7.5](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-021-what-is-a-noun.md#75-same-word-noun-or-verb).
13. **Why this is correct:** each word in its natural position.
14. **Other versions:** *The teacher tests us on Fridays. · I passed the test!*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *We test to the students.* → ✅ *test the students* · ❌ *The test is in Monday.* → ✅ *on Monday*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *We've got a test on Monday.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** "TEST" (same for noun and verb).
19. **Pattern:** **We + verb + the + noun / The + noun + is on + day.**
20. **More examples:** *We study every evening. / I have a study in my house.* (*study* = a small room for working)
21. **Quick check:** In *I passed the test*, noun or verb? → **Answer:** noun.
