# Example Bank 026 — Countable and Uncountable Nouns (EX-026-013 to EX-026-100)

**For lesson:** [L-026 — Countable and Uncountable Nouns](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-026-countable-and-uncountable-nouns.md) · EX-026-001 to 012 are in the lesson.

## How to use this bank

- **Cover the notes.** For each **bold** noun: **C** (countable) or **U** (uncountable)? Then check the partners: *a/an*, -s, *is/are*, *much/many*.
- **Key:** **C** countable · **U** uncountable · **C/U** both (meaning changes)

> 🔁 U nouns: no *a/an*, no -s, singular verb, *much / a little / How much*. Count them with *a glass of, a piece of…* ([L-026 §7](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-026-countable-and-uncountable-nouns.md#7-detailed-explanation)).

---

## Step 1 — C or U? Very short (EX-026-013 to 022)

**EX-026-013** — *I have an **apple**.* — C · *an*
**EX-026-014** — *I drink **milk**.* — U · no *a*
**EX-026-015** — ***Rice** is cheap.* — U · *is*
**EX-026-016** — *The **eggs** are fresh.* — C plural · *are*
**EX-026-017** — ***Music** helps me relax.* — U
**EX-026-018** — *She has a **car**.* — C
**EX-026-019** — ***Sugar** is sweet.* — U
**EX-026-020** — *I need **help**.* — U · ❌ *a help* (but: *You're a great help!* — fixed phrase)
**EX-026-021** — *We have two **dogs**.* — C plural
**EX-026-022** — ***Snow** is beautiful.* — U (weather)

---

## Step 2 — Different nouns (EX-026-023 to 032)

**EX-026-023** — *I eat **bread** every morning.* — U · ❌ *a bread*
**EX-026-024** — *There's **sand** in my shoes!* — U (material) · *There's* = There is
**EX-026-025** — *She bought three **books**.* — C
**EX-026-026** — *This table is made of **wood**.* — U (material). *a wood* (C) = a small forest — different meaning!
**EX-026-027** — *I love **chocolate**.* — U (in general). *a box of chocolates* — C (single sweets) 🍫
**EX-026-028** — *We need **oil** for the car.* — U (liquid)
**EX-026-029** — *My grandfather has a lot of **knowledge**.* — U · *knowledge* = what you know
**EX-026-030** — *Put the **butter** in the fridge.* — U
**EX-026-031** — *The children had **fun** at the park.* — U · ❌ *a fun*
**EX-026-032** — *I have three **questions**.* — C

---

## Step 3 — With pronouns (it / they) (EX-026-033 to 042)

> 🔁 U nouns → **it** (singular). Plural C → **they**.

**EX-026-033** — *I like this **music**. **It** is relaxing.* — U → *it*
**EX-026-034** — *Where are the **eggs**? **They** are in the fridge.* — C → *they*
**EX-026-035** — *The **soup** is hot. Don't drink **it** yet.* — U → *it*
**EX-026-036** — *Thank you for the **advice**. **It** was very useful.* — U → *it* · ❌ *They were useful*
**EX-026-037** — *My **luggage** is heavy. Can you help me carry **it**?* — U → *it*
**EX-026-038** — *My **bags** are heavy. Can you help me carry **them**?* — C → *them*
**EX-026-039** — *The **news** is terrible. I can't believe **it**.* — U → *it*
**EX-026-040** — *This **money** is for you. Spend **it** wisely.* — U → *it* · *wisely* = in a clever way
**EX-026-041** — *I bought new **furniture**. **It** arrives tomorrow.* — U → *it*, *arrives* (-s)
**EX-026-042** — *Those **cookies** look good. Did you make **them**?* — C → *them*

---

## Step 4 — Questions and negatives (EX-026-043 to 052)

**EX-026-043** — *How much **water** do you drink every day?* — U → *How much*
**EX-026-044** — *How many **glasses** of water do you drink?* — C (*glasses*) → *How many*
**EX-026-045** — *We don't have much **time**.* — U → *much* (common in negatives)
**EX-026-046** — *We don't have many **chairs**.* — C → *many*
**EX-026-047** — *Is there any **milk**?* — U → *Is there*
**EX-026-048** — *Are there any **bananas**?* — C plural → *Are there*
**EX-026-049** — *I don't need any **help**, thanks.* — U
**EX-026-050** — *Is the **weather** good in May?* — U · ❌ *a good weather*
**EX-026-051** — *How much **luggage** can I take on the plane?* — U
**EX-026-052** — *There isn't much **traffic** at night.* — U

---

## Step 5 — Different times (EX-026-053 to 060)

**EX-026-053** — *The **food** was delicious.* — U → *was*
**EX-026-054** — *The **dishes** were delicious.* — C plural → *were*
**EX-026-055** — *There will be **rain** tomorrow.* — U · *There will be* (future)
**EX-026-056** — *We had a lot of **homework** last week.* — U
**EX-026-057** — *I spent all my **money** on clothes.* — U · *spent* = past of *spend*
**EX-026-058** — *The **information** was wrong.* — U → *was*
**EX-026-059** — *Last night, there was a lot of **noise** in the street.* — U (general sound). *a strange noise* — C (one sound)
**EX-026-060** — *He gave me three **pieces of advice**.* — *pieces* C · *advice* U

---

## Step 6 — C/U + adjectives (EX-026-061 to 070)

> 🔁 With U nouns, an adjective still doesn't need *a*: ❌ *a good advice* → ✅ *good advice*. (Exception: some fixed phrases like *a good time, a great help*.)

**EX-026-061** — *That's **good advice**.* — U · ❌ *a good advice*
**EX-026-062** — *We had **terrible weather** on holiday.* — U · ❌ *a terrible weather*
**EX-026-063** — *She has **beautiful long hair**.* — U (all hair) · ❌ *hairs*
**EX-026-064** — *I like **strong coffee**.* — U (in general)
**EX-026-065** — *They sell **fresh bread** and **hot drinks**.* — U · C plural
**EX-026-066** — *We need **new equipment** for the lab.* — U · ❌ *equipments*
**EX-026-067** — *This is **important information**.* — U
**EX-026-068** — *I have **some interesting news**!* — U · *some*
**EX-026-069** — *We had **a great time** at the party.* — fixed phrase: *a … time* = an experience (C)
**EX-026-070** — *Children need **healthy food** and **clean water**.* — U · U

---

## Step 7 — Units and containers (EX-026-071 to 078)

**EX-026-071** — *Can I have **a slice of** pizza?* — *slice* C · *pizza* U here (also C: *two pizzas* = whole pizzas)
**EX-026-072** — *I bought **two loaves of** bread.* — *loaves* (f → ves, [L-024](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-024-plural-spelling-rules.md))
**EX-026-073** — *Add **a spoonful of** sugar.* — *spoonful* = as much as a spoon holds
**EX-026-074** — *She drinks **a bottle of** water at the gym.* — container
**EX-026-075** — *We need **a kilo of** rice and **two litres of** milk.* — measures
**EX-026-076** — *There's **a piece of** paper on the floor.* — *paper* U (material)
**EX-026-077** — *Would you like **a bowl of** soup?* — container
**EX-026-078** — *He ate **three bars of** chocolate!* — *bars* C · *chocolate* U

---

## Step 8 — Longer sentences (EX-026-079 to 086)

**EX-026-079** — *There is a lot of furniture in the living room, but there aren't many chairs.*
- **furniture** U → *is* · **chairs** C → *aren't many*.

**EX-026-080** — *The teacher gave us some useful information and three short exercises for homework.*
- **information** U · **exercises** C · **homework** U.

**EX-026-081** — *My suitcase is full, so I can't take any more luggage.*
- **suitcase** C · **luggage** U.

**EX-026-082** — *We don't have much money, but we have a lot of good friends.*
- **money** U → *much* · **friends** C → *a lot of*.

**EX-026-083** — *How much sugar and how many eggs do I need for this cake?*
- **sugar** U → *How much* · **eggs** C → *How many*.

**EX-026-084** — *The news about the storm is bad: there will be heavy rain and strong wind tonight.*
- **news, rain, wind** — all U.

**EX-026-085** — *I'd like two coffees, a glass of orange juice and a piece of chocolate cake, please.*
- **coffees** (C — cups, ordering) · **juice** U · **cake** U (with *a piece of*).

**EX-026-086** — *She has five years of experience in nursing and many good experiences to share.*
- **experience** U (knowledge from work) · **experiences** C (events that happened to her).

## Step 9 — Conversations (EX-026-087 to 090)

**EX-026-087**
> **A:** Do you take **sugar**?
> **B:** Just **a little**, please.
- U → *a little*.

**EX-026-088**
> **A:** How many **bags** do you have?
> **B:** Just this one. I don't have much **luggage**.
- **bags** C · **luggage** U.

**EX-026-089**
> **A:** I have a problem. Can I ask you for some **advice**?
> **B:** Of course. What's wrong?
- U → *some advice*.

**EX-026-090**
> **A:** What's the **weather** like?
> **B:** Terrible! There's a lot of **rain** and **wind**.
- *What's … like?* = describe it. All U.

## Step 10 — Short story (EX-026-091 to 093)

> *(1) On Saturday, Leo went shopping for a picnic. (2) He bought some **cheese**, two **loaves** of **bread**, a bag of **apples** and a big bottle of **water**. (3) But it started to rain, so they had the picnic in the living room!*

**EX-026-091** — (1) *a picnic* — C (an event: a meal outside).
**EX-026-092** — (2) **cheese** U (*some*) · **loaves** C · **bread** U · **apples** C plural · **water** U.
**EX-026-093** — (3) *rain* here is a verb (*it started to rain*). As a noun it's U: *a lot of rain*.

---

## Step 11 — Real writing: a recipe (EX-026-094 to 096)

> ***Easy pancakes (for 4 people)***
> *You need: 200 grams of **flour**, 2 **eggs**, 300 ml of **milk**, a pinch of **salt** and some **butter** for the pan.*

**EX-026-094** — *200 grams of flour / 300 ml of milk* — measures + U nouns.
**EX-026-095** — *2 eggs* — C · number directly.
**EX-026-096** — *a pinch of salt* — *pinch* = a very small amount you hold between two fingers · *some butter* — U.

---

## Step 12 — Same word, C or U? (EX-026-097 to 100)

**EX-026-097** — *I need some **paper**. / I read **a paper** every morning.*
- U (material) / C (a newspaper).

**EX-026-098** — *Is there **room** in the car? / Our flat has three **rooms**.*
- U (space) / C (rooms in a house).

**EX-026-099** — ***Light** travels very fast. / Turn off **the lights**, please.*
- U (energy from the sun) / C (lamps).

**EX-026-100** — *I don't like **lamb**. / Look at the baby **lambs**!*
- U (the meat) / C (young sheep 🐑).
