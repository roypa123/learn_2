**Short reminders:** **Noun** = naming word (👩 person · 🐾 animal · 📍 place · 📦 thing · 💭 idea). **Positions:** **S** subject · **O** object · **B** after *be* · **P** after a preposition. **Head noun** decides the verb ([L-021 §7.4](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-021-what-is-a-noun.md#74-noun-phrases)).

> 🔁 **New head-noun traps in this part:** *one of the* + **plural** noun → **singular** verb (*One of the bridges **is** closed*). *each / every* + **singular** noun → singular verb (*Each house **has**…*).

**New words in this part:** firefighter · bin · traffic lights · statue · rent · skyline · steps · upstairs · postman · factory · warehouse · fire · green space · sandy · three-bedroom · independent · ground floor · high street · rush hour · hairdresser · toilets · advert · bright · block of flats.

## Step A — One noun, short sentences

### EX-021-751 — "Firefighters are brave."

1. **Meaning:** People who stop fires are not afraid of danger. 🧑‍🚒🔥
2. **Situation:** A school talk about city jobs.
3. **Main lesson:** A plural job noun in general as the subject.
4. **Grammar used:** plural noun + *are* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Firefighters** (S) → **are** → **brave**.
6. **Subject:** *Firefighters* (all firefighters).
7. **Verb:** *are*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *brave* — not afraid.
10. **Word by word:** *firefighters* 👩 — compound noun (*fire* + *fighter*) · *brave*.
11. **How the rules work together:** general plural → no *the*; plural → *are*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Teachers work hard* (EX-021-021) — general plural job noun.
13. **Why this is correct:** plural + *are*.
14. **Other versions:** *A firefighter is brave.* · *My uncle is a firefighter.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Firefighters is brave.* → ✅ *are* · ❌ *The firefighters are brave.* (only for specific ones)
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Firefighters are so brave.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** *firefighter* — one word (older word: *fireman*).
18. **Pronunciation:** "FY-er-fy-terz".
19. **Pattern:** **Plural job noun + are + adjective.**
20. **More examples:** *Nurses are kind. · Police officers are busy.*
21. **Quick check:** Two nouns inside *firefighter*? → **Answer:** *fire* + *fighter*.

2. **Situation:** Rubbish collection is late.
3. **Main lesson:** plural thing noun → *are*.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + plural noun + *are* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The bins** (S) → **are** → **full**.
6. **Subject:** *The bins*.
7. **Verb:** *are*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *full*.
10. **Word by word:** *bins* 📦 — UK: containers for rubbish (US *trash cans*) · *full*.
11. **How the rules work together:** plural → *are*.

18. **Pronunciation:** "BINZ".
19. **Pattern:** **The + plural noun + are + adjective.**
20. **More examples:** *The bottles are empty. · The boxes are heavy.*
21. **Quick check:** US words for *bins*? → **Answer:** *trash cans*.

### EX-021-753 — "The traffic lights are red."

1. **Meaning:** The road signals show red — cars must stop. 🚦🔴
2. **Situation:** A driving lesson.
3. **Main lesson:** noun + noun with a **plural** head (*lights*).
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + plural noun + *are* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The traffic lights** (S) → **are** → **red**.
6. **Subject:** *The traffic lights* (head **lights**).
7. **Verb:** *are*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *red*.
10. **Word by word:** *traffic* — describes (U on its own) · *lights* — head, plural · *red*.
11. **How the rules work together:** head plural → *are*; *traffic* stays singular as a describing noun.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *…turn left at the traffic lights* (EX-021-642).
13. **Why this is correct:** agreement with head noun.
14. **Other versions:** *The traffic lights are green — go!*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The traffics lights…* → ✅ *traffic lights* · ❌ *The traffic lights is red.* → ✅ *are*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *The lights are red.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two words.
18. **Pronunciation:** "TRAF-ik-LYTS".
19. **Pattern:** **The + noun + plural noun + are + adjective.**
20. **More examples:** *The street lamps are on.*
21. **Quick check:** Head noun? → **Answer:** *lights*.

### EX-021-754 — "This area is noisy."

1. **Meaning:** There is a lot of noise in this part of town. 🔊🏙️
2. **Situation:** Looking for a quiet place to live.
3. **Main lesson:** *this* + noun + adjective made from a noun (*noise* → *noisy*).
4. **Grammar used:** *This* + noun + *is* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** **This area** (S) → **is** → **noisy**.
6. **Subject:** *This area*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *noisy*.
10. **Word by word:** *This* — near me / here · *area* 📍 — part of a city · *noisy* — adjective from *noise*.
11. **How the rules work together:** *this* + singular → *is*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The noise from the kitchen in this restaurant is terrible* (EX-021-588).
13. **Why this is correct:** S + *be* + adjective.
14. **Other versions:** *These areas are noisy.* · *This area is quiet.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *This area is noise.* → ✅ *noisy* · ❌ *This areas…* → ✅ *These areas*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *It's really noisy round here.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "AIR-ee-uh" · "NOY-zee".
19. **Pattern:** **This + noun + is + adjective.**
20. **More examples:** *This street is dark. · This park is clean.*
21. **Quick check:** Adjective from *noise*? → **Answer:** *noisy*.

### EX-021-756 — "Rent is very high in the city centre."

1. **Meaning:** In the middle of the city, people pay a lot to live in a flat. 🏢💸
2. **Situation:** Friends discuss moving.
3. **Main lesson:** U noun *rent* (in general) + *high*.
4. **Grammar used:** U noun + *is very* + adjective + *in the* + noun + noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Rent** (S) → **is** → **very high** → **in the city centre** (P).
6. **Subject:** *Rent*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *very high* · *in the city centre*.
10. **Word by word:** *rent* 💭 — money paid to live in a place (U in general) · *high* — used with prices/costs.
11. **How the rules work together:** U → *is*; prices are *high/low*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The cost of petrol in this country is very high* (EX-021-638) · *I pay the rent on the first of the month* (EX-021-500).
13. **Why this is correct:** U + *is* + *high*.
14. **Other versions:** *Rents are very high in London.* (plural for many rents) · *The rent for my flat is high.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Rent is very expensive.* (better: *Rent is very high* / *Flats are expensive*) · ❌ *…in city centre.* → ✅ *in the city centre*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Rent in the centre is crazy.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "RENT".
19. **Pattern:** **U noun + is very high + in the + place.**
20. **More examples:** *Tax is high in this country.*
21. **Quick check:** *high* or *expensive* with *rent*? → **Answer:** *high*.

### EX-021-759 — "The market is noisy and colourful."

1. **Meaning:** The market is loud and full of bright colours. 🛍️🎨🔊
2. **Situation:** Describing a market in a travel diary.
3. **Main lesson:** one noun + two adjectives joined with *and*.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + *is* + adj + *and* + adj.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The market** (S) → **is** → **noisy and colourful**.
6. **Subject:** *The market*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *noisy and colourful*.
10. **Word by word:** *market* 📍 · *noisy* · *and* · *colourful* — *colour* + *-ful*.
11. **How the rules work together:** two adjectives after *be* → *and* between them.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Every Saturday, Grandma goes to the market* (EX-021-443).
13. **Why this is correct:** S + *be* + adj + *and* + adj.
14. **Other versions:** *It's a noisy, colourful market.* (before the noun → comma)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The market is noisy colourful.* → ✅ *noisy and colourful* · ❌ *colourfull* → ✅ *colourful*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *The market's loud and really colourful.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** UK *colourful* / US *colorful*.
18. **Pronunciation:** "KUL-er-ful".
19. **Pattern:** **The + noun + is + adjective + and + adjective.**
20. **More examples:** *The city is big and busy.*
21. **Quick check:** After *be*, how do you join two adjectives? → **Answer:** with *and*.

## Step B — City nouns with pronouns

### EX-021-761 — "My flat is small, but I love it."

1. **Meaning:** My flat isn't big, but I like it very much. 🏠❤️
2. **Situation:** Talking about your home.
3. **Main lesson:** *my flat* → *it*; contrast with *but*.
4. **Grammar used:** *My* + noun + *is* + adj, *but I love it*.
5. **Sentence structure:** **My flat** (S) → **is** → **small** · **but** · **I** (S) → **love** (V) → **it** (O).
6. **Subject:** *My flat* · *I*.
7. **Verb:** *is* · *love*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *small* · *it*.
10. **Word by word:** *flat* (US *apartment*) · *small* · *but* — contrast · *it* — the flat.
11. **How the rules work together:** thing → *it*; comma before *but* joining two clauses.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *A: Where do you live? B: In a small flat above a bakery* (EX-021-742).
13. **Why this is correct:** pronoun + contrast.
14. **Other versions:** *My flat is old, but it's comfortable.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *…but I love.* → ✅ *I love it* (*love* needs an object) · ❌ *…but I love him.* → ✅ *it*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *It's tiny, but I love my flat.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** comma before *but*.
18. **Pronunciation:** "my-FLAT-iz-SMAWL".
19. **Pattern:** **My + noun + is + adj, but I love it.**
20. **More examples:** *My car is old, but I love it.*
21. **Quick check:** *I love* alone — complete here? → **Answer:** No: *I love it*.

### EX-021-762 — "The neighbours upstairs are noisy. I can hear them every night."

1. **Meaning:** The people who live above me make a lot of noise; I hear them each night. 🔊⬆️
2. **Situation:** Complaining to a friend.
3. **Main lesson:** noun + place word after it (*upstairs*) → *them*.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + plural noun + *upstairs* + *are* + adj · *I can hear them* + time.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The neighbours upstairs** (S) → **are** → **noisy** · **I** (S) → **can hear** (V) → **them** (O) → **every night**.
6. **Subject:** *The neighbours upstairs* (head **neighbours**) · *I*.
7. **Verb:** *are* · *hear*.
8. **Helping verbs:** *can*.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *noisy* · *them* · *every night*.
10. **Word by word:** *neighbours* · *upstairs* — on the floor above (comes **after** the noun here) · *them* — the neighbours.
11. **How the rules work together:** plural people → *them*; *can* + base verb.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *This area is noisy* (EX-021-754).
13. **Why this is correct:** agreement + object pronoun.
14. **Other versions:** *The man upstairs is noisy. I can hear him every night.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The upstairs neighbours are noisy.* (also correct!) · ❌ *I can hear they.* → ✅ *them*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *My upstairs neighbours are so loud — I hear them every night.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** "up-STAIRZ".
19. **Pattern:** **The + plural noun + upstairs + are + adj. I can hear them + time.**
20. **More examples:** *The children next door are loud. I can hear them in the morning.*
21. **Quick check:** Object pronoun for *the neighbours*? → **Answer:** *them*.

### EX-021-763 — "Our postman is very friendly. He always says hello."

1. **Meaning:** The man who brings our letters is nice; he greets us every time. 📬👋
2. **Situation:** Talking about people in your street.
3. **Main lesson:** person noun → *he*; frequency adverb before the main verb.
4. **Grammar used:** *Our* + noun + *is very* + adj · *He always* + verb-s + *hello*.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Our postman** (S) → **is** → **very friendly** · **He** (S) → **always** → **says** (V) → **hello** (O).
6. **Subject:** *Our postman* · *He*.
7. **Verb:** *is* · *says*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *very friendly* · *hello*.
10. **Word by word:** *postman* 👨 — person who delivers post (plural *postmen*; neutral *postal worker*) · *always* — every time · *says hello*.
11. **How the rules work together:** man → *he*; *always* goes before normal verbs.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Our neighbours have a dog. It barks at the postman* (EX-021-112).
13. **Why this is correct:** pronoun + adverb position.
14. **Other versions:** *Our postwoman is very friendly. She always says hello.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *He says always hello.* → ✅ *He always says hello.* · ❌ *…postmans* → ✅ *postmen*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Our postman's lovely — he always stops to chat.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** *postman* — one word.
18. **Pronunciation:** "POHST-mun" · *says* = "SEZ".
19. **Pattern:** **Our + person noun + is very + adj. He always + verb-s + …**
20. **More examples:** *Our teacher is very kind. She always smiles.*
21. **Quick check:** Plural of *postman*? → **Answer:** *postmen*.

### EX-021-764 — "This city has many parks. My favourite one is Hyde Park."

1. **Meaning:** There are lots of parks in this city; the one I like most is Hyde Park. 🌳🇬🇧
2. **Situation:** Showing a visitor around London.
3. **Main lesson:** *one* replaces *park*; proper noun for a specific park.
4. **Grammar used:** *This* + noun + *has many* + plural noun · *My favourite one is* + proper noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **This city** (S) → **has** (V) → **many parks** (O) · **My favourite one** (S) → **is** → **Hyde Park** (B).
6. **Subject:** *This city* · *My favourite one*.
7. **Verb:** *has* · *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *many parks* · *Hyde Park*.
10. **Word by word:** *many* + plural · *favourite* · *one* = park · *Hyde Park* — proper noun, both words capital, no *the*.
11. **How the rules work together:** *one* avoids repeating the noun; names of parks have capitals and usually no *the*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *A: Is there a bank near here? B: Yes, there's one on Green Street* (EX-021-741).
13. **Why this is correct:** natural pronoun *one*; proper noun.
14. **Other versions:** *My favourite is Hyde Park.* (*one* can be dropped)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *…My favourite one is the Hyde Park.* → ✅ *Hyde Park* · ❌ *…many park.* → ✅ *many parks*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *There are loads of parks — Hyde Park's my favourite.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** capital *H* and *P*.
18. **Pronunciation:** "HYD-PARK".
19. **Pattern:** **This + noun + has many + plural noun. My favourite one is + name.**
20. **More examples:** *This city has many museums. My favourite one is the Science Museum.* (some names do take *the*)
21. **Quick check:** What does *one* replace? → **Answer:** *park*.

### EX-021-765 — "The firefighters arrived quickly. They saved the family."

1. **Meaning:** The firefighters came fast and rescued the family. 🚒👨‍👩‍👧
2. **Situation:** A local news report.
3. **Main lesson:** *the firefighters* → *they*; *family* as a singular object.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + plural noun + past verb + adverb · *They* + past verb + *the* + noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The firefighters** (S) → **arrived** (V) → **quickly** · **They** (S) → **saved** (V) → **the family** (O).
6. **Subject:** *The firefighters* · *They*.
7. **Verb:** *arrived* · *saved*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *quickly* · *the family*.
10. **Word by word:** *arrived* · *quickly* — adverb (how) · *saved* — rescued · *family* — a group noun (C: *families*).
11. **How the rules work together:** plural → *they*; past simple for a finished event.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Firefighters are brave* (EX-021-751) — general; here specific → *the*.
13. **Why this is correct:** clear pronoun; past forms.
14. **Other versions:** *The firefighter arrived quickly. She saved the family.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *They save the family yesterday.* → ✅ *saved* · ❌ *The firefighters arrived quick.* → ✅ *quickly* (adverb)
16. **Natural spoken English:** *The fire brigade got there fast and saved everyone.*

11. **How the rules work together:** *the lake* because the reader can guess which one (the park's lake).
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The new park in the middle of the city has a lake and a café* (EX-021-739).
13. **Why this is correct:** habit → present simple; article by context.
14. **Other versions:** *Every morning, she walks around the park with her friend.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Every evening, he run…* → ✅ *runs* · ❌ *Every evenings…* → ✅ *Every evening*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *He goes running round the lake with his dog every evening.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** comma after *Every evening*.
18. **Pronunciation:** "uh-ROWND-thuh-LAYK".
19. **Pattern:** **Every + time, he + verb-s + around the + noun + with his + noun.**
20. **More examples:** *Every Sunday, she cycles along the river with her son.*
21. **Quick check:** Why *the lake* and not *a lake*? → **Answer:** the reader knows which lake (the one in the park).

## Step J — Real writing: a flat advert

> 🏠 **TO RENT** — *Bright two-bedroom **flat** near the **station**. The **rent** includes **water** and **electricity**. Call 07700 900123.*

### EX-021-797 — "Bright two-bedroom flat near the station."

1. **Meaning:** A flat with lots of light and two bedrooms, close to the station, is available. ☀️🏢🚉
2. **Situation:** An online advert.
3. **Main lesson:** an advert can be **only a noun phrase** (no verb, no *a*).
4. **Grammar used:** adj + number-noun + noun + *near the* + noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Bright two-bedroom flat** (head **flat**) → **near the station** (P). (No verb — advert style.)
6. **Subject:** none (noun phrase only).
7. **Verb:** none.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *near the station*.
10. **Word by word:** *Bright* — full of light · *two-bedroom* — no *-s* · *flat* — head · *near the station*.
11. **How the rules work together:** adverts and headlines often drop *a/the* and verbs to save space.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *For rent: two-bedroom flat in the city centre* (EX-021-195) · *They live in a three-bedroom house* (EX-021-781).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct for advert style (not for normal sentences).
14. **Other versions:** *There is a bright two-bedroom flat near the station for rent.* (full sentence)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Bright two-bedrooms flat* → ✅ *two-bedroom* · ❌ In a normal essay: *I live in bright flat.* → ✅ *a bright flat*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *It's a bright two-bed flat near the station.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** adverts may have no verb; normal sentences need one.
18. **Pronunciation:** "BRYT-TOO-BED-room-FLAT".
19. **Pattern:** **Adj + number-noun + noun + near the + place.** (advert style)
20. **More examples:** *Large garden flat near the park. · Quiet room in shared house.*
21. **Quick check:** Can a normal sentence drop *a* like an advert? → **Answer:** No.

### EX-021-798 — "The rent includes water and electricity."

1. **Meaning:** The money you pay for the flat also pays for water and electricity. 💧⚡
2. **Situation:** The advert continues.
3. **Main lesson:** two U nouns as the object (no *a*, no *-s*).
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + verb-s + U noun + *and* + U noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The rent** (S) → **includes** (V) → **water and electricity** (O).
6. **Subject:** *The rent*.
7. **Verb:** *includes*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *water and electricity*.
10. **Word by word:** *rent* · *includes* — has as a part · *water* U · *electricity* U.
11. **How the rules work together:** singular subject → *-s*; U nouns → no article.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Is breakfast included in the price?* (EX-021-620) · *Yesterday, I paid the electricity bill* (EX-021-475).
13. **Why this is correct:** agreement + U nouns.
14. **Other versions:** *Water and electricity are included in the rent.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The rent include…* → ✅ *includes* · ❌ *…a water and an electricity.* → ✅ *water and electricity*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Bills are included.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "in-KLOODZ" · "ih-lek-TRIS-uh-tee".
19. **Pattern:** **The + noun + includes + U noun + and + U noun.**
20. **More examples:** *The price includes breakfast and dinner.*
21. **Quick check:** *an electricity* — correct? → **Answer:** No: *electricity* (U).

## Step K — Noun or verb?

### EX-021-799 — "Let's meet at the station. / We had a meeting at the town hall."

1. **Meaning:** (1) Let's see each other at the station. (2) We had an organised discussion at the town hall. 🤝 / 🗣️
2. **Situation:** Making plans; then talking about a local meeting.
3. **Main lesson:** verb *meet* (1) and its *-ing* noun *meeting* (2, C).
4. **Grammar used:** *Let's* + verb + *at the* + noun · *We had a* + noun + *at the* + noun + noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** (1) **(we)** → **meet** (V) → **at the station** · (2) **We** → **had** (V) → **a meeting** (O) → **at the town hall**.
6. **Subject:** (1) *Let's* (= let us) · (2) *We*.
7. **Verb:** (1) *meet* · (2) *had*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *at the station* · *a meeting* · *at the town hall*.
10. **Word by word:** (1) *meet* after *Let's* → verb · (2) *a meeting* — after *a* → noun (verb + *-ing* can become a C noun).
11. **How the rules work together:** after *a* → noun; *-ing* nouns can be C (*a meeting, a building, a painting*).
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *These buildings are very tall* (EX-021-715) — *building* is another *-ing* noun.
13. **Why this is correct:** each word in its position.
14. **Other versions:** *We met at the town hall.* (verb) · *The meeting starts at six.* (noun)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Let's meeting at the station.* → ✅ *meet* · ❌ *We had meeting.* → ✅ *a meeting*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Meet you at the station?* · *There was a big meeting at the town hall.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** "MEET" · "MEE-ting".
19. **Pattern:** **Let's + verb + at the + place / We had a + verb-ing noun + at the + place**
20. **More examples:** *Let's build a garden. / That building is new.*
21. **Quick check:** In *a meeting*, noun or verb? → **Answer:** noun.

### EX-021-800 — "Please don't block the road. / We live in a block of flats."

1. **Meaning:** (1) Don't stop cars from passing on the road. (2) Our home is in a big building with many flats. 🚧 / 🏢
2. **Situation:** A neighbour parks badly; later you describe where you live.
3. **Main lesson:** *block* as a verb (1) and a C noun (2).
4. **Grammar used:** *Please don't* + verb + *the* + noun · *We live in a* + noun + *of* + plural noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** (1) **(You)** → **don't block** (V) → **the road** (O) · (2) **We** → **live** (V) → **in a block of flats** (P).
6. **Subject:** (1) hidden *you* · (2) *We*.
7. **Verb:** (1) *block* · (2) *live*.
8. **Helping verbs:** (1) *don't*.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *the road* · *in a block of flats*.
10. **Word by word:** (1) *block* after *don't* → verb (stop movement) · (2) *a block of flats* — after *a* → noun (UK; US *apartment building*).
11. **How the rules work together:** after *don't* → verb; after *a* → noun.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Please don't park here. / There's a park near our house* (EX-021-200).
13. **Why this is correct:** each word in its position.
14. **Other versions:** *The road is blocked.* · *Walk two blocks and turn left.* (US: *block* = section of street)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Please don't blocking the road.* → ✅ *block* · ❌ *We live in block of flats.* → ✅ *a block of flats*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Don't block the road!* · *We're in a block of flats.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** "BLOK" (noun and verb the same).
19. **Pattern:** **Please don't + verb + the + noun / We live in a + noun + of + plural noun**
20. **More examples:** *Please don't light the fire. / The light is on.*
21. **Quick check:** In *a block of flats*, is *block* a noun or a verb? → **Answer:** noun.
