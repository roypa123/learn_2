# Example Bank 023 — Singular and Plural Nouns (EX-023-013 to EX-023-100)

**For lesson:** [L-023 — Singular and Plural Nouns](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-023-singular-and-plural-nouns.md) · EX-023-001 to 012 are in the lesson.

## How to use this bank

- Go in order (easy → hard).
- For each example, **cover the notes** and ask: *Singular or plural? Which partners changed (a/an, this/these, is/are, it/they, verb -s)?*
- **Key:** **S** = singular · **P** = plural · **→** = what it agrees with

> 🔁 **The 5 partners:** a/an · this/these · is/are · it/they · verb -s ([L-023 §7.2](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-023-singular-and-plural-nouns.md#72-agreement-make-everything-match)).

---

## Step 1 — One rule, very short (EX-023-013 to 022)

**EX-023-013** — *I have a pen.* / *I have two pens.*
- S *a pen* → P *two pens*. Note: *a* disappears; number + -s.

**EX-023-014** — *The door is open.* / *The doors are open.*
- *is → are*. *open* (adjective) doesn't change.

**EX-023-015** — *My friend is here.* / *My friends are here.*
- *friend → friends*, *is → are*.

**EX-023-016** — *An egg is on the plate.* / *Eggs are on the plate.*
- *An* disappears in the plural. *plate* stays singular (one plate).

**EX-023-017** — *That car is fast.* / *Those cars are fast.*
- *That → Those*, *car → cars*, *is → are*.

**EX-023-018** — *The bird sings.* / *The birds sing.*
- One -s: on the verb (S) or on the noun (P).

**EX-023-019** — *It is a nice idea.* / *They are nice ideas.*
- *It → They*, *a nice idea → nice ideas*.

**EX-023-020** — *A teacher works hard.* / *Teachers work hard.*
- General statement: plural, no article.

**EX-023-021** — *Is the shop open?* / *Are the shops open?*
- Question: *Is → Are*.

**EX-023-022** — *This book is old.* / *These books are old.*
- *This → These*, *book → books*, *is → are*.

---

## Step 2 — Different nouns and verbs (EX-023-023 to 032)

**EX-023-023** — *Two students are absent today.*
- P: *students* → *are*. *absent* = not here.

**EX-023-024** — *My parents work in a hotel.*
- P subject → *work* (no -s). *a hotel* — singular (one hotel).

**EX-023-025** — *The flowers smell nice.*
- P → *smell*. Compare S: *The flower smell**s** nice.*

**EX-023-026** — *We have three bedrooms and a bathroom.*
- P *bedrooms*; S *a bathroom*.

**EX-023-027** — *The computers in the office are new.*
- Head noun *computers* (P) → *are* (not *office*).

**EX-023-028** — *I drink two glasses of water every day.*
- *glasses* here = cups made of glass (P of *glass*). *water* — uncountable (no -s).
- ⚠️ *glasses* (for eyes) is a different meaning!

**EX-023-029** — *Apples are good for you.*
- General: P, no *the*.

**EX-023-030** — *The museum opens at 10.*
- S → *opens*.

**EX-023-031** — *Ten people live in this building.*
- *people* = plural of *person* ([L-025](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-025-irregular-plurals.md)) → *live*.

**EX-023-032** — *The students write emails to their teacher.*
- P subject → *write*; P object *emails* (each student writes one or more). *teacher* S.

---

## Step 3 — With pronouns (it / they) (EX-023-033 to 042)

**EX-023-033** — *I bought a new lamp. It is very bright.*
- *a lamp* → **it**.

**EX-023-034** — *I bought new lamps. They are very bright.*
- *lamps* → **they**.

**EX-023-035** — *Where are my keys? I can't find them.*
- *keys* (P) → **them** (object form of *they*) ([L-013](../../00-foundations/05-basic-word-order/lesson-013-the-object.md)).

**EX-023-036** — *Where is my key? I can't find it.*
- *key* (S) → **it**.

**EX-023-037** — *My grandparents are kind. They live with us.*
- P → **They**.

**EX-023-038** — *The children are tired. Put them to bed.*
- *children* → **them**.

**EX-023-039** — *This sandwich is delicious. Who made it?*
- S → **it**.

**EX-023-040** — *Those shoes are beautiful. How much are they?*
- P → **they**, **are**.

**EX-023-041** — *My sister has two cats. She loves them.*
- *She* = sister (S, verb *has/loves* with -s); *them* = cats (P).

**EX-023-042** — *I like your earrings. Are they new?*
- *earrings* (jewellery for ears — usually a pair) → **they**.

---

## Step 4 — Questions and negatives (EX-023-043 to 052)

**EX-023-043** — *Are the tomatoes fresh?* — *Yes, they are.*
- P question + short answer with **they**. (*tomatoes* — spelling with -es: [L-024](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-024-plural-spelling-rules.md).)

**EX-023-044** — *The trains aren't running today.*
- P → *aren't*. *running* = working, going (for trains).

**EX-023-045** — *Do your brothers play football?*
- P subject → **do** (not *does*).

**EX-023-046** — *Does your brother play football?*
- S subject → **does**.

**EX-023-047** — *How many eggs do we need?* — *Six.*
- *How many* + P.

**EX-023-048** — *My neighbours don't have a car.*
- P subject → **don't**. *a car* S (they don't have one).

**EX-023-049** — *This isn't my bag. My bag is red.*
- S → *isn't*, *is*.

**EX-023-050** — *These aren't my shoes. Mine are black.*
- P → *aren't*, *are*. (*Mine* = my shoes — [L-066](../../01-beginner-grammar/pronouns/lesson-066-possessive-pronouns.md).)

**EX-023-051** — *Are there any questions?*
- *questions* P → *Are there* ([L-041](../../01-beginner-grammar/basic-sentences/lesson-041-there-is-there-are.md)). *any* in questions ([L-069](../../01-beginner-grammar/articles-and-determiners/lesson-069-some-and-any.md)).

**EX-023-052** — *Is there a bank near here?* — *No, there isn't.*
- *a bank* S → *Is there*.

## Step 5 — Different times (EX-023-053 to 060)

> 🔁 Past: *was* (S) / *were* (P) ([L-061](../../01-beginner-grammar/verbs/lesson-061-was-were.md)). Future: *will* (same for S and P).

**EX-023-053** — *The test was easy.* / *The tests were easy.*
- Past: *was → were*.

**EX-023-054** — *The guests will arrive at seven.*
- *will* + verb — the same for singular and plural.

**EX-023-055** — *My shoes were wet after the rain.*
- P → *were*.

**EX-023-056** — *The film was long, but the songs were great.*
- S *film* → *was*; P *songs* → *were*.

**EX-023-057** — *Yesterday, three customers complained.*
- Past -ed is the same for S and P: *complained*.

**EX-023-058** — *The bus was late again.* / *The buses were late again.*
- *bus → buses* (-es spelling, [L-024](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-024-plural-spelling-rules.md)).

**EX-023-059** — *Next week, the shops will close early.*
- P subject; *will close* — no change for plural.

**EX-023-060** — *There was one problem. Now there are two problems.*
- *was* + S; *are* + P.

---

## Step 6 — Two topics: plurals + adjectives (EX-023-061 to 070)

> 🔁 Adjectives **never** take -s ([L-008](../../00-foundations/03-words-and-meaning/lesson-008-things-actions-describing-words.md)).

**EX-023-061** — *She has long black hair.*
- *hair* (on the head) is usually **uncountable** → no -s, no *a*. ⚠️ ❌ *long black hairs*. (*There are two hairs in my soup!* — single hairs can be counted.)

**EX-023-062** — *We saw some beautiful old houses.*
- *houses* P; *beautiful, old* unchanged.

**EX-023-063** — *These red apples are sweet.*
- *These* + P; *red, sweet* unchanged.

**EX-023-064** — *Small children need a lot of sleep.*
- General P; *sleep* uncountable.

**EX-023-065** — *The new students are very friendly.*
- *new, friendly* unchanged.

**EX-023-066** — *I need two large boxes for my books.*
- *boxes* (-es); *large* unchanged.

**EX-023-067** — *Fast cars are expensive.*
- General statement.

**EX-023-068** — *My little brothers are noisy.*
- *little* (young/small); *noisy* = making a lot of noise.

**EX-023-069** — *The hotel has clean rooms and a big pool.*
- P *rooms*; S *a pool*. Each adjective unchanged.

**EX-023-070** — *Good friends are important.*
- General; *good, important* unchanged.

## Step 7 — Three topics: plurals + adjectives + prepositions/places (EX-023-071 to 078)

**EX-023-071** — *There are two empty chairs in the front row.*
- *chairs* P → *There are*; *row* S (one row). *empty* = with nothing/no one in it.

**EX-023-072** — *The books on the top shelf are mine.*
- Head *books* → *are*. *shelf* S.

**EX-023-073** — *The windows in my bedroom face the garden.*
- Head *windows* → *face* (no -s). *face* (verb) = look towards.

**EX-023-074** — *My friends from university live in different cities.*
- Head *friends* → *live*; *cities* (y → ies, [L-024](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-024-plural-spelling-rules.md)).

**EX-023-075** — *A box of chocolates is on the table.*
- Head *box* (S) → *is*, even though *chocolates* is P.

**EX-023-076** — *The plates in the cupboard are clean.*
- Head *plates* → *are*.

**EX-023-077** — *The keys to the front door are on the hook.*
- Head *keys* → *are*. *hook* = a curved piece of metal for hanging things.

**EX-023-078** — *The price of the tickets is very high.*
- Head *price* (S) → *is*. ❌ *are* (don't follow *tickets*).

---

## Step 8 — Longer sentences (EX-023-079 to 086)

**EX-023-079** — *Every morning, the children in our street walk to school together.*
- *Every morning* (S after *every*); head *children* → *walk*.

**EX-023-080** — *One of the lights in the kitchen doesn't work.*
- *one of + P*; head *One* → *doesn't*.

**EX-023-081** — *Each room in the hotel has a balcony with a view of the sea.*
- *Each room* (S) → *has*. *balcony* = a small platform outside a window.

**EX-023-082** — *My glasses are on the table next to the newspapers.*
- *glasses* (eyes — always P) → *are*; *newspapers* P.

**EX-023-083** — *The information on these websites isn't always correct.*
- Head *information* (uncountable, singular) → *isn't*; *these websites* P.

**EX-023-084** — *All the students in my class have a phone, but only two of them have laptops.*
- *All the students* → *have*; *two of them* → *have*. *a phone* each.

**EX-023-085** — *The news about the new trains is good: they are faster and cleaner.*
- *news* → *is*; *they* = the trains → *are*.

**EX-023-086** — *The scissors in the drawer are not sharp enough to cut this paper.*
- *scissors* (always P) → *are*; *paper* uncountable. *drawer* = a box that slides out of furniture.

## Step 9 — Conversations (EX-023-087 to 090)

**EX-023-087**
> **A:** How many **bags** do you have?
> **B:** Just one **suitcase** and a small **backpack**.
- *How many* + P; answer with singulars (*one suitcase, a backpack*).

**EX-023-088**
> **A:** Are these your **gloves**?
> **B:** Yes, they are! Thank you.
- *gloves* (a pair) → *these, are, they*.

**EX-023-089**
> **A:** I need some **advice**.
> **B:** Sure. What's the problem?
- *advice* — uncountable → *some advice* (❌ *an advice*, ❌ *advices*).

**EX-023-090**
> **A:** Do you have any **brothers** or **sisters**?
> **B:** I have one **sister**. No **brothers**.
- Questions about family usually use the plural (*any brothers or sisters*). *No* + plural.

---

## Step 10 — In a short story (EX-023-091 to 093)

> *(1) Mia has a small shop. (2) She sells hats, scarves and gloves. (3) In winter, the shelves are always empty because everyone wants warm clothes.*

**EX-023-091** — (1) *a small shop* S; *Mia has* — S subject → *has*.
**EX-023-092** — (2) P objects: *hats, scarves* (f → ves, [L-024](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-024-plural-spelling-rules.md)), *gloves*. *She sells* — S subject → -s on verb.
**EX-023-093** — (3) *the shelves* P → *are*; *everyone* (singular! — like *every*) → *wants*; *clothes* (always P).

---

## Step 11 — Real writing: a shopping list and note (EX-023-094 to 096)

> *Hi Sam! Please buy: 6 eggs, 2 onions, a loaf of bread, some milk and 3 bananas. The bags are in the cupboard. Thanks! — Lou*

**EX-023-094** — *6 eggs, 2 onions, 3 bananas* — numbers + P (written as numerals in a list).
**EX-023-095** — *a loaf of bread, some milk* — *bread* and *milk* are uncountable → *a loaf of* / *some*. A *loaf* = one whole bread 🍞.
**EX-023-096** — *The bags are in the cupboard.* — P → *are*.

---

## Step 12 — Telling similar things apart (EX-023-097 to 100)

**EX-023-097** — *My glass is empty.* / *My glasses are dirty.*
- *glass* (S) = a cup for drinking 🥛. *glasses* (P) — could be more cups **or** the things for your eyes 👓. Context tells you.

**EX-023-098** — *Every student has a locker.* / *All students have lockers.*
- *every + S + has* vs *all + P + have*. Same meaning.

**EX-023-099** — *The news is on TV at nine.* / *The newspapers are on the table.*
- *news* (S, uncountable) vs *newspapers* (P, countable).

**EX-023-100** — *I like dogs.* / *I like the dogs.*
- 1st = dogs in general. 2nd = specific dogs (e.g., the dogs at my friend's house) ([L-023 §7.4](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-023-singular-and-plural-nouns.md#74-plurals-for-things-in-general)).
