**Short reminders:** **Noun** = naming word (👩 person · 🐾 animal · 📍 place · 📦 thing · 💭 idea). **Positions:** **S** subject · **O** object · **B** after *be* · **P** after a preposition. **Head noun** decides the verb ([L-021 §7.4](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-021-what-is-a-noun.md#74-noun-phrases)).

**New words in this part:** contract · deadline · staff · mechanic · teamwork · client · builder · training · sales · pharmacy · public holiday · miner · skill · dress code · basement · photocopier · double shift.

## Step A — One noun, short sentences

### EX-021-351 — "The contract is ready."

1. **Meaning:** The official work agreement (on paper) is finished and can be signed. 📄✍️
2. **Situation:** A manager tells a new employee to come and sign.
3. **Main lesson:** An official **document** noun as the subject.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + *is* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The contract** (S) → **is** → **ready**.
6. **Subject:** *The contract*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *ready* — prepared.
10. **Word by word:** *contract* 📦/💭 — a written legal agreement · *ready*.
11. **How the rules work together:** singular noun → *is*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Breakfast is ready* (EX-021-022).
13. **Why this is correct:** S + *be* + adjective.
14. **Other versions:** *The contracts are ready.* · *Is the contract ready?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Contract is ready.* → ✅ *The contract* · ❌ *The contract ready.* → ✅ *is ready*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Your contract's ready to sign.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** noun ***KON**-trakt* (stress first).
19. **Pattern:** **The + document + is + ready.**
20. **More examples:** *The report is ready. · The invoice is ready.*
21. **Quick check:** Stress in the noun *contract*? → **Answer:** first part: *CON-tract*.

### EX-021-352 — "Doctors save lives."

1. **Meaning:** Doctors stop people from dying. 🩺❤️
2. **Situation:** A teacher talks about important jobs.
3. **Main lesson:** Job noun (S) + irregular-spelling plural (O): *life → lives*.
4. **Grammar used:** plural + verb + plural.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Doctors** (S) → **save** (V) → **lives** (O).
6. **Subject:** *Doctors*.
7. **Verb:** *save*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *lives* — plural of *life* (f → ves).
10. **Word by word:** *Doctors* · *save* — keep safe · *lives* 💭 — "lyvz".
11. **How the rules work together:** general plurals; f → ves.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *life → lives* ([L-024](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-024-plural-spelling-rules.md#74-rule-4--many-nouns-in--f---fe-change-to--ves)).
13. **Why this is correct:** S-V-O with correct plural.
14. **Other versions:** *A doctor saves lives.* · *Firefighters save lives too.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Doctors save lifes.* → ✅ *lives* · ❌ *Doctors saves…* → ✅ *save*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Doctors save lives every day.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** noun *lives* "LYVZ" (verb *lives* "LIVZ").
19. **Pattern:** **Plural job noun + save + lives.**
20. **More examples:** *Lifeguards save swimmers. · Nurses help patients.*
21. **Quick check:** Plural of *life*? → **Answer:** *lives*.

3. **Main lesson:** *uniform* — singular C noun (one set of clothes).

6. **Subject:** *My uniform*.

9. **Object / complement / extras:** *blue*.
10. **Word by word:** *uniform* — one uniform = one set of clothes (singular) · *blue*.
11. **How the rules work together:** singular → *is*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *a uniform* (EX-021-255).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct agreement.
14. **Other versions:** *Our uniforms are blue.* · *What colour is your uniform?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *My uniform are blue.* → ✅ *is* · ❌ *My uniform is blues.* → ✅ *blue*

19. **Pattern:** **My + noun + is + colour.**

### EX-021-354 — "Business is good this year."

1. **Meaning:** This year, the company is selling a lot and making money. 📈
2. **Situation:** A shop owner talks to a friend.
3. **Main lesson:** *business* as an **uncountable** noun (= trade, activity).
4. **Grammar used:** U noun + *is* + adj + time.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Business** (S) → **is** → **good** → **this year**.
6. **Subject:** *Business*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *good* · *this year*.
10. **Word by word:** *Business* — U here (trade/sales in general); C *a business* = a company · *this year*.
11. **How the rules work together:** U meaning → no article; C meaning → *a business*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** nouns that are U and C with different meanings ([L-026 §7.4](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-026-countable-and-uncountable-nouns.md#74-nouns-that-can-be-both--with-different-meanings)).
13. **Why this is correct:** U meaning, singular verb.
14. **Other versions:** *She has a small business.* (C = a company) · *How's business?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The business is good this year.* (if you mean trade in general) · ❌ *Businesses is…* → ✅ *are*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Business is booming!* (*booming* = growing fast)
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "BIZ-nis" (2 syllables).
19. **Pattern:** **U noun + is + good/bad + this year.**
20. **More examples:** *Traffic is bad today. · Weather is important for farmers.*
21. **Quick check:** *a business* = ? → **Answer:** a company.

### EX-021-355 — "The shop needs more staff."

1. **Meaning:** The shop must have more workers. 👥
2. **Situation:** A busy shop at Christmas.
3. **Main lesson:** *staff* — a group noun for workers (no -s for people).
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + verb-s + *more* + group noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The shop** (S) → **needs** (V) → **more staff** (O).
6. **Subject:** *The shop*.
7. **Verb:** *needs*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *more staff*.
10. **Word by word:** *staff* 👩 — all the workers of a place (group noun, no -s for "more people": ❌ *staffs*) · *more*.
11. **How the rules work together:** *staff* behaves like a mass/group; count with *members of staff*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *staff room* (EX-021-290).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct group noun.
14. **Other versions:** *We need three more members of staff.* · *The staff are friendly.* (UK, thinking of the people)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *…needs more staffs.* → ✅ *more staff* · ❌ *…needs three staff.* — common in UK speech, but *three members of staff / three staff members* is clearer
16. **Natural spoken English:** *We're short-staffed.* (= we don't have enough staff)
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "STAHF" (UK) / "STAF" (US).
19. **Pattern:** **The + place + needs more + staff/workers.**
20. **More examples:** *The hospital needs more nurses. · The school needs more teachers.*
21. **Quick check:** *staffs* or *staff* for workers? → **Answer:** *staff*.

### EX-021-356 — "Lunch break is at one."

1. **Meaning:** The break for eating lunch at work is at 1:00. 🥪
2. **Situation:** Explaining the workday to a new colleague.
3. **Main lesson:** Compound time noun without an article.
4. **Grammar used:** noun + noun + *is at* + time.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Lunch break** (S) → **is** → **at one**.
6. **Subject:** *Lunch break* — head **break**.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *at one*.
10. **Word by word:** *lunch break* — a pause for lunch · *at one* — 1:00.
11. **How the rules work together:** routine time nouns can drop the article (like *bedtime*).
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Bedtime is at eight* (EX-021-145).
13. **Why this is correct:** natural routine form.
14. **Other versions:** *Our lunch break is at one.* · *The lunch break is short.* (with *the*/possessive also fine)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Lunch break is in one.* → ✅ *at one* · ❌ *Lunch's break…* → ✅ *Lunch break*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *We break for lunch at one.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "LUNCH BRAYK".
19. **Pattern:** **Lunch break / Coffee break + is at + time.**
20. **More examples:** *Coffee break is at eleven. · Bedtime is at nine.*
21. **Quick check:** Head noun of *lunch break*? → **Answer:** *break*.

### EX-021-357 — "Mechanics fix cars."

1. **Meaning:** Repairing cars is a mechanic's job. 🔧🚗
2. **Situation:** A child asks what a mechanic does.
3. **Main lesson:** Job noun (S) ending in *-ic* + thing noun (O).
4. **Grammar used:** plural + verb + plural.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Mechanics** (S) → **fix** (V) → **cars** (O).
6. **Subject:** *Mechanics* — people who repair machines.
7. **Verb:** *fix*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *cars*.
10. **Word by word:** *mechanics* 👩 — ⚠️ here a **plural person noun**; *mechanics* is also a science subject (*Mechanics **is** a part of physics*) · *fix* · *cars*.
11. **How the rules work together:** context decides the meaning.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *economics is* (EX-021-275) — subjects ending in -s.
13. **Why this is correct:** plural people → *fix*.
14. **Other versions:** *A mechanic fixes cars.* · *My uncle is a mechanic.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Mechanics fixes cars.* → ✅ *fix* · ❌ *A mechanics fixes…* → ✅ *A mechanic fixes…*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *The mechanic fixed my car.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "muh-KAN-iks".
19. **Pattern:** **Plural job noun + verb + plural noun.**
20. **More examples:** *Plumbers fix pipes. · Electricians fix lights.*
21. **Quick check:** Singular of the person noun? → **Answer:** *a mechanic*.

### EX-021-358 — "The deadline is Friday."

1. **Meaning:** The last day to finish the work is Friday. ⏳
2. **Situation:** A manager reminds the team.
3. **Main lesson:** Abstract noun (S) + day noun after *be* (no *on* here).
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + *is* + day.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The deadline** (S) → **is** → **Friday** (complement).
6. **Subject:** *The deadline*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *Friday* — after *be*, *on* is optional: *The deadline is (on) Friday.*
10. **Word by word:** *deadline* 💭 — the latest time to finish something · *Friday*.
11. **How the rules work together:** *be* + day (with or without *on*).
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The party is on Saturday* (EX-021-031).
13. **Why this is correct:** both forms are natural.
14. **Other versions:** *The deadline is on Friday at 5 pm.* · *When is the deadline?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The deadline is in Friday.* → ✅ *(on) Friday* · ❌ *The dead line…* → ✅ *deadline*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *It's due Friday.* (*due* = expected)
17. **Writing & punctuation:** one word: *deadline*.
18. **Pronunciation:** "DED-lyn".
19. **Pattern:** **The deadline is (on) + day.**
20. **More examples:** *The meeting is Monday. · My exam is on Tuesday.*
21. **Quick check:** One word or two: *dead line*? → **Answer:** one: *deadline*.

## Step B — Work nouns with pronouns

### EX-021-361 — "Our new receptionist is from Spain. She speaks four languages."

1. **Meaning:** The new person at our front desk comes from Spain and can speak four languages.
2. **Situation:** Introducing a new colleague.
3. **Main lesson:** Job noun → *She*; number + plural noun.
4. **Grammar used:** *our* + adj + noun + *is from* + country · *She speaks* + number + plural.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Our new receptionist** (S) → **is** → **from Spain** · **She** (S) → **speaks** → **four languages** (O).
6. **Subject:** *Our new receptionist* · *She*.
7. **Verb:** *is* · *speaks*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *from Spain* · *four languages*.
10. **Word by word:** *receptionist* · *Spain* (proper) · *languages* (sound /ɪz/).
11. **How the rules work together:** noun → pronoun; number + plural.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Our teacher is from Ireland. She teaches English.* (EX-021-211).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct reference and agreement.
14. **Other versions:** *Our new receptionists are from Spain. They speak four languages.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *…four language.* → ✅ *languages* · ❌ *…from the Spain.* → ✅ *from Spain*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *The new receptionist's Spanish — she speaks four languages!*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** capital **S**.
18. **Pronunciation:** "LANG-gwij-iz".
19. **Pattern:** **Our new + job + is from + country. She/He speaks + number + languages.**
20. **More examples:** *Our new driver is from Kenya. He speaks three languages.*
21. **Quick check:** Plural of *language*? Sound? → **Answer:** *languages*, /ɪz/.

### EX-021-362 — "The clients are unhappy. We need to call them."

1. **Meaning:** The customers of our business are not happy; we must phone them.
2. **Situation:** A problem at a company.
3. **Main lesson:** Plural people noun → *them*.
4. **Grammar used:** plural + *are* + adj · *We need to* + verb + *them*.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The clients** (S) → **are** → **unhappy** · **We** → **need to call** → **them** (O).
6. **Subject:** *The clients* · *We*.
7. **Verb:** *are* · *need to call*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none (*need to* + verb).
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *unhappy* · *them*.
10. **Word by word:** *clients* 👩 — people who pay a business for a service · *unhappy* — *un-* = not · *them*.
11. **How the rules work together:** plural → *them*; *need to* + base verb.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *un-* prefix ([L-007](../../00-foundations/03-words-and-meaning/lesson-007-what-is-a-word.md)).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct pronoun and verb pattern.
14. **Other versions:** *The client is unhappy. We need to call her.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *We need to call they.* → ✅ *them* · ❌ *We need call them.* → ✅ *need to call*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *The clients aren't happy — let's give them a call.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** "KLY-ents".
19. **Pattern:** **The + plural noun + are + adj. We need to + verb + them.**
20. **More examples:** *The customers are waiting. We need to help them.*
21. **Quick check:** *client* vs *customer* — similar? → **Answer:** Yes; *client* is used more for services (lawyers, banks), *customer* for shops.

### EX-021-363 — "I sent an email to my boss. She answered it in five minutes."

1. **Meaning:** I emailed my boss, and she replied after only five minutes. 📧⚡
2. **Situation:** Telling a colleague how fast the boss is.
3. **Main lesson:** *an email* → *it*; time noun.
4. **Grammar used:** *I sent an* + noun + *to my* + noun · *She answered it in* + number + plural.
5. **Sentence structure:** **I** → **sent** → **an email** (O) → **to my boss** · **She** → **answered** → **it** (O) → **in five minutes**.
6. **Subject:** *I* · *She* (= my boss).
7. **Verb:** *sent* (past of *send*) · *answered*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *an email* · *to my boss* · *it* · *in five minutes* (= after 5 minutes).
10. **Word by word:** *an email* · *to* · *boss* · *it* = the email · *in five minutes* — how long it took.
11. **How the rules work together:** first *an email*, then *it*; *in* + time = how long until.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *I got an email from my boss* (EX-021-097).
13. **Why this is correct:** clear reference and correct past forms.
14. **Other versions:** *I sent my boss an email.* (person + thing)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *I sent to my boss an email.* → ✅ *I sent an email to my boss* · ❌ *She answered in five minutes it.* → ✅ *answered it in five minutes*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *She got back to me in five minutes!* (*get back to* = reply)
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** "SENT"; "AN-serd".
19. **Pattern:** **I sent a/an + noun + to + person. He/She answered it in + time.**
20. **More examples:** *I sent a message to my friend. He replied in an hour.*
21. **Quick check:** Past of *send*? → **Answer:** *sent*.

### EX-021-364 — "The builders are working on our roof. They start at seven."

1. **Meaning:** Workers are repairing the top of our house; they begin each day at 7:00. 🏗️
2. **Situation:** Explaining early morning noise to a guest.
3. **Main lesson:** Plural job noun → *They*.
4. **Grammar used:** plural + *are* + verb-ing + *on our* + noun · *They start at* + time.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The builders** (S) → **are working** → **on our roof** · **They** (S) → **start** → **at seven**.
6. **Subject:** *The builders* · *They*.
7. **Verb:** *are working* · *start*.
8. **Helping verbs:** *are*.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *on our roof* · *at seven*.
10. **Word by word:** *builders* — *build* + *-er* · *working on* — repairing/doing work on · *roof* (plural *roofs*).
11. **How the rules work together:** action now (*are working*) vs routine (*start*).
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *roofs* exception ([L-024](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-024-plural-spelling-rules.md)).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct tenses and reference.
14. **Other versions:** *The builder is working on the roof. He starts at seven.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The builders is working…* → ✅ *are* · ❌ *They starts…* → ✅ *start*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *The builders are doing our roof — they start at seven!*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** "BIL-derz" (silent **u**).
19. **Pattern:** **The + plural job + are working on + our + noun. They start at + time.**
20. **More examples:** *The painters are working on the kitchen. They finish at five.*
21. **Quick check:** Silent letter in *builder*? → **Answer:** *u*.

### EX-021-365 — "My brother is a chef. He cooks Italian food."

1. **Meaning:** My brother works as a professional cook; he makes food from Italy. 🍝
2. **Situation:** Talking about family jobs.
3. **Main lesson:** Job noun after *be* + U noun with a nationality adjective.
4. **Grammar used:** noun + *is a* + noun · *He cooks* + proper adj + U noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **My brother** → **is** → **a chef** · **He** → **cooks** → **Italian food** (O).
6. **Subject:** *My brother* · *He*.
7. **Verb:** *is* · *cooks*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *a chef* · *Italian food*.
10. **Word by word:** *chef* — professional cook (plural *chefs*) · *Italian* — capital · *food* (U).
11. **How the rules work together:** nationality adjectives take capitals; *food* U → no *a*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *I love Italian food* ([L-022](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-022-common-and-proper-nouns.md) bank).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct article, capital and U noun.
14. **Other versions:** *My sister is a chef. She cooks Thai food.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *He cooks an Italian food.* → ✅ *Italian food* · ❌ *…italian food.* → ✅ *Italian*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *My brother's a chef — Italian food.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** capital **I**.
18. **Pronunciation:** "SHEF"; "i-TAL-yun".
19. **Pattern:** **Person + is a chef. He/She cooks + Nationality + food.**
20. **More examples:** *My aunt is a baker. She makes French bread.*
21. **Quick check:** Plural of *chef*? → **Answer:** *chefs*.

### EX-021-366 — "Does your company pay for training?"

2. **Situation:** In an office.
3. **Main lesson:** *sign* as a verb (1) and a noun (2).
4. **Grammar used:** instruction + verb + *the* + noun · *There's a* + noun + *on the* + noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** (1) **Please sign** (V) → **the contract** (O) · (2) **There's** → **a sign** → **on the door**.
6. **Subject:** hidden *you* · real subject *a sign*.
7. **Verb:** (1) *sign* · (2) *'s* (is).
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** (1) *the contract* · (2) place.
10. **Word by word:** (1) *sign* after *Please* → verb · (2) *a sign* after *a* → noun (a notice with words/pictures) · silent **g** in both!
11. **How the rules work together:** position decides; related noun *signature* = your written name.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The contract is ready* (EX-021-351).
13. **Why this is correct:** each word in its position.
14. **Other versions:** *She signed the form. · Look at the sign!*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Please sign on the contract.* (common, but *sign the contract* is standard) · ❌ *There's sign on the door.* → ✅ *a sign*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Just sign here, please.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** "SYN" (silent **g**); *signature* "SIG-na-cher" (the **g** is pronounced!).
19. **Pattern:** **Please + verb + the + noun / There's a + noun + on the + noun**
20. **More examples:** *Please mark the answers. / There's a mark on my shirt.*
21. **Quick check:** Noun for your written name? → **Answer:** *signature*.

### EX-021-400 — "Our team will present the results. / This is a present for you."

1. **Meaning:** (1) Our team will show and explain the results (to an audience). (2) This is a gift for you. 🎁
2. **Situation:** At work (1) / a birthday (2).
3. **Main lesson:** *present* as a verb (stress on the **second** part) and a noun (stress on the **first** part).
4. **Grammar used:** *will* + verb + *the* + plural · *This is a* + noun + *for you*.
5. **Sentence structure:** (1) **Our team** (S) → **will present** (V) → **the results** (O) · (2) **This** (S) → **is** → **a present** → **for you**.
6. **Subject:** *Our team* · *This*.
7. **Verb:** (1) *will present* · (2) *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** (1) *will*.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** (1) *the results* · (2) *a present for you*.
10. **Word by word:** (1) *present* (verb) = show formally · (2) *a present* (noun) = a gift.
11. **How the rules work together:** **stress** changes with the job: noun ***PRES**ent* / verb *pre**SENT***.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *record / present* stress ([L-021 §16](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-021-what-is-a-noun.md#16-pronunciation-and-listening-notes-)).
13. **Why this is correct:** each word in its position.
14. **Other versions:** *She presented her project. · Thank you for the present!* · Also *present* (adjective) = here now: *All students are present.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Our team will present to the results.* → ✅ *present the results* · ❌ saying the noun with stress at the end
16. **Natural spoken English:** *We'll be presenting the results tomorrow.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** noun ***PREZ**-ent* · verb *pri-**ZENT***.
19. **Pattern:** **will + present + the + noun / This is a + present + for you**
20. **More examples:** *We record every meeting. / I have a record of the meeting.* (noun ***REK**-ord*, verb *ri-**KORD***)
21. **Quick check:** Where's the stress in the noun *present*? → **Answer:** first part: *PRES-ent*.
