**Short reminders:** **Noun** = naming word (👩 person · 🐾 animal · 📍 place · 📦 thing · 💭 idea). **Positions:** **S** subject · **O** object · **B** after *be* · **P** after a preposition. **Head noun** decides the verb ([L-021 §7.4](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-021-what-is-a-noun.md#74-noun-phrases)).

> 🔁 **From Part 13:** *luggage, traffic, travel* are **U**; **by + transport** has no article (*by bus*); noun + number has no article (*row 12, gate 5*). New U nouns here: *scenery, accommodation, sunscreen, air conditioning*.

**New words in this part:** guide · tourist · tent · waterproof · motorway · scenery · lift · floor · guidebook · staff · tyre · backpacker · souvenir · pharmacy · visa · storm · thief · tourist attraction · heated · roof · cash machine · corridor · boarding gate · key card · sausage · coast · hill.

## Step A — One noun, short sentences

### EX-021-651 — "The guide is funny."

1. **Meaning:** The person who shows us around tells jokes and makes us laugh. 🧑‍🏫😄
2. **Situation:** On a walking tour of a city.
3. **Main lesson:** A person noun as the subject.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + *is* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The guide** (S) → **is** → **funny**.
6. **Subject:** *The guide*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *funny* — makes people laugh.
10. **Word by word:** *the* — the one with us · *guide* 👩 — a person who shows tourists places · *funny*.
11. **How the rules work together:** one person → *is*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The waiter is busy* (EX-021-503) — another job noun as subject.
13. **Why this is correct:** S + *be* + adjective.
14. **Other versions:** *The guides are funny.* · *Is the guide funny?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The guide is fun.* (possible, but *funny* = makes you laugh) → ✅ *funny* · ❌ *Guide is funny.* → ✅ *The guide*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Our guide's hilarious!*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** in *guide*, the *u* is silent.
18. **Pronunciation:** "GYD" (rhymes with *side*).
19. **Pattern:** **The + person noun + is + adjective.**
20. **More examples:** *The driver is friendly. · The pilot is calm.*
21. **Quick check:** *funny* or *fun* — which means "makes you laugh"? → **Answer:** *funny*.

### EX-021-652 — "Tourists are everywhere."

1. **Meaning:** There are visitors in every part of the place. 📸🧍🧍🧍
2. **Situation:** A famous city in August.
3. **Main lesson:** plural noun in general + place word.
4. **Grammar used:** plural noun + *are* + adverb of place.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Tourists** (S) → **are** → **everywhere**.
6. **Subject:** *Tourists*.
7. **Verb:** *are*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *everywhere* — in all places.
10. **Word by word:** *tourists* 👩 — people visiting for fun (*tourist* + *s*) · *everywhere* — one word.
11. **How the rules work together:** general plural → no *the*; plural → *are*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Taxis are expensive in London* (EX-021-604).
13. **Why this is correct:** plural + *are*.
14. **Other versions:** *There are tourists everywhere.* · *The city is full of tourists.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Tourists is everywhere.* → ✅ *are* · ❌ *…every where.* → ✅ *everywhere*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *There are tourists everywhere you look!*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** *everywhere* — one word.
18. **Pronunciation:** "TOOR-ists" · "EV-ree-wair".
19. **Pattern:** **Plural noun + are + everywhere.**
20. **More examples:** *Bikes are everywhere in Amsterdam.*
21. **Quick check:** Singular of *tourists*? → **Answer:** *tourist*.

### EX-021-653 — "Our tent is waterproof."

1. **Meaning:** Rain cannot come into our tent. ⛺🌧️
2. **Situation:** Camping when it starts to rain.
3. **Main lesson:** *our* + noun + adjective.
4. **Grammar used:** *Our* + noun + *is* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Our tent** (S) → **is** → **waterproof**.
6. **Subject:** *Our tent*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *waterproof*.
10. **Word by word:** *Our* — belongs to us · *tent* 📦 — a small "house" of cloth for camping · *waterproof* — adjective made from *water* + *proof* (= protected).
11. **How the rules work together:** one tent → *is*; *our* replaces *the*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *My passport is in my bag* (EX-021-602) — possessive + noun.
13. **Why this is correct:** S + *be* + adjective.
14. **Other versions:** *Our tents are waterproof.* · *Is your tent waterproof?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Our the tent…* → ✅ *Our tent* · ❌ *Our tent is water proof.* → ✅ *waterproof*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Don't worry, the tent's waterproof.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** *waterproof* — one word.
18. **Pronunciation:** "WAW-ter-proof".
19. **Pattern:** **Our + noun + is + adjective.**
20. **More examples:** *My jacket is waterproof. · Our car is new.*
21. **Quick check:** Which word is the noun? → **Answer:** *tent*.

### EX-021-654 — "The museum is free."

1. **Meaning:** You don't pay to go into the museum. 🏛️🆓
2. **Situation:** Planning a cheap day in a city.
3. **Main lesson:** *free* = no money (different from *free* = not taken).
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + *is* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The museum** (S) → **is** → **free**.
6. **Subject:** *The museum*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *free* — costs nothing.
10. **Word by word:** *museum* 📍 — building with old or interesting things to see · *free* — no cost.
11. **How the rules work together:** singular → *is*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Is this table free?* (EX-021-592) — there, *free* = not being used.
13. **Why this is correct:** S + *be* + adjective.
14. **Other versions:** *Entry to the museum is free.* · *Museums are free in London.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The museum is for free.* (common, but simple is best) → ✅ *is free* · ❌ *The museum is gratis.* → ✅ *free*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *It's free to get into the museum.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** plural *museums*.
18. **Pronunciation:** "myoo-ZEE-um".
19. **Pattern:** **The + place + is + free.**
20. **More examples:** *The park is free. · The concert is free.*
21. **Quick check:** Meaning of *free* here? → **Answer:** costs no money.

### EX-021-655 — "Bicycles are popular in Amsterdam."

1. **Meaning:** Many people in Amsterdam like and use bicycles. 🚲🇳🇱
2. **Situation:** A travel video about the Netherlands.
3. **Main lesson:** plural noun in general + city.
4. **Grammar used:** plural noun + *are* + adjective + *in* + city.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Bicycles** (S) → **are** → **popular** → **in Amsterdam** (P).
6. **Subject:** *Bicycles*.
7. **Verb:** *are*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *popular* · *in Amsterdam*.
10. **Word by word:** *bicycles* 📦 — two-wheel transport (short: *bikes*) · *popular* — liked by many · *Amsterdam* — proper noun.
11. **How the rules work together:** general plural → no article; plural → *are*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *I bought a second-hand bike* (EX-021-481) — *bike* = *bicycle*.
13. **Why this is correct:** agreement + no article for general meaning.
14. **Other versions:** *Cycling is popular in Amsterdam.* (U *-ing* noun → *is*)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The bicycles are popular in Amsterdam.* (general → no *the*) · ❌ *…in the Amsterdam.* → ✅ *in Amsterdam*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Everyone cycles in Amsterdam.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** capital *A*.
18. **Pronunciation:** "BY-sik-ulz".
19. **Pattern:** **Plural noun + are + popular + in + place.**
20. **More examples:** *Scooters are popular in Rome.*
21. **Quick check:** Short word for *bicycle*? → **Answer:** *bike*.

### EX-021-657 — "The scenery is beautiful."

1. **Meaning:** The natural things you can see (mountains, lakes, fields) are lovely. 🏞️
2. **Situation:** Driving through the countryside.
3. **Main lesson:** *scenery* is **U** → *is*, no *-s*.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + U noun + *is* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The scenery** (S) → **is** → **beautiful**.
6. **Subject:** *The scenery*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *beautiful*.
10. **Word by word:** *scenery* 💭 — the natural views of a place (U) · *beautiful*.
11. **How the rules work together:** U → singular verb, never *sceneries*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *My luggage is heavy* (EX-021-606) — another U noun.
13. **Why this is correct:** U + *is*.
14. **Other versions:** *The views are beautiful.* (C *view* → *views*)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The sceneries are beautiful.* → ✅ *The scenery is* · ❌ *a beautiful scenery* → ✅ *beautiful scenery*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *The scenery's stunning.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "SEE-nuh-ree" (the *c* is silent).
19. **Pattern:** **The + U noun + is + adjective.**
20. **More examples:** *The weather is perfect. · The accommodation is comfortable.*
21. **Quick check:** C noun with a similar meaning to *scenery*? → **Answer:** *view(s)*.

## Step B — Travel nouns with pronouns

### EX-021-661 — "The guidebook is very useful. I take it everywhere."

1. **Meaning:** The travel book helps me a lot; it's always with me. 📘🗺️
2. **Situation:** A tourist talks about their trip.
3. **Main lesson:** *the guidebook* → *it* (object).
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + *is very* + adj · *I take it everywhere.*
5. **Sentence structure:** **The guidebook** (S) → **is** → **very useful** · **I** (S) → **take** (V) → **it** (O) → **everywhere**.
6. **Subject:** *The guidebook* · *I*.
7. **Verb:** *is* · *take*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *very useful* · *it* · *everywhere*.
10. **Word by word:** *guidebook* — compound noun (*guide* + *book*) · *useful* · *it* — the guidebook.
11. **How the rules work together:** thing → *it*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *These maps are old. Don't use them* (EX-021-615).
13. **Why this is correct:** clear pronoun.
14. **Other versions:** *These guidebooks are useful. I take them everywhere.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *I take him everywhere.* → ✅ *it* · ❌ *The guidebook is very usefull.* → ✅ *useful* (one *l*)
16. **Natural spoken English:** *This guidebook's brilliant — I never go anywhere without it.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** *useful* — the suffix *-ful* has one *l*.
18. **Pronunciation:** "GYD-buk" · "YOOS-ful".
19. **Pattern:** **The + noun + is + adj. I take it + place.**
20. **More examples:** *This bag is very light. I take it on every trip.*
21. **Quick check:** *usefull* or *useful*? → **Answer:** *useful*.

### EX-021-662 — "The hotel staff are wonderful. They remember our names."

1. **Meaning:** The people who work in the hotel are great; they know what we are called. 🏨🙂
2. **Situation:** Writing about a hotel.
3. **Main lesson:** *staff* = a group of people → often plural verb (UK) and *they*.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + group noun + *are* + adj · *They remember our* + plural noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The hotel staff** (S) → **are** → **wonderful** · **They** (S) → **remember** (V) → **our names** (O).
6. **Subject:** *The hotel staff* (head **staff**) · *They*.
7. **Verb:** *are* · *remember*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *wonderful* · *our names*.
10. **Word by word:** *hotel* — describes · *staff* 👩👨 — all the workers (never *staffs* for people) · *names* — C plural.
11. **How the rules work together:** group noun → *they*; UK English often uses *are* with *staff* (US often *is*).
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The flight attendants are very kind. They help the passengers* (EX-021-612).
13. **Why this is correct:** group noun treated as people.
14. **Other versions:** *The staff is very friendly.* (US style) · *The receptionists are wonderful.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The hotel staffs are…* → ✅ *staff* · ❌ *It remembers our names.* → ✅ *They remember* (people)
16. **Natural spoken English:** *The staff here are lovely — they know us by name.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stops.
18. **Pronunciation:** "STAHF" (UK) / "STAF" (US).
19. **Pattern:** **The + noun + staff + are + adj. They + verb + our + noun.**
20. **More examples:** *The kitchen staff are busy. They start at six.*
21. **Quick check:** *staffs* or *staff* for workers? → **Answer:** *staff*.

### EX-021-663 — "My bike has a flat tyre. Can you fix it?"

1. **Meaning:** One wheel of my bicycle has no air; can you repair it? 🚲🔧
2. **Situation:** At a bike shop on a cycling holiday.
3. **Main lesson:** *a flat tyre* → *it*.
4. **Grammar used:** *My* + noun + *has a* + adj + noun · *Can you* + verb + *it*?
5. **Sentence structure:** **My bike** (S) → **has** (V) → **a flat tyre** (O) · **Can** → **you** (S) → **fix** (V) → **it** (O)?
6. **Subject:** *My bike* · *you*.
7. **Verb:** *has* · *fix*.
8. **Helping verbs:** *Can*.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *a flat tyre* · *it* (= the tyre).
10. **Word by word:** *flat* — here: without air · *tyre* 📦 — rubber ring on a wheel (UK; US *tire*) · *fix* — repair.
11. **How the rules work together:** last C singular noun → *it*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *My suitcase is too small. I can't close it* (EX-021-611).
13. **Why this is correct:** clear pronoun; *can* + base verb.
14. **Other versions:** *My car has two flat tyres. Can you fix them?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Can you fix?* → ✅ *fix it* (*fix* needs an object) · ❌ *Can you fixing it?* → ✅ *fix*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *I've got a flat — can you sort it out?*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** UK *tyre* / US *tire*.
18. **Pronunciation:** "TY-er".
19. **Pattern:** **My + noun + has a + adj + noun. Can you + verb + it?**
20. **More examples:** *My watch has a broken strap. Can you change it?*
21. **Quick check:** US spelling of *tyre*? → **Answer:** *tire*.

### EX-021-664 — "We met two backpackers from Chile. We had dinner with them."

1. **Meaning:** We got to know two young travellers from Chile and ate dinner together. 🎒🇨🇱
2. **Situation:** Telling a hostel story.
3. **Main lesson:** *two backpackers* → *them* after a preposition.
4. **Grammar used:** *We met* + number + plural noun + *from* + country · *We had dinner with them.*
5. **Sentence structure:** **We** → **met** (V) → **two backpackers from Chile** (O) · **We** → **had** (V) → **dinner** (O) → **with them** (P).
6. **Subject:** *We* (both).
7. **Verb:** *met* (past of *meet*) · *had*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *two backpackers from Chile* · *dinner* · *with them*.
10. **Word by word:** *backpackers* 👩 — travellers with a backpack, usually on a budget · *dinner* — no article · *them* — object pronoun after *with*.
11. **How the rules work together:** plural people → *them*; after prepositions use object pronouns.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** object pronouns after prepositions ([L-028](../../01-beginner-grammar/pronouns/lesson-028-object-pronouns.md)).
13. **Why this is correct:** pronoun form matches its position.
14. **Other versions:** *We met a backpacker from Chile. We had dinner with her.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *…with they.* → ✅ *with them* · ❌ *We had a dinner with them.* → ✅ *had dinner*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *We met these two Chilean backpackers and had dinner with them.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** capital *C*.
18. **Pronunciation:** "BAK-pak-erz" · "CHIL-ee".
19. **Pattern:** **We met + number + nouns + from + country. We had dinner with them.**
20. **More examples:** *We met a family from Kenya. We went hiking with them.*
21. **Quick check:** *with they* or *with them*? → **Answer:** *with them*.

### EX-021-665 — "The souvenirs were expensive, so I didn't buy any."

1. **Meaning:** The small presents for tourists cost too much, so I bought none. 🗿💸
2. **Situation:** Leaving a gift shop.
3. **Main lesson:** *any* replaces a plural noun (*any* = any souvenirs).
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + plural noun + *were* + adj, *so I didn't* + verb + *any*.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The souvenirs** (S) → **were** → **expensive** · **so** · **I** (S) → **didn't buy** (V) → **any** (O).
6. **Subject:** *The souvenirs* · *I*.
7. **Verb:** *were* · *buy*.
8. **Helping verbs:** *didn't*.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *expensive* · *any* (= any souvenirs).
10. **Word by word:** *souvenirs* 📦 — things you buy to remember a place · *were* — past of *are* · *so* — result · *any* — used in negatives.
11. **How the rules work together:** plural → *were*; *any* can stand alone when the noun is clear.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *We don't have any eggs* (EX-021-567).
13. **Why this is correct:** agreement + natural short negative.

8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *in a tent* · *near a river*.
10. **Word by word:** *They* · *sleep* — no *-s* · *in a tent* · *near* — close to · *river* 📍.
11. **How the rules work together:** two people → *they* → base verb; *a* = not a specific tent/river.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *We camped by a lake in the mountains* (EX-021-685).
13. **Why this is correct:** pronoun + agreement + articles.
14. **Other versions:** *They sleep in the same tent every year.* (specific → *the*)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *They sleeps…* → ✅ *sleep* · ❌ *…near of a river.* → ✅ *near a river*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *They pitch their tent by a river.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "SLEEP-in-uh-TENT".
19. **Pattern:** **They + verb + in a + noun + near a + noun.**
20. **More examples:** *They stay in a cabin near a lake.*
21. **Quick check:** *near of a river* — correct? → **Answer:** No: *near a river*.

### EX-021-696 — "At night, they cook sausages on a small fire."

1. **Meaning:** In the dark evening, they make sausages hot on a little fire. 🔥🌭🌙
2. **Situation:** Last line of the story.
3. **Main lesson:** plural object + *on a* + adjective + noun; *at night*.
4. **Grammar used:** *At night,* + *they* + verb + plural noun + *on a* + adj + noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **At night,** → **they** (S) → **cook** (V) → **sausages** (O) → **on a small fire** (P).
6. **Subject:** *they*.
7. **Verb:** *cook*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *sausages* · *on a small fire*.
10. **Word by word:** *At night* — no *the* · *sausages* 📦 — C plural · *on a small fire* — cooking on top of fire.
11. **How the rules work together:** *at night* but *in the evening/morning*; general plural object → no article.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *We arrived at the hotel late in the evening* (EX-021-674).
13. **Why this is correct:** correct time phrase and articles.
14. **Other versions:** *In the evening, they cook dinner on a small fire.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *In the night, they cook…* → ✅ *At night* (for "every night") · ❌ *…cook sausage on a small fire.* → ✅ *sausages*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *At night they cook sausages over the fire.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** comma after *At night*.
18. **Pronunciation:** "SOS-i-jiz".
19. **Pattern:** **At night, they + verb + plural noun + on a + adj + noun.**
20. **More examples:** *At night, we sing songs around the fire.*
21. **Quick check:** *in the night* or *at night* for every night? → **Answer:** *at night*.

## Step J — Real writing: a travel blog

> 📝 **Day 3** — *This **morning**, we climbed the **hill** behind the **town**. The **views** of the **coast** were unforgettable.*

### EX-021-697 — "This morning, we climbed the hill behind the town."

1. **Meaning:** Earlier today, we walked up the hill that is at the back of the town. ⛰️🥾
2. **Situation:** A travel blog post.
3. **Main lesson:** object noun + place noun after *behind*.
4. **Grammar used:** *This morning,* + *we* + past verb + *the* + noun + *behind the* + noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **This morning,** → **we** (S) → **climbed** (V) → **the hill** (O) → **behind the town** (P).
6. **Subject:** *we*.
7. **Verb:** *climbed* (the *b* is silent).
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *the hill* · *behind the town*.
10. **Word by word:** *This morning* — no *in* · *climbed* — went up · *hill* 📍 — small mountain · *behind* · *town* 📍.
11. **How the rules work together:** finished time today → past; *the hill* = known one (the only hill there).
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The car park is behind the hotel* (EX-021-633).
13. **Why this is correct:** clear S–V–O + place.
14. **Other versions:** *Yesterday, we walked along the beach.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *In this morning…* → ✅ *This morning* · ❌ *…we climbed on the hill.* (OK if you mean the surface, but *climb the hill* = go up it)
16. **Natural spoken English:** *We hiked up the hill behind town this morning.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** comma after *This morning*.
18. **Pronunciation:** "KLYMD" (silent *b*).
19. **Pattern:** **This morning, we + past verb + the + noun + behind the + noun.**
20. **More examples:** *This afternoon, we visited the market near the harbour.*
21. **Quick check:** Is the *b* in *climbed* pronounced? → **Answer:** No.

### EX-021-698 — "The views of the coast were unforgettable."

1. **Meaning:** What we saw of the land next to the sea was so beautiful we'll always remember it. 🌅🌊
2. **Situation:** The blog post continues.
3. **Main lesson:** plural head *views* → *were*.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + plural noun + *of the* + noun + *were* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The views of the coast** (S) → **were** → **unforgettable**.
6. **Subject:** head **views**.
7. **Verb:** *were*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *unforgettable*.
10. **Word by word:** *views* — C plural · *of the coast* · *coast* 📍 — land next to the sea · *unforgettable* — *un-* + *forget* + *-able*.
11. **How the rules work together:** plural head → *were*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The scenery is beautiful* (EX-021-657) — U *scenery* vs C *views*.
13. **Why this is correct:** agreement with head noun.
14. **Other versions:** *The view of the coast was unforgettable.* (one view → *was*)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The views of the coast was…* → ✅ *were* · ❌ *unforgetable* → ✅ *unforgettable* (double *t*)
16. **Natural spoken English:** *The views of the coast were just incredible.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** spelling: *unforge**tt**able*.
18. **Pronunciation:** "un-fer-GET-uh-bl".
19. **Pattern:** **The + plural noun + of the + noun + were + adjective.**
20. **More examples:** *The colours of the sunset were amazing.*
21. **Quick check:** *was* or *were*? → **Answer:** *were* (*views* is plural).

## Step K — Noun or verb?

### EX-021-699 — "We land at six. / The land here is very dry."

1. **Meaning:** (1) Our plane comes down to the ground at 6. (2) The ground/earth in this area has no water. 🛬 / 🏜️
2. **Situation:** Flying to a desert country.
3. **Main lesson:** *land* as a verb (1) and a U noun (2).
4. **Grammar used:** *We* + verb + *at* + time · *The* + U noun + *here is very* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** (1) **We** → **land** (V) → **at six** · (2) **The land here** (S) → **is** → **very dry**.
6. **Subject:** (1) *We* · (2) *The land*.
7. **Verb:** (1) *land* · (2) *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *at six* · *very dry*.
10. **Word by word:** (1) *land* after *We* → verb · (2) *The land* → noun (U: ground, earth).
11. **How the rules work together:** after a subject → verb; after *the* → noun.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The plane landed at midnight* (EX-021-624).
13. **Why this is correct:** each word in its position.
14. **Other versions:** *The plane is landing now.* · *They bought some land.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The lands here are dry.* (for ground) → ✅ *The land here is dry.* · ❌ *We landing at six.* → ✅ *We land* / *We're landing*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *We get in at six.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** "LAND" (noun and verb the same).
19. **Pattern:** **We + verb + at + time / The + U noun + is + adjective**
20. **More examples:** *We camp here. / The camp is near the lake.*
21. **Quick check:** In *The land is dry*, noun or verb? → **Answer:** noun.

### EX-021-700 — "We fly to Rome tomorrow. / There's a fly in my soup!"

1. **Meaning:** (1) We go to Rome by plane tomorrow. (2) A small insect is in my soup! ✈️ / 🪰🍲
2. **Situation:** Before a trip; then a funny restaurant moment.
3. **Main lesson:** *fly* as a verb (1) and a C noun with a **different meaning** (2: an insect).
4. **Grammar used:** *We* + verb + *to* + city + time · *There's a* + noun + *in my* + noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** (1) **We** → **fly** (V) → **to Rome** → **tomorrow** · (2) **There** → **'s** → **a fly** (S) → **in my soup** (P).
6. **Subject:** (1) *We* · (2) *a fly*.
7. **Verb:** (1) *fly* · (2) *'s* (*is*).
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *to Rome* · *tomorrow* · *in my soup*.
10. **Word by word:** (1) *fly* after *We* → verb (present for a timetable plan) · (2) *a fly* — after *a* → noun: an insect (plural *flies*).
11. **How the rules work together:** after *a* → always a noun; same spelling can have a totally different meaning.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *My uncle is a pilot. He flies to Asia every week* (EX-021-614) — verb.
13. **Why this is correct:** each word in its position.
14. **Other versions:** *Flies are annoying in summer.* (noun, plural)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *There's a flies in my soup.* → ✅ *a fly* · ❌ *We flies to Rome.* → ✅ *We fly*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Waiter, there's a fly in my soup!* (a famous joke opener)
17. **Writing & punctuation:** **!** for surprise.
18. **Pronunciation:** "FLY" (noun and verb the same).
19. **Pattern:** **We + verb + to + place / There's a + noun + in my + noun**
20. **More examples:** *I watch TV. / My watch is new.*
21. **Quick check:** In *a fly*, is *fly* a noun or a verb? → **Answer:** noun (an insect).
