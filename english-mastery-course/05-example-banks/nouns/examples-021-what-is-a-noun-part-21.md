**Short reminders:** **Noun** = naming word (👩 person · 🐾 animal · 📍 place · 📦 thing · 💭 idea). **Positions:** **S** subject · **O** object · **B** after *be* · **P** after a preposition. **Head noun** decides the verb ([L-021 §7.4](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-021-what-is-a-noun.md#74-noun-phrases)).

> 🔁 **Weather & nature nouns:** *weather, rain, snow, wind, sunshine, thunder, lightning, fog, ice, water, grass* are usually **U** (no *a*, no *-s*): ❌ *a rain, good weathers*. One of a kind → **the**: *the sun, the moon, the sky, the sea, the world*. Weather verbs use **it**: *It's raining*. Irregular plural: *leaf → leaves*.

**New words in this part:** sky · wind · cloud · moon · calm · leaf/leaves · forest · thunder · grass · rainbow · plant · farmer · star · lightning · temperature · minus · flood · windy · thick · heavy · island · nest · waterfall · rock · foot (of a mountain) · desert · wave · forecast · sunset · valley · degree.

## Step A — One noun, short sentences

### EX-021-1001 — "The sky is grey."

1. **Meaning:** The sky has a grey colour (there are clouds). ☁️
2. **Situation:** Looking out of the window in the morning.
3. **Main lesson:** A "one of a kind" noun with **the**.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + *is* + colour.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The sky** (S) → **is** → **grey**.
6. **Subject:** *The sky*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *grey*.
10. **Word by word:** *the* — there is only one sky · *sky* 📍 — the space above the earth · *grey* (US *gray*).
11. **How the rules work together:** unique things → *the*; singular → *is*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The sun is hot* (EX-021-017) — another "only one" noun.
13. **Why this is correct:** *the* + unique noun + *be* + adjective.
14. **Other versions:** *The sky is blue today.* · *Is the sky clear?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Sky is grey.* → ✅ *The sky* · ❌ *A sky is grey.* → ✅ *The sky*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *It's a grey day.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** UK *grey* / US *gray*.
18. **Pronunciation:** "SKY" · "GRAY".
19. **Pattern:** **The + unique noun + is + adjective.**
20. **More examples:** *The moon is full. · The sea is blue.*
21. **Quick check:** Why *the sky*? → **Answer:** there is only one sky.

### EX-021-1002 — "Snow is beautiful."

1. **Meaning:** Snow looks lovely. ❄️😍
2. **Situation:** The first snow of winter.
3. **Main lesson:** U weather noun in general → no article.
4. **Grammar used:** U noun + *is* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Snow** (S) → **is** → **beautiful**.
6. **Subject:** *Snow*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *beautiful*.
10. **Word by word:** *snow* — U: soft white ice from the sky · *beautiful*.
11. **How the rules work together:** U in general → no *the*, singular verb.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The children played in the snow* (EX-021-059) — specific snow → *the*.
13. **Why this is correct:** U + *is*.
14. **Other versions:** *The snow is deep today.* (specific)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Snows are beautiful.* → ✅ *Snow is* · ❌ *A snow is beautiful.* → ✅ *Snow*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *I love snow!*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "SNOH".
19. **Pattern:** **U noun + is + adjective.**
20. **More examples:** *Rain is good for plants. · Ice is slippery.*
21. **Quick check:** C or U: *snow*? → **Answer:** U.

### EX-021-1004 — "Clouds are made of tiny drops of water."

1. **Meaning:** Clouds are made from very small water drops. ☁️💧
2. **Situation:** A science lesson.
3. **Main lesson:** general plural subject + *made of* + U noun.
4. **Grammar used:** plural noun + *are made of* + adj + plural noun + *of* + U noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Clouds** (S) → **are made** (V) → **of tiny drops of water** (P).
6. **Subject:** *Clouds*.
7. **Verb:** *made* (passive idea).
8. **Helping verbs:** *are*.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *of tiny drops of water*.
10. **Word by word:** *clouds* — C plural · *made of* — material · *tiny* · *drops* — C · *water* — U.
11. **How the rules work together:** *drops of* makes U *water* countable.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *a cup of tea, a piece of cake* (EX-021-080).
13. **Why this is correct:** agreement + count phrase.
14. **Other versions:** *Snow is made of ice crystals.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Clouds are made from waters.* → ✅ *water* · ❌ *Clouds is made…* → ✅ *are*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Clouds are just tiny bits of water.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "KLOWDZ".
19. **Pattern:** **Plural noun + are made of + noun + of + U noun.**
20. **More examples:** *Tables are made of wood.*
21. **Quick check:** How do you count *water*? → **Answer:** with words like *drops of, a glass of*.

### EX-021-1005 — "The moon is very bright tonight."

1. **Meaning:** The moon is giving a lot of light this night. 🌕✨
2. **Situation:** Walking home at night.
3. **Main lesson:** unique noun + adjective + *tonight*.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + *is very* + adjective + *tonight*.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The moon** (S) → **is** → **very bright** → **tonight**.
6. **Subject:** *The moon*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *very bright* · *tonight*.
10. **Word by word:** *the moon* — only one · *bright* — full of light · *tonight* — this night.
11. **How the rules work together:** unique → *the*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The sky is grey* (EX-021-1001).
13. **Why this is correct:** S + *be* + adjective.
14. **Other versions:** *There's a full moon tonight.* (*a* + adjective + *moon* = one type/phase)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Moon is very bright.* → ✅ *The moon* · ❌ *…at tonight.* → ✅ *tonight*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *The moon's so bright tonight!*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** *moon* — small letter in normal use.
18. **Pronunciation:** "MOON" · "BRYT".
19. **Pattern:** **The + unique noun + is very + adjective + tonight.**
20. **More examples:** *The stars are very bright tonight.*
21. **Quick check:** *a full moon* — why *a*? → **Answer:** an adjective describes one type/phase of the moon.

### EX-021-1007 — "The leaves are falling."

1. **Meaning:** The leaves are coming down from the trees. 🍂⬇️
2. **Situation:** Autumn in the park.
3. **Main lesson:** irregular plural *leaf → leaves* + present continuous.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + plural noun + *are* + verb-*ing*.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The leaves** (S) → **are falling** (V).
6. **Subject:** *The leaves*.
7. **Verb:** *falling*.
8. **Helping verbs:** *are*.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** none.
10. **Word by word:** *leaves* — plural of *leaf* (*-f* → *-ves*) · *are falling* — now.
11. **How the rules work together:** plural → *are* + *-ing*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The knives are sharp* (EX-021-559) — same *-f/-fe → -ves* rule.
13. **Why this is correct:** correct plural + agreement.
14. **Other versions:** *A leaf is falling.* · *The leaves are turning red.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The leafs are falling.* → ✅ *leaves* · ❌ *The leaves is falling.* → ✅ *are*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *The leaves are coming down.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "LEEVZ".
19. **Pattern:** **The + plural noun + are + verb-ing.**
20. **More examples:** *The birds are singing. · The flowers are opening.*
21. **Quick check:** Plural of *leaf*? → **Answer:** *leaves*.

### EX-021-1008 — "The forest is dark and quiet."

1. **Meaning:** In the forest, there's little light and little noise. 🌲🌲🤫
2. **Situation:** A walk among the trees.
3. **Main lesson:** place noun + two adjectives with *and*.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + *is* + adj + *and* + adj.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The forest** (S) → **is** → **dark and quiet**.
6. **Subject:** *The forest*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *dark and quiet*.
10. **Word by word:** *forest* 📍 — large area with many trees · *dark* · *quiet*.
11. **How the rules work together:** singular → *is*; two adjectives after *be* → *and*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The market is noisy and colourful* (EX-021-759).
13. **Why this is correct:** S + *be* + adj + *and* + adj.
14. **Other versions:** *It's a dark, quiet forest.* (before the noun → comma)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The forest is dark quiet.* → ✅ *dark and quiet* · ❌ *The forest is quite.* → ✅ *quiet*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *It's so dark and quiet in the woods.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** *quiet* ≠ *quite*.
18. **Pronunciation:** "FOR-ist".
19. **Pattern:** **The + place + is + adj + and + adj.**
20. **More examples:** *The desert is hot and dry.*
21. **Quick check:** *quiet* or *quite* = not noisy? → **Answer:** *quiet*.

## Step B — Nature nouns with pronouns

### EX-021-1011 — "Look at the rainbow! It's beautiful."

1. **Meaning:** See the coloured arc in the sky — it's lovely! 🌈
2. **Situation:** After rain, the sun comes out.
3. **Main lesson:** *the rainbow* → *it*; compound noun (*rain* + *bow*).
4. **Grammar used:** *Look at the* + noun! · *It's* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** **(You)** → **Look** (V) → **at the rainbow** (P)! · **It** (S) → **'s** → **beautiful**.
6. **Subject:** hidden *you* · *It*.
7. **Verb:** *Look* · *'s*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *at the rainbow* · *beautiful*.
10. **Word by word:** *Look at* — always *at* before the thing · *rainbow* — compound noun · *It* — the rainbow.
11. **How the rules work together:** thing → *it*; *look at* + noun.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The new library is huge. Have you seen it?* (EX-021-711).
13. **Why this is correct:** clear pronoun.
14. **Other versions:** *Look at the stars! They're amazing.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Look the rainbow!* → ✅ *Look at* · ❌ *…She's beautiful.* → ✅ *It's*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Ooh, look — a rainbow!*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** **!** after the command.
18. **Pronunciation:** "RAYN-boh".
19. **Pattern:** **Look at the + noun! It's + adjective.**
20. **More examples:** *Look at the sunset! It's amazing.*
21. **Quick check:** *Look the rainbow* or *Look at the rainbow*? → **Answer:** *Look at the rainbow*.

### EX-021-1012 — "The mountains are very high. Snow covers them all year."

1. **Meaning:** The mountains are tall; there is snow on them every month. 🏔️❄️
2. **Situation:** A guide describes the Alps.
3. **Main lesson:** *the mountains* → *them*; U subject *snow* → *covers*.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + plural noun + *are very* + adj · U noun + verb-s + *them all year*.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The mountains** (S) → **are** → **very high** · **Snow** (S) → **covers** (V) → **them** (O) → **all year**.
6. **Subject:** *The mountains* · *Snow*.
7. **Verb:** *are* · *covers*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *very high* · *them* · *all year*.
10. **Word by word:** *mountains* · *high* · *Snow* — U → *covers* (with *-s*) · *them* — the mountains · *all year* — no *the*.
11. **How the rules work together:** U subject → verb-*s*; plural object → *them*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *We camped by a lake in the mountains* (EX-021-685).
13. **Why this is correct:** two correct agreements.
14. **Other versions:** *The mountain is very high. Snow covers it all year.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Snow cover them…* → ✅ *covers* · ❌ *…covers they…* → ✅ *them*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Those mountains have snow on them all year round.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** "MOWN-tunz".
19. **Pattern:** **The + plural noun + are + adj. U noun + verb-s + them + time.**
20. **More examples:** *The roads are icy. Ice covers them every winter.*
21. **Quick check:** *Snow cover* or *Snow covers*? → **Answer:** *Snow covers*.

### EX-021-1013 — "This plant needs water. Can you give it some?"

1. **Meaning:** This plant is dry; please give it a little water. 🪴💧
2. **Situation:** Asking a flatmate before you go away.
3. **Main lesson:** *this plant* → *it*; *some* replaces *some water*.
4. **Grammar used:** *This* + noun + verb-s + U noun · *Can you give it some?*
5. **Sentence structure:** **This plant** (S) → **needs** (V) → **water** (O) · **Can** → **you** (S) → **give** (V) → **it** (O1) → **some** (O2)?
6. **Subject:** *This plant* · *you*.
7. **Verb:** *needs* · *give*.
8. **Helping verbs:** *Can*.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *water* · *it* · *some*.
10. **Word by word:** *plant* · *needs* · *water* — U · *it* — the plant · *some* — some water (pronoun).
11. **How the rules work together:** *some* can stand alone when the U noun is clear.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The souvenirs were expensive, so I didn't buy any* (EX-021-665) — *any* standing alone.
13. **Why this is correct:** clear references.
14. **Other versions:** *These plants need water. Can you give them some?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *This plant need water.* → ✅ *needs* · ❌ *Can you give it a water?* → ✅ *some (water)*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Can you water this plant?* (verb *water*)
17. **Writing & punctuation:** **?**
18. **Pronunciation:** "PLAHNT" (UK) / "PLANT" (US).
19. **Pattern:** **This + noun + needs + U noun. Can you give it some?**
20. **More examples:** *The dog is hungry. Can you give him some food?*
21. **Quick check:** What does *some* mean here? → **Answer:** some water.

### EX-021-1014 — "The farmers are worried. They need rain."

1. **Meaning:** The farmers are anxious; there hasn't been rain and their crops need it. 👨‍🌾🌧️
2. **Situation:** A dry summer news story.
3. **Main lesson:** *the farmers* → *they*; U noun *rain* as the object.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + plural noun + *are* + adj · *They need* + U noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The farmers** (S) → **are** → **worried** · **They** (S) → **need** (V) → **rain** (O).
6. **Subject:** *The farmers* · *They*.
7. **Verb:** *are* · *need*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *worried* · *rain*.
10. **Word by word:** *farmers* · *worried* — *-ed* feeling · *rain* — U.
11. **How the rules work together:** plural → *they*; U object → no article.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *My uncle is a farmer* (EX-021-023) · *Rain falls* (EX-021-018).

15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *They need a rain.* → ✅ *rain* · ❌ *The farmers are worrying.* (= making others worry) → ✅ *worried*

17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** "FAR-merz" · "WUR-eed".
19. **Pattern:** **The + plural noun + are + adj. They need + U noun.**
20. **More examples:** *The plants are dry. They need water.*
21. **Quick check:** *a rain* or *rain*? → **Answer:** *rain*.

### EX-021-1015 — "The river is dangerous here. Don't swim in it."

1. **Meaning:** The river is not safe at this place; don't go swimming in it. 🏞️⚠️
2. **Situation:** A warning sign.
3. **Main lesson:** *the river* → *it* after *in*.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + *is* + adj + *here* · *Don't* + verb + *in it*.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The river** (S) → **is** → **dangerous** → **here** · **(You)** → **Don't swim** (V) → **in it** (P).
6. **Subject:** *The river* · hidden *you*.
7. **Verb:** *is* · *swim*.
8. **Helping verbs:** *Don't*.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *dangerous* · *here* · *in it*.
10. **Word by word:** *river* · *dangerous* · *here* · *Don't swim* · *in it* — inside the river.
11. **How the rules work together:** thing → *it* after a preposition.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The road is very dangerous for children at night* (EX-021-748).
13. **Why this is correct:** clear pronoun.
14. **Other versions:** *The lake is deep. Don't swim in it.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Don't swim on it.* → ✅ *in it* · ❌ *Don't swimming in it.* → ✅ *Don't swim*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *No swimming — it's dangerous here.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** "RIV-er".
19. **Pattern:** **The + noun + is dangerous here. Don't + verb + in it.**
20. **More examples:** *The ice is thin. Don't walk on it.*
21. **Quick check:** *swim on* or *swim in* a river? → **Answer:** *swim in*.

## Step C — Questions and negatives

### EX-021-1016 — "What's the weather like today?"

1. **Meaning:** How is the weather today? (sunny, rainy, cold…) 🌤️❓

1. **Meaning:** In the south, it will get as warm as 25 °C. 🌡️☀️
2. **Situation:** The forecast continues.
3. **Main lesson:** plural *temperatures* (forecast style) + number + *degrees*.
4. **Grammar used:** plural noun + *will reach* + number + plural noun + *in the* + noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Temperatures** (S) → **will reach** (V) → **twenty-five degrees** (O) → **in the south** (P).
6. **Subject:** *Temperatures*.
7. **Verb:** *reach*.
8. **Helping verbs:** *will*.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *twenty-five degrees* · *in the south*.
10. **Word by word:** *Temperatures* — plural (different places) · *reach* — get to · *degrees* — units (°).
11. **How the rules work together:** number above one → plural *degrees*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Tomorrow, the temperature will drop to minus five* (EX-021-1025).
13. **Why this is correct:** future + plural number.
14. **Other versions:** *It will be 25 degrees in the south.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *…twenty-five degree…* → ✅ *degrees* · ❌ *…will reaches…* → ✅ *will reach*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *It'll hit 25 in the south.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** 25 °C; hyphen in *twenty-five*.
18. **Pronunciation:** "dih-GREEZ".
19. **Pattern:** **Plural noun + will reach + number + degrees + in the + direction.**
20. **More examples:** *Temperatures will fall to five degrees tonight.*
21. **Quick check:** *25 degree* or *25 degrees*? → **Answer:** *25 degrees*.

## Step K — Noun or verb?

### EX-021-1049 — "Please water the plants. / Plants need water."

1. **Meaning:** (1) Please give the plants water. (2) Plants can't live without water. 🪴💧
2. **Situation:** Leaving instructions for a neighbour.
3. **Main lesson:** *water* as a verb (1) and a U noun (2).
4. **Grammar used:** *Please* + verb + *the* + plural noun · plural noun + *need* + U noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** (1) **(You)** → **water** (V) → **the plants** (O) · (2) **Plants** (S) → **need** (V) → **water** (O).
6. **Subject:** (1) hidden *you* · (2) *Plants*.
7. **Verb:** (1) *water* · (2) *need*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *the plants* · *water*.
10. **Word by word:** (1) *water* after *Please* → verb (give water to) · (2) *water* after *need* → noun (U).
11. **How the rules work together:** command position → verb; object position → noun.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *This plant needs water. Can you give it some?* (EX-021-1013).
13. **Why this is correct:** each word in its position.
14. **Other versions:** *I water my garden every evening.* (verb) · *The water is cold.* (noun)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Please waters the plants.* → ✅ *water* · ❌ *Plants need a water.* → ✅ *water*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Can you water the plants?*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** "WAW-ter" (noun and verb the same).
19. **Pattern:** **Please + verb + the + noun / Plural noun + need + U noun**
20. **More examples:** *Please dust the shelves. / There's dust everywhere.*
21. **Quick check:** In *Plants need water*, is *water* a noun or a verb? → **Answer:** noun.

### EX-021-1050 — "It's snowing! / The snow is very deep."

1. **Meaning:** (1) Snow is falling now! (2) There's a lot of snow on the ground. ❄️🌨️
2. **Situation:** A winter morning.
3. **Main lesson:** *snow* as a verb (1, with weather *it*) and a U noun (2).
4. **Grammar used:** *It's* + verb-*ing*! · *The* + U noun + *is very* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** (1) **It** (S) → **'s snowing** (V)! · (2) **The snow** (S) → **is** → **very deep**.
6. **Subject:** (1) *It* (weather) · (2) *The snow*.
7. **Verb:** (1) *snowing* · (2) *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** (1) *'s* (*is*).
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *very deep*.
10. **Word by word:** (1) *snowing* — verb (*is* + *-ing*) · (2) *The snow* — after *the* → noun (U).
11. **How the rules work together:** after *is* + *-ing* → verb; after *the* → noun.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Snow is beautiful* (EX-021-1002) — noun.
13. **Why this is correct:** each word in its position.
14. **Other versions:** *It snowed all night.* (verb) · *We played in the snow.* (noun)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Is snowing!* → ✅ *It's snowing!* · ❌ *The snows are very deep.* → ✅ *The snow is*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *It's snowing! The snow's really deep!*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** **!** for excitement.
18. **Pronunciation:** "SNOH-ing".
19. **Pattern:** **It's + weather verb-ing! / The + U noun + is + adjective.**
20. **More examples:** *It's raining! / The rain is heavy.*
21. **Quick check:** In *The snow is very deep*, noun or verb? → **Answer:** noun.
