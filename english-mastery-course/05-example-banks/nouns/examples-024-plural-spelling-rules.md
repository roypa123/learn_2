# Example Bank 024 — Plural Spelling Rules (EX-024-013 to EX-024-100)

**For lesson:** [L-024 — Plural Spelling Rules](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-024-plural-spelling-rules.md) · EX-024-001 to 012 are in the lesson.

## How to use this bank

- **Cover the notes.** For each **bold** plural: (1) which **rule** (1–5)? (2) which **sound** — /s/, /z/ or /ɪz/? Then check.
- **Rules:** **R1** + s · **R2** + es (s, ss, sh, ch, x, z) · **R3** consonant+y → ies / vowel+y → s · **R4** f/fe → ves (or exception + s) · **R5** -o → s / es

> 🔁 Sound: **/ɪz/** after hissing sounds · **/s/** after voiceless (p, t, k, f, th) · **/z/** after voiced and vowels ([L-024 §7.6](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-024-plural-spelling-rules.md#76-how-to-pronounce-plural--s-and--es)).

## Step 1 — One rule at a time, short sentences (EX-024-013 to 022)

**EX-024-013** — *I have three **cups**.* — R1 · /s/ (p voiceless)
**EX-024-014** — *The **dogs** are asleep.* — R1 · /z/ (g voiced)
**EX-024-015** — *I need new **glasses**.* — *glass* + es — R2 · /ɪz/ (here *glasses* = for your eyes 👓, always plural)
**EX-024-016** — *Two **churches** are on this street.* — R2 (-ch) · /ɪz/
**EX-024-017** — *The **babies** are sleeping.* — R3 (b + y → ies) · /z/
**EX-024-018** — *The **boys** play outside.* — R3 (vowel o + y → s) · /z/
**EX-024-019** — *Autumn **leaves** fall.* — R4 · /z/
**EX-024-020** — *Hello, **heroes**!* — R5 (+es) · /z/
**EX-024-021** — *Look at these **photos**.* — R5 (+s) · /z/
**EX-024-022** — *The **foxes** are hungry.* — R2 (-x) · /ɪz/

## Step 2 — Many nouns, each rule (EX-024-023 to 032)

**EX-024-023** — *We have two **classes** on Monday.*
- *class* (-ss) → R2 · /ɪz/ (2 syllables: CLASS-iz).

**EX-024-024** — *The **brushes** are in the bathroom.*
- *brush* (-sh) → R2 · /ɪz/.

**EX-024-025** — *I love **stories** about animals.*
- *story* (r + y) → R3 · /z/. *animals* — R1 · /z/.

**EX-024-026** — *All the **keys** are on the hook.*
- *key* (e + y) → R3 vowel → +s · /z/. ❌ *keies*.

**EX-024-027** — *Cut the cake into two **halves**.*
- *half* → R4 · /z/ (silent l: "hahvz").

**EX-024-028** — *The **chefs** in this restaurant are French.*
- *chef* → R4 **exception** → +s · /s/.

**EX-024-029** — *Bring your **radios**.*
- *radio* (vowel i + o) → R5 +s · /z/.

**EX-024-030** — *The **buses** stop here.*
- *bus* → R2 · /ɪz/.

**EX-024-031** — *Many **countries** speak Spanish.*
- *country* (r + y) → R3 · /z/.

**EX-024-032** — *These **toys** are for the children.*
- *toy* (vowel o + y) → +s · /z/.

## Step 3 — With pronouns (EX-024-033 to 042)

**EX-024-033** — *I bought some **peaches**. They are sweet.*
- *peach* → R2 · /ɪz/ · *They* = peaches.

**EX-024-034** — *Where are the **knives**? I can't find them.*
- R4 · /z/ · *them* = knives.

**EX-024-035** — *My **cousins** are funny. They make me laugh.*
- R1 · /z/.

**EX-024-036** — *The **parties** were great. Everyone loved them.*
- *party* → R3 · /z/.

**EX-024-037** — *Those **wolves** are dangerous. Don't go near them.*
- *wolf* → R4 · /z/ ("woolvz").

**EX-024-038** — *I have two **wishes**. Can you guess them?*
- *wish* → R2 · /ɪz/.

**EX-024-039** — *These **tomatoes** are from my garden. I grow them myself.*
- R5 +es · /z/.

**EX-024-040** — *The **pianos** are old, but they sound beautiful.*
- *piano* → R5 +s · /z/.

**EX-024-041** — *My **days** are busy. They start at 6 am.*
- *day* → R3 vowel → +s · /z/.

**EX-024-042** — *The **monkeys** at the zoo are cute. They eat bananas.*
- *monkey* (vowel e + y) → +s · /z/. *bananas* R1.

## Step 5 — Different times (EX-024-053 to 060)

**EX-024-053** — *The **thieves** stole three **watches** last night.*
- *thief* R4 · *watch* R2. *stole* = past of *steal* (take without asking).

**EX-024-054** — *We will need more **batteries** tomorrow.*
- *battery* R3 · /z/.

**EX-024-055** — *The **quizzes** were difficult.*
- *quiz* → **zz** + es · /ɪz/.

**EX-024-056** — *Our **holidays** were too short.*
- *holiday* (vowel a + y) → +s.

**EX-024-057** — *Ten years ago, there were only two **cafés** in this town.*
- *café* R1 · /z/.

**EX-024-058** — *The **heroes** of the story will save the city.*
- R5 +es.

**EX-024-059** — *The **echoes** in the cave were loud.*
- *echo* R5 +es · /z/. *cave* = a big hole in a mountain.

**EX-024-060** — *Last summer, we picked **cherries** every day.*
- *cherry* R3 · /z/.

## Step 7 — Plurals + adjectives + prepositions/places (EX-024-071 to 078)

**EX-024-071** — *The **benches** in the park are wet.*
- *bench* R2 · /ɪz/ · head *benches* → *are*.

**EX-024-072** — *There are many **libraries** in the city centre.*
- *library* R3 · /z/.

**EX-024-073** — *The **loaves** of bread on the table are still warm.*
- *loaf* R4 · head *loaves* → *are*.

**EX-024-074** — *Put the **glasses** in the cupboard above the sink.*
- R2. Here *glasses* = drinking glasses.

**EX-024-075** — *The **videos** on this website are free.*
- R5 +s.

**EX-024-076** — *We stayed in small **villages** in the mountains.*
- *village* R1 (ends in **e**) — but the sound is **/ɪz/** because of the "j" sound /dʒ/: "VIL-ij-iz".

**EX-024-077** — *The **cliffs** near the sea are very high.*
- *cliff* → R4 exception (+s) · /s/.

**EX-024-078** — *The **lives** of people in the past were hard.*
- *life* R4 · /z/ ("lyvz"). ⚠️ *lives* (noun, "lyvz") vs *he lives* (verb, "livz").

## Step 8 — Longer sentences (EX-024-079 to 086)

**EX-024-079** — *In the kitchen, there are two **knives**, four **forks**, six **spoons** and a lot of **dishes**.*
- R4, R1 (/s/), R1 (/z/), R2 (/ɪz/) — all four sounds of plural in one sentence!

**EX-024-080** — *The **countries** of Europe have many different **languages** and **currencies**.*
- *country, currency* R3 · *language* R1 but sound /ɪz/ (ends in /dʒ/). *currency* = a country's money (€, £).

**EX-024-081** — *My grandparents keep old **photos**, **letters** and **diaries** in three wooden **boxes**.*
- R5, R1, R3 (*diary*), R2.

**EX-024-082** — *The **thieves** took the **watches**, but the police found them in two **bushes** behind the shop.*
- R4, R2, R2 (*bush* = a small tree).

**EX-024-083** — *On sunny **days**, the **beaches** are full of **families** with **babies**.*
- R3 vowel (*days*), R2, R3, R3.

**EX-024-084** — *We bought **potatoes**, **tomatoes** and **mangoes** at the market, and two **loaves** for breakfast.*
- R5 (+es ×3 — *mangos* is also correct), R4.

**EX-024-085** — *The **chefs** in the hotel use sharp **knives** and very hot **dishes**.*
- R4 exception, R4, R2.

**EX-024-086** — *All the **classes** at the school have **quizzes** on Fridays.*
- R2, R2 (zz). *Fridays* R1 (proper noun + s = every Friday).

## Step 10 — Short story (EX-024-091 to 093)

> *(1) Two little **foxes** live in the **bushes** behind our house. (2) At night, they look for food near the **boxes** and **dishes** outside. (3) In the morning, we find **leaves** and small **footprints** everywhere.*

**EX-024-091** — (1) *foxes, bushes* — R2 · /ɪz/.
**EX-024-092** — (2) *boxes, dishes* — R2 · /ɪz/. *they* = the foxes.
**EX-024-093** — (3) *leaves* — R4 · *footprints* — R1 /s/ (*footprint* = the mark a foot makes).

## Step 11 — Real writing: a hotel review (EX-024-094 to 096)

> *Great hotel! The **rooms** are big, the **beds** are comfortable, and the **balconies** have beautiful **views**. The **buses** to the city stop right outside. Five **stars**!*

**EX-024-094** — *rooms, beds, views, stars* — R1 · all /z/.
**EX-024-095** — *balconies* — *balcony* (n + y) → R3.
**EX-024-096** — *buses* — R2 · /ɪz/ · head *buses* → *stop* (no -s).

## Step 12 — Tricky pairs (EX-024-097 to 100)

**EX-024-097** — *two **days*** vs *two **babies***
- *d**a**y* (vowel + y) → +s. *ba**b**y* (consonant + y) → ies. Look at the letter **before** *y*.

**EX-024-098** — *two **roofs*** vs *two **wolves***
- *roof* = exception (+s). *wolf* = rule (→ ves). Learn the exceptions as a list: *roofs, chefs, cliffs, beliefs, chiefs, safes*.

**EX-024-099** — *two **photos*** vs *two **potatoes***
- *photo* (short form) → +s. *potato* → +es. Memory: "*heroes eat tomatoes and potatoes*".

**EX-024-100** — *the **lives** of animals* vs *she **lives** in Rome*
- Noun *lives* ("lyvz", plural of *life*) vs verb *lives* ("livz", she/he form of *live*). Same spelling, different sound and meaning!
