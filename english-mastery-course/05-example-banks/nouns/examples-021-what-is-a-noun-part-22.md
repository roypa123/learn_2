**Short reminders:** **Noun** = naming word (👩 person · 🐾 animal · 📍 place · 📦 thing · 💭 idea). **Positions:** **S** subject · **O** object · **B** after *be* · **P** after a preposition. **Head noun** decides the verb ([L-021 §7.4](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-021-what-is-a-noun.md#74-noun-phrases)).

> 🔁 **From Part 21:** weather nouns are usually **U**; unique things take **the** (*the sun, the earth*). **New here — quantity heads:** *the amount of* + U → singular verb; *some of / most of* + **plural** → plural verb, + **U** → singular verb.

**New words in this part:** season · earth · plastic · ocean · recycling · rose · soil · fog · nature · cliff · vegetable garden · bin · oak · scientist · climate · sunflower · pollution · wolf/wolves · volunteer · frost · energy · pond · path · edge · pole · penguin · amount · danger · rubbish · tap · drop · waste.

## Step A — One noun, short sentences

### EX-021-1051 — "Spring is my favourite season."

1. **Meaning:** Of the four seasons, I like spring the most. 🌸
2. **Situation:** A class discussion about seasons.
3. **Main lesson:** A season noun (no article) as the subject.
4. **Grammar used:** season + *is my favourite* + noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Spring** (S) → **is** → **my favourite season** (B).
6. **Subject:** *Spring*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *my favourite season*.
10. **Word by word:** *Spring* — season (small letter, no *the* in general) · *favourite* · *season* — C (*four seasons*).
11. **How the rules work together:** seasons in general → no article; noun after *be*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Potatoes are cheap in autumn* (EX-021-556).
13. **Why this is correct:** S + *be* + noun phrase.
14. **Other versions:** *Winter is my least favourite season.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The spring is my favourite season.* (general → *Spring*) · ❌ *Spring is my favourite seasons.* → ✅ *season*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *I love spring best.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** seasons: small letters (*spring, summer*).
18. **Pronunciation:** "SPRING" · "SEE-zun".
19. **Pattern:** **Season + is my favourite season.**
20. **More examples:** *Summer is the hottest season.*
21. **Quick check:** Capital or small letter for seasons? → **Answer:** small.

### EX-021-1052 — "The earth goes around the sun."

1. **Meaning:** Our planet moves in a circle around the sun. 🌍➡️☀️
2. **Situation:** A science lesson.
3. **Main lesson:** two unique nouns with *the*.
4. **Grammar used:** *the* + noun + verb-s + *around the* + noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The earth** (S) → **goes** (V) → **around the sun** (P).
6. **Subject:** *The earth*.
7. **Verb:** *goes*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *around the sun*.
10. **Word by word:** *the earth* — our planet (also *Earth* as a name) · *goes around* · *the sun*.
11. **How the rules work together:** unique nouns → *the*; scientific fact → present simple.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The sky is grey* (EX-021-1001).
13. **Why this is correct:** fact + agreement.
14. **Other versions:** *The moon goes around the earth.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The earth go around…* → ✅ *goes* · ❌ *The earth is going around the sun.* (fact → simple)
16. **Natural spoken English:** *The Earth orbits the Sun.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** capital *Earth* when used as a planet name (also fine small).
18. **Pronunciation:** "URTH".
19. **Pattern:** **The + unique noun + verb-s + around the + unique noun.**
20. **More examples:** *The moon goes around the earth.*
21. **Quick check:** *go* or *goes*? → **Answer:** *goes*.

### EX-021-1053 — "Plastic is a big problem for the oceans."

1. **Meaning:** Plastic rubbish seriously harms the seas. 🧴🌊⚠️
2. **Situation:** An environment talk.
3. **Main lesson:** U material noun as the subject.
4. **Grammar used:** U noun + *is a big* + noun + *for the* + plural noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Plastic** (S) → **is** → **a big problem** (B) → **for the oceans** (P).
6. **Subject:** *Plastic*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *a big problem* · *for the oceans*.
10. **Word by word:** *plastic* — U material · *problem* — C · *oceans* — the world's big seas.
11. **How the rules work together:** U subject → *is*; C complement → *a*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Pollution is a big problem* (EX-021-703).
13. **Why this is correct:** U + *is* + *a* + C.
14. **Other versions:** *Plastic bags are a big problem.* (C plural → *are*)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The plastic is a big problem.* (general → *Plastic*) · ❌ *Plastics is…* → ✅ *Plastic is*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Plastic is killing the oceans.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "PLAS-tik" · "OH-shunz".
19. **Pattern:** **U noun + is a big problem for the + plural noun.**
20. **More examples:** *Rubbish is a big problem for the city.*
21. **Quick check:** C or U: *plastic* (the material)? → **Answer:** U.

### EX-021-1054 — "Recycling is important."

1. **Meaning:** Using old things again (paper, glass, plastic) matters. ♻️
2. **Situation:** A school poster.
3. **Main lesson:** *-ing* activity noun subject.
4. **Grammar used:** verb-*ing* + *is* + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Recycling** (S) → **is** → **important**.
6. **Subject:** *Recycling*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *important*.
10. **Word by word:** *recycling* — the activity of *recycle* (*re-* = again + *cycle*) · *important*.
11. **How the rules work together:** activity → singular verb.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Smoking is dangerous* (EX-021-856) · *Reading is relaxing* (EX-021-952).
13. **Why this is correct:** *-ing* noun + *is*.
14. **Other versions:** *Recycling helps the planet.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Recycle is important.* → ✅ *Recycling* · ❌ *Recycling are important.* → ✅ *is*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Recycling really matters.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "ree-SY-kling".
19. **Pattern:** **Verb-ing + is + adjective.**
20. **More examples:** *Saving water is important.*
21. **Quick check:** *Recycle is important* — correct? → **Answer:** No: *Recycling is important*.

### EX-021-1055 — "These roses smell lovely."

1. **Meaning:** These roses have a nice smell. 🌹👃
2. **Situation:** In a garden.
3. **Main lesson:** *these* + plural noun + sense verb + adjective.
4. **Grammar used:** *These* + plural noun + verb + adjective.
5. **Sentence structure:** **These roses** (S) → **smell** (V) → **lovely**.
6. **Subject:** *These roses*.
7. **Verb:** *smell*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *lovely* (adjective, not adverb).
10. **Word by word:** *These* · *roses* — *rose* + *s* · *smell* — sense verb · *lovely* — adjective (looks like an adverb but isn't!).
11. **How the rules work together:** plural → no *-s* on the verb; sense verb + adjective.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *My guitar is old, but it sounds great* (EX-021-908).
13. **Why this is correct:** agreement + sense-verb pattern.
14. **Other versions:** *This rose smells lovely.* (singular → *smells*)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *These roses smells…* → ✅ *smell* · ❌ *These roses smell lovelily.* → ✅ *lovely*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *These roses smell gorgeous.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** full stop.
18. **Pronunciation:** "ROH-ziz".
19. **Pattern:** **These + plural noun + smell + adjective.**
20. **More examples:** *These cakes look delicious.*
21. **Quick check:** Is *lovely* an adjective or adverb? → **Answer:** adjective.

## Step B — Nature nouns with pronouns

### EX-021-1061 — "We have a vegetable garden. We grow tomatoes in it."

1. **Meaning:** We have a garden for vegetables, and we grow tomatoes there. 🥕🍅
2. **Situation:** Talking about home.
3. **Main lesson:** *a vegetable garden* → *it* after *in*.
4. **Grammar used:** *We have a* + noun + noun · *We grow* + plural noun + *in it*.
5. **Sentence structure:** **We** → **have** (V) → **a vegetable garden** (O) · **We** → **grow** (V) → **tomatoes** (O) → **in it** (P).
6. **Subject:** *We* (both).
7. **Verb:** *have* · *grow*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *a vegetable garden* · *tomatoes* · *in it*.
10. **Word by word:** *vegetable* — type · *garden* — head · *tomatoes* · *it* — the garden.
11. **How the rules work together:** first mention *a*; thing → *it*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Last year, we grew tomatoes in our garden* (EX-021-574).
13. **Why this is correct:** clear pronoun.
14. **Other versions:** *We have two gardens. We grow vegetables in them.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *…a vegetables garden.* → ✅ *vegetable garden* · ❌ *We grow tomatos…* → ✅ *tomatoes*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *We've got a veg patch — we grow tomatoes.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** "VEJ-tuh-bul-GAR-dn".
19. **Pattern:** **We have a + noun + noun. We grow + plural noun + in it.**
20. **More examples:** *We have a greenhouse. We grow peppers in it.*
21. **Quick check:** *a vegetables garden* — correct? → **Answer:** No: *a vegetable garden*.

18. **Pronunciation:** "OHK".

## Step C — Questions and negatives

### EX-021-1066 — "How much rain did you get last night?"

1. **Meaning:** What amount of rain fell where you are last night? 🌧️❓
2. **Situation:** Neighbours comparing weather.
3. **Main lesson:** *How much* + U noun (object) in a past question.
4. **Grammar used:** *How much* + U noun + *did you get* + time?
5. **Sentence structure:** **How much rain** (O) → **did** → **you** (S) → **get** (V) → **last night**?

## Step K — Noun or verb?

### EX-021-1099 — "Don't waste paper. / This is a waste of time."

1. **Meaning:** (1) Don't use more paper than you need. (2) This activity uses time badly. 📄🗑️ / ⏳😩
2. **Situation:** (1) At the printer; (2) a boring meeting.
3. **Main lesson:** *waste* as a verb (1) and a C noun (2: *a waste of*).
4. **Grammar used:** *Don't* + verb + U noun · *This is a* + noun + *of* + U noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** (1) **(You)** → **Don't waste** (V) → **paper** (O) · (2) **This** (S) → **is** → **a waste of time** (B).
6. **Subject:** (1) hidden *you* · (2) *This*.
7. **Verb:** (1) *waste* · (2) *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** (1) *Don't*.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *paper* · *a waste of time*.
10. **Word by word:** (1) *waste* after *Don't* → verb · (2) *a waste* — after *a* → noun.
11. **How the rules work together:** after *don't* → verb; after *a* → noun.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Please water the plants. / Plants need water* (EX-021-1049).
13. **Why this is correct:** each word in its position.
14. **Other versions:** *We waste a lot of food.* (verb) · *Food waste is a big problem.* (U noun)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Don't wasting paper.* → ✅ *Don't waste* · ❌ *This is waste of time.* → ✅ *a waste of time*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *What a waste of time!*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** "WAYST" (same as *waist*!).
19. **Pattern:** **Don't + verb + noun / This is a + noun + of + U noun**
20. **More examples:** *Don't waste money. / It's a waste of money.*
21. **Quick check:** In *a waste of time*, noun or verb? → **Answer:** noun.
