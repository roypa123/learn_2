**Short reminders:** **P** = proper noun — the name of **one particular** person, place, day, month, language… → **Capital letter**. **C** = common noun — a general word for a type of thing → small letter (unless it starts the sentence). Most names have **no *the*** (*Tokyo, Peru, Ana*). Some take *the*: rivers, seas, oceans, deserts, mountain ranges, *the USA / the UK*, plural family names (*the Garcias*) ([L-022 §7.4](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-022-common-and-proper-nouns.md#74-proper-nouns-and-the)).

> 🔁 **From L-021:** a noun is a naming word; it can be the subject (S), object (O), after *be* (B) or after a preposition (P) ([L-021](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-021-what-is-a-noun.md)). In this bank, **P** in step 10 means *proper noun*, not *preposition position*.

**New words in this part:** Hindi · continent · ocean · Eid · Ramadan · brand · Brazilian · Mount · cousin · neighbours · capital (city) · lake · desert.

## Step A — Common or proper? Short sentences

### EX-022-013 — "Ana is a student."

1. **Meaning:** The person called Ana studies at a school, college or university. 👩‍🎓
2. **Situation:** Introducing a new classmate.
3. **Main lesson:** A person's **name** (proper) and a **type of person** (common) in one sentence.
4. **Grammar used:** proper noun + *is a* + common noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Ana** (S) → **is** → **a student** (B).
6. **Subject:** *Ana* — a proper noun.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *a student* — says what Ana is.
10. **Word by word:** **Ana** — P: the name of one person → capital *A* · *is* · *a* — one · *student* — C: any person who studies → small *s*.
11. **How the rules work together:** a name needs a capital and no article; a common noun after *is* needs *a*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *My father is a pilot* (EX-021-003) — the same *is a* + job pattern.
13. **Why this is correct:** capital for the name, small letter + *a* for the common noun.
14. **Other versions:** *Ana and Ben are students.* · *Is Ana a student?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *ana is a student.* → ✅ *Ana* · ❌ *Ana is a Student.* → ✅ *student* (common noun, small)
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Ana's a student.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** capital *A* (it's a name **and** the first word).
18. **Pronunciation:** "AH-nuh" or "AN-uh".
19. **Pattern:** **Name + is a + common noun.**
20. **More examples:** *Tom is a doctor. · Mei is a teacher.*
21. **Quick check:** Which word is the proper noun? → **Answer:** *Ana*.

### EX-022-014 — "Tokyo is a big city."

1. **Meaning:** Tokyo is a large city. 🗼🏙️
2. **Situation:** A geography lesson about Japan.
3. **Main lesson:** A city's **name** (proper) vs the word **city** (common).
4. **Grammar used:** proper noun + *is a* + adjective + common noun.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Tokyo** (S) → **is** → **a big city** (B).
6. **Subject:** *Tokyo*.
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *a big city*.
10. **Word by word:** **Tokyo** — P: one particular city → capital, **no *the*** · *big* · *city* — C: any city → small.
11. **How the rules work together:** city names have no article; the common noun *city* needs *a*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Rome is a beautiful city* (EX-022-001).
13. **Why this is correct:** capital + no article for the name; *a* + small letter for the common noun.
14. **Other versions:** *Tokyo and Osaka are big cities.* · *Tokyo is the capital of Japan.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The Tokyo is a big city.* → ✅ *Tokyo* · ❌ *Tokyo is a big City.* → ✅ *city*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Tokyo's huge!*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** capital *T*; *city* small.
18. **Pronunciation:** "TOH-kee-oh".
19. **Pattern:** **City name + is a + adjective + city.**
20. **More examples:** *Lagos is a busy city. · Lima is an old city.*
21. **Quick check:** Common noun in the sentence? → **Answer:** *city*.

### EX-022-019 — "The baby's name is Noor."

1. **Meaning:** The baby is called Noor. 👶🏷️
2. **Situation:** Visiting a new baby.
3. **Main lesson:** common nouns (*baby, name*) + a proper noun (*Noor*) after *is*.
4. **Grammar used:** *The* + noun's + noun + *is* + name.
5. **Sentence structure:** **The baby's name** (S) → **is** → **Noor** (B).
6. **Subject:** *The baby's name* (head **name**).
7. **Verb:** *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *Noor*.
10. **Word by word:** *baby's* — C, with *'s* = belonging to the baby ([L-037](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-037-possessive-s.md) later) · *name* — C · **Noor** — P → capital.
11. **How the rules work together:** head *name* → *is*; the name itself has a capital.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *I have a cat. Her name is Luna* (EX-021-036).
13. **Why this is correct:** capital for the name only.
14. **Other versions:** *Her name is Noor.* · *The baby is called Noor.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *The Baby's Name is Noor.* → ✅ *baby's name* (common nouns small) · ❌ *…is noor.* → ✅ *Noor*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *She's called Noor.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** apostrophe in *baby's*.
18. **Pronunciation:** "NOOR".
19. **Pattern:** **The + noun's name is + Name.**
20. **More examples:** *The dog's name is Max.*
21. **Quick check:** How many proper nouns? → **Answer:** one (*Noor*).

1. **Meaning:** The mountain called Fuji is very pretty. 🗻
2. **Situation:** Looking at a photo of Japan.
3. **Main lesson:** a single mountain's name → **no *the***; *Mount* is part of the name → capital.

## Step B — Many kinds of proper nouns

### EX-022-023 — "Dr Patel works at City Hospital."

1. **Meaning:** The doctor called Patel works at the hospital named City Hospital. 👨‍⚕️🏥
2. **Situation:** Telling someone where your doctor works.
3. **Main lesson:** a **title + surname** and a **building name** — both proper.
4. **Grammar used:** title + surname + verb-s + *at* + name of a place.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Dr Patel** (S) → **works** (V) → **at City Hospital** (P).
6. **Subject:** *Dr Patel*.
7. **Verb:** *works*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *at City Hospital*.
10. **Word by word:** **Dr** — short for *Doctor* (title, capital) · **Patel** — surname · **City Hospital** — P: every word capital, no *the*.
11. **How the rules work together:** when *hospital* is part of a name → capital (*City Hospital*); alone → small (*the hospital*).
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *My teacher is Mrs Clark* (EX-022-002) · *Marta is a nurse at a big hospital* (EX-021-346).
13. **Why this is correct:** titles and names capitalised.
14. **Other versions:** *Dr Patel works at the hospital.* (common → small + *the*)
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *dr Patel works at city hospital.* → ✅ *Dr Patel … City Hospital* · ❌ *…at the City Hospital.* → ✅ *at City Hospital*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Dr Patel's at City Hospital.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** UK *Dr* (no full stop) / US *Dr.*
18. **Pronunciation:** "DOK-ter-puh-TEL".
19. **Pattern:** **Title + Surname + works at + Place Name.**
20. **More examples:** *Mrs Kim works at Park School.*
21. **Quick check:** Proper nouns? → **Answer:** *Dr Patel*, *City Hospital*.

11. **How the rules work together:** names with *States, Kingdom, Republic* or plural form → *the*.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *We need a bigger tent* (EX-021-980) — *big → bigger*.
13. **Why this is correct:** correct articles + comparative.
14. **Other versions:** *Is Russia bigger than China?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *…than USA?* → ✅ *than the USA* · ❌ *Is the Canada…* → ✅ *Canada*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *Is Canada bigger than the States?*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** *USA* — all capitals (letters).
18. **Pronunciation:** "yoo-es-AY".
19. **Pattern:** **Is + Country + comparative + than the + Country?**
20. **More examples:** *Is France bigger than the UK?*
21. **Quick check:** *USA* with or without *the*? → **Answer:** with *the*.

### EX-022-044 — "Paris isn't the capital of Italy. Rome is."

1. **Meaning:** The main city of Italy is not Paris; it's Rome. 🇮🇹🏛️
2. **Situation:** Correcting a mistake in a quiz.
3. **Main lesson:** three proper nouns + common noun *capital* (= main city).
4. **Grammar used:** city + *isn't the* + common noun + *of* + country · city + *is*.
5. **Sentence structure:** **Paris** (S) → **isn't** → **the capital of Italy** (B) · **Rome** (S) → **is** (short answer).
6. **Subject:** *Paris* · *Rome*.
7. **Verb:** *isn't* · *is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *the capital of Italy*.
10. **Word by word:** **Paris**, **Italy**, **Rome** — P · *capital* — C: the main city (also = capital letter!).
11. **How the rules work together:** *capital* here is a common noun → small.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *Rome is a beautiful city* (EX-022-001).
13. **Why this is correct:** negative + short positive.
14. **Other versions:** *Paris is the capital of France.*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *…the Capital of Italy.* → ✅ *capital* · ❌ *Rome is it.* → ✅ *Rome is.*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *No — the capital of Italy is Rome, not Paris.*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** two sentences.
18. **Pronunciation:** "PAR-is" · "ROHM".
19. **Pattern:** **City + isn't the capital of + Country. City + is.**
20. **More examples:** *Sydney isn't the capital of Australia. Canberra is.*
21. **Quick check:** Is *capital* (main city) common or proper? → **Answer:** common.

19. **Pattern:** **Where is + Lake + Name?**
20. **More examples:** *Where is Lake Geneva?*
21. **Quick check:** Lakes: with or without *the*? → **Answer:** without.

### EX-022-050 — "Is the Sahara in Africa?"

1. **Meaning:** Is the big desert called the Sahara on the continent of Africa? 🏜️❓
2. **Situation:** A geography lesson. (Answer: Yes.)
3. **Main lesson:** **deserts** take *the*; continents don't.
4. **Grammar used:** *Is the* + desert + *in* + continent?
5. **Sentence structure:** **Is** → **the Sahara** (S) → **in Africa** (P)?
6. **Subject:** *the Sahara*.
7. **Verb:** *Is*.
8. **Helping verbs:** none.
9. **Object / complement / extras:** *in Africa*.
10. **Word by word:** **the Sahara** — P + *the* (desert) · **Africa** — P, no *the*.
11. **How the rules work together:** deserts, rivers, oceans → *the*; continents, countries → usually not.
12. **Remember from before 🔁:** *The temperature in the desert at night is very low* (EX-021-1037) — common noun *desert*.
13. **Why this is correct:** correct articles.
14. **Other versions:** *The Gobi is in Asia.* · *Is the Amazon in Brazil?*
15. **Wrong ❌ → ✅:** ❌ *Is Sahara in Africa?* → ✅ *the Sahara* · ❌ *…in the Africa?* → ✅ *in Africa*
16. **Natural spoken English:** *The Sahara's in Africa, right?*
17. **Writing & punctuation:** **?**
18. **Pronunciation:** "suh-HAH-ruh".
19. **Pattern:** **Is the + Desert + in + Continent?**
20. **More examples:** *Is the Atacama in Chile?*
21. **Quick check:** Deserts: with or without *the*? → **Answer:** with *the*.
