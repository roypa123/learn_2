# Example Bank 021 — What Is a Noun? (EX-021-013 to EX-021-100)

**For lesson:** [L-021 — What Is a Noun?](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-021-what-is-a-noun.md) · Examples EX-021-001 to 012 are in the lesson (full template).

## How to use this bank

- Go **in order**. The examples get harder step by step (the 12-step ladder from [MASTER_PLAN §7](../../MASTER_PLAN.md)).
- For each example: **cover the "Nouns" line**, find the nouns yourself, then check.
- Every example is short-explained: **meaning**, **nouns** (with group and position), and a **note** (why, reminder, or common mistake).

**Groups:** 👩 person · 🐾 animal · 📍 place · 📦 thing · 💭 idea
**Positions:** **S** = subject · **O** = object · **B** = after *be* · **P** = after a preposition

> 🔁 **Remember the 4 tests:** (1) *a/the/my* before it · (2) plural -s · (3) adjective before it · (4) noun position.

## Step 1 — One noun, very short sentences (EX-021-013 to 022)

**EX-021-013** — *Dogs bark.*
- Meaning: Dogs make their loud short sound ("woof!").
- Nouns: **Dogs** 🐾 S
- Note: plural subject → verb with no -s (*bark*).

**EX-021-014** — *Water is life.*
- Meaning: We need water to live.
- Nouns: **Water** 📦 S · **life** 💭 B
- Note: both used in general → no *a/the* (like *Time is money*, EX-021-012).

**EX-021-015** — *The phone is ringing.*
- Meaning: The phone is making its sound now. 📱🔔
- Nouns: **phone** 📦 S
- Note: *is ringing* = helping verb + -ing (action now) ([L-012](../../00-foundations/04-what-is-a-sentence/lesson-012-the-verb.md)).

**EX-021-016** — *Children play.*
- Meaning: Young people have fun with games.
- Nouns: **Children** 👩 S
- Note: *children* = irregular plural of *child* (no -s) ([L-025](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-025-irregular-plurals.md)).

**EX-021-017** — *The sun is hot.*
- Meaning: The sun gives a lot of heat. ☀️
- Nouns: **sun** 📦 S
- Note: *the sun* — there is only one, so we use **the**.

**EX-021-018** — *Rain falls.*
- Meaning: Water drops come down from the sky. 🌧️
- Nouns: **Rain** 📦 S
- Note: *rain* can also be a verb: *It rains.* Here it's before the verb → noun.

**EX-021-019** — *Music helps.*
- Meaning: Listening to music makes things better (for example, when you are sad).
- Nouns: **Music** 💭/📦 S
- Note: you can hear music but not touch it. Still a noun (test 3: *good music* ✅).

**EX-021-020** — *The baby is asleep.*
- Meaning: The baby is sleeping now. 👶💤
- Nouns: **baby** 👩 S
- Note: *asleep* is an adjective (describes the baby), not a noun.

**EX-021-021** — *Teachers work hard.*
- Meaning: Teachers do a lot of work.
- Nouns: **Teachers** 👩 S
- Note: *hard* here describes *how* they work (adverb) — not a noun.

**EX-021-022** — *Breakfast is ready.*
- Meaning: The morning meal is prepared; you can eat now. 🍳
- Nouns: **Breakfast** 📦 S
- Note: meal names (*breakfast, lunch, dinner*) usually have no *a/the* in general use.

## Step 2 — Different nouns from all 5 groups (EX-021-023 to 032)

**EX-021-023** — *My uncle is a farmer.*
- Meaning: My mother's or father's brother works on a farm. 🧑‍🌾
- Nouns: **uncle** 👩 S · **farmer** 👩 B
- Note: jobs after *be* need **a/an** (EX-021-003).

**EX-021-024** — *The horse is in the field.*
- Meaning: The horse is standing in an area of grass. 🐎
- Nouns: **horse** 🐾 S · **field** 📍 P
- Note: *in the field* = preposition + noun (position P).

**EX-021-025** — *Paris is a beautiful city.*
- Meaning: Paris is a city, and it's very nice to see.
- Nouns: **Paris** 📍 S · **city** 📍 B
- Note: *Paris* is a name (proper noun, capital P); *city* is a general noun (common noun) ([L-022](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-022-common-and-proper-nouns.md)).

**EX-021-026** — *I need a pen.*
- Meaning: I must have something to write with. 🖊️
- Nouns: **pen** 📦 O
- Note: *I* is a pronoun, not a noun (it replaces a name).

**EX-021-027** — *Fear is normal.*
- Meaning: Feeling afraid is a usual, human thing.
- Nouns: **Fear** 💭 S
- Note: abstract noun; *fear* can also be a verb (*I fear spiders*).

**EX-021-028** — *The library has thousands of books.*
- Meaning: There are very many books in the library. 📚
- Nouns: **library** 📍 S · **thousands** (a number word used as a noun) · **books** 📦 P
- Note: *thousands of* = very many (1,000 = one thousand). *books* comes after the preposition *of*.

**EX-021-029** — *Bees make honey.*
- Meaning: Bees produce the sweet food honey. 🐝🍯
- Nouns: **Bees** 🐾 S · **honey** 📦 O
- Note: *honey* is uncountable (no *a*, no -s).

**EX-021-030** — *Our team has a new coach.*
- Meaning: Our sports group has a new trainer. ⚽
- Nouns: **team** 👩 (a group of people) S · **coach** 👩 O
- Note: *team* is a **collective noun** (one word for a group) ([L-091](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-091-collective-nouns.md)).

**EX-021-031** — *The party is on Saturday.*
- Meaning: The celebration will happen on Saturday. 🎉
- Nouns: **party** 💭 (an event) S · **Saturday** 💭 (a time) P
- Note: events and days are nouns too.

**EX-021-032** — *Friendship needs time.*
- Meaning: To have a good friendship, you must spend time together.
- Nouns: **Friendship** 💭 S · **time** 💭 O
- Note: *friend* (person) + **-ship** = *friendship* (idea). Word families ([L-007](../../00-foundations/03-words-and-meaning/lesson-007-what-is-a-word.md)).

## Step 3 — Nouns and the pronouns that replace them (EX-021-033 to 042)

> 🔁 A **pronoun** (*he, she, it, they*) replaces a noun so we don't repeat it ([L-011](../../00-foundations/04-what-is-a-sentence/lesson-011-the-subject.md)).

**EX-021-033** — *My brother is tall. He plays basketball.*
- Nouns: **brother** 👩 S · **basketball** 📦 O
- Note: **He** = *my brother*. He is a pronoun, not a noun.

**EX-021-034** — *The cake is delicious. It has chocolate in it.*
- Nouns: **cake** 📦 S · **chocolate** 📦 O
- Note: both *it*s = the cake.

**EX-021-035** — *Ana and Ben are students. They study music.*
- Nouns: **Ana**, **Ben** 👩 S · **students** 👩 B · **music** 💭 O
- Note: **They** = Ana and Ben.

**EX-021-036** — *I have a cat. Her name is Luna.*
- Nouns: **cat** 🐾 O · **name** 💭 S · **Luna** 🐾 B
- Note: **Her** (= the cat's) is a possessive determiner ([L-036](../../01-beginner-grammar/pronouns/lesson-036-possessive-determiners.md)). People often use *he/she* for pets.

**EX-021-037** — *The shoes are old. I want new ones.*
- Nouns: **shoes** 📦 S
- Note: **ones** replaces *shoes* (so we don't say *new shoes* again). A useful replacing word.

**EX-021-038** — *Mr Ali is my neighbour. I like him.*
- Nouns: **Mr Ali** 👩 S · **neighbour** 👩 B
- Note: **him** = Mr Ali, as an object pronoun ([L-013](../../00-foundations/05-basic-word-order/lesson-013-the-object.md)). US spelling: *neighbor*.

**EX-021-039** — *The bus is late again. It is always late!*
- Nouns: **bus** 📦 S
- Note: **It** = the bus. *always* = every time.

**EX-021-040** — *My parents live in Lima. I visit them every summer.*
- Nouns: **parents** 👩 S · **Lima** 📍 P · **summer** 💭 (time)
- Note: **them** = my parents (object).

**EX-021-041** — *This coffee is cold. Can I have a hot one?*
- Nouns: **coffee** 📦 S
- Note: **one** = a coffee (a cup of coffee). *a hot one* = a hot coffee.

**EX-021-042** — *The children are hungry. Give them some bread.*
- Nouns: **children** 👩 S · **bread** 📦 O
- Note: **them** = the children. *bread* is uncountable → *some bread*, not *a bread*.

## Step 4 — Nouns in questions and negatives (EX-021-043 to 052)

> 🔁 Questions: move *be* / helper, or add *do/does* ([L-015](../../00-foundations/05-basic-word-order/lesson-015-what-makes-a-question.md)). Negatives: *isn't, aren't, don't, doesn't* ([L-018](../../00-foundations/06-first-words/lesson-018-first-sentences-with-am-is-are.md), [L-017](../../00-foundations/06-first-words/lesson-017-greetings-and-classroom-english.md)).

**EX-021-043** — *Is your brother a teacher?*
- Nouns: **brother** 👩 S · **teacher** 👩 B
- Note: subject noun comes **after** *is* in the question.

**EX-021-044** — *The shop isn't open today.*
- Nouns: **shop** 📍 S · (*today* = time word)
- Note: *isn't* = is not.

**EX-021-045** — *Do your children like vegetables?*
- Nouns: **children** 👩 S · **vegetables** 📦 O
- Note: plural subject → **do** (not *does*).

**EX-021-046** — *My grandfather doesn't drink tea.*
- Nouns: **grandfather** 👩 S · **tea** 📦 O
- Note: *doesn't* + verb with no -s (*drink*).

**EX-021-047** — *Where is the post office?*
- Nouns: **post office** 📍 S
- Note: *post office* = **two words, one noun** (compound noun — [L-090](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-090-compound-nouns.md)).

**EX-021-048** — *Are the tickets expensive?*
- Nouns: **tickets** 📦 S
- Note: plural subject → **Are**. *expensive* = costs a lot of money.

**EX-021-049** — *We don't have milk.*
- Nouns: **milk** 📦 O
- Note: uncountable; no *a*. (Also: *We don't have **any** milk* — [L-069](../../01-beginner-grammar/articles-and-determiners/lesson-069-some-and-any.md).)

**EX-021-050** — *Does the hotel have a pool?*
- Nouns: **hotel** 📍 S · **pool** 📍 O
- Note: *hotel* = one → **Does**; *have* (no -s after *does*).

**EX-021-051** — *What is your favourite food?*
- Nouns: **food** 📦 S
- Note: *favourite* (US: *favorite*) = the one you like most; adjective before the noun.

**EX-021-052** — *My phone doesn't have a camera.*
- Nouns: **phone** 📦 S · **camera** 📦 O
- Note: object noun with *a* (one countable thing).

## Step 5 — Nouns with different times (EX-021-053 to 060)

> 🔁 Verbs show time; **nouns don't change** for time ([L-012](../../00-foundations/04-what-is-a-sentence/lesson-012-the-verb.md)). Past *was/were* and *-ed*, future *will*: first look only (full lessons later).

**EX-021-053** — *The weather was bad yesterday.*
- Nouns: **weather** 💭 S · (*yesterday* = time)
- Note: **was** = past of *is* ([L-061](../../01-beginner-grammar/verbs/lesson-061-was-were.md)). *weather* is uncountable.

**EX-021-054** — *The students will have a test tomorrow.*
- Nouns: **students** 👩 S · **test** 💭 O
- Note: **will have** = future. The nouns don't change.

**EX-021-055** — *My grandmother cooked rice and fish.*
- Nouns: **grandmother** 👩 S · **rice** 📦 O · **fish** 📦 O
- Note: *cooked* = past (-ed). Two object nouns joined by *and*.

**EX-021-056** — *Every day, the train is full.*
- Nouns: **day** 💭 · **train** 📦 S
- Note: time first + comma ([L-014](../../00-foundations/05-basic-word-order/lesson-014-why-word-order-matters.md)).

**EX-021-057** — *The concert was amazing!*
- Nouns: **concert** 💭 (event) S
- Note: *was* = past; *amazing* = very, very good.

**EX-021-058** — *Next year, my sister will start university.*
- Nouns: **year** 💭 · **sister** 👩 S · **university** 📍 O
- Note: *a university* — "y" sound → **a**, not *an* ([L-005](../../00-foundations/02-letters-and-sounds/lesson-005-vowels-and-consonants.md)).

**EX-021-059** — *The children played in the snow.*
- Nouns: **children** 👩 S · **snow** 📦 P
- Note: *played* = past; *in the snow* = P.

**EX-021-060** — *Our flight is at seven, but the taxi is late.*
- Nouns: **flight** 💭 S · **taxi** 📦 S
- Note: two subjects in two parts joined by *but*.

## Step 6 — Two topics: nouns + adjectives (EX-021-061 to 070)

> 🔁 Adjective **before** the noun; adjectives never take -s ([L-008](../../00-foundations/03-words-and-meaning/lesson-008-things-actions-describing-words.md)). The noun is the head.

**EX-021-061** — *She has a small house with a big garden.*
- Nouns: **house** 📍 O · **garden** 📍 P
- Adjectives: *small, big*.

**EX-021-062** — *Fresh bread smells wonderful.*
- Nouns: **bread** 📦 S
- Note: *fresh* (new, just made) describes *bread*; *wonderful* describes the smell.

**EX-021-063** — *I want a quiet room, please.*
- Nouns: **room** 📍 O
- Note: said at a hotel desk. *quiet* = not noisy.

**EX-021-064** — *These are expensive shoes.*
- Nouns: **shoes** 📦 B
- Note: ❌ *expensives shoes* — adjectives never take -s.

**EX-021-065** — *My new teacher is very kind.*
- Nouns: **teacher** 👩 S
- Note: *new* (before the noun) and *kind* (after *is*) are both adjectives.

**EX-021-066** — *We stayed in an old hotel near the sea.*
- Nouns: **hotel** 📍 P · **sea** 📍 P
- Note: **an** old — *old* starts with a vowel sound; *a/an* depends on the **next** word, not the noun ([L-005](../../00-foundations/02-letters-and-sounds/lesson-005-vowels-and-consonants.md)).

**EX-021-067** — *That was a difficult question.*
- Nouns: **question** 💭 B
- Note: *difficult* = hard, not easy.

**EX-021-068** — *Hot soup is good on cold days.*
- Nouns: **soup** 📦 S · **days** 💭 P
- Note: two adjective + noun pairs.

**EX-021-069** — *He gave me a long, boring book.*
- Nouns: **book** 📦 O
- Note: two adjectives can go before one noun, often with a comma between them.

**EX-021-070** — *The red apples are sweet, but the green apples are sour.*
- Nouns: **apples** 📦 S (×2)
- Note: *sour* = with a sharp taste, like a lemon 🍋.

## Step 7 — Three topics: nouns + adjectives + prepositions (EX-021-071 to 078)

**EX-021-071** — *There is a black cat on the wall.*
- Nouns: **cat** 🐾 · **wall** 📦 P
- Note: *There is* = a cat exists in that place ([L-041](../../01-beginner-grammar/basic-sentences/lesson-041-there-is-there-are.md)).

**EX-021-072** — *My keys are in the small pocket of my bag.*
- Nouns: **keys** 📦 S · **pocket** 📦 P · **bag** 📦 P
- Note: two prepositions: *in* + *of*.

**EX-021-073** — *The children sleep in a big room at the back of the house.*
- Nouns: **children** S · **room** P · **back** P · **house** P
- Note: *the back of the house* = the rear part.

**EX-021-074** — *We had lunch at a nice café by the river.*
- Nouns: **lunch** O · **café** P · **river** P
- Note: *by* = next to.

**EX-021-075** — *She works for a large company in the city centre.*
- Nouns: **company** P · **city centre** P
- Note: *city centre* (US *downtown*) = compound noun.

**EX-021-076** — *The photo on the wall is from our holiday in Greece.*
- Nouns: **photo** S · **wall** P · **holiday** P · **Greece** P
- Note: head noun of the subject = **photo** → **is**.

**EX-021-077** — *I bought a warm coat for the winter.*
- Nouns: **coat** O · **winter** P
- Note: *bought* = past of *buy* (irregular).

**EX-021-078** — *Put the dirty cups in the sink, please.*
- Nouns: **cups** O · **sink** P
- Note: an instruction (hidden *you*). *sink* = the kitchen basin for washing.

## Step 8 — Long sentences and noun phrases (EX-021-079 to 086)

> 🔁 Find the **head noun** of each noun phrase. The verb agrees with the head of the subject.

**EX-021-079** — *The woman in the blue dress is my aunt.*
- Subject noun phrase: *The **woman** in the blue dress* → head **woman** → **is**.
- Other nouns: **dress** P · **aunt** B.

**EX-021-080** — *A cup of tea and a piece of cake cost five pounds.*
- Subject: *a **cup** of tea* + *a **piece** of cake* (two heads, joined by *and*) → plural verb **cost**. (People also say *costs* if they think of it as one order — both are heard.)
- Nouns: cup, tea, piece, cake, pounds.

**EX-021-081** — *The boxes in the kitchen are full of old books.*
- Head: **boxes** → **are** (*kitchen* is not the head).
- Nouns: boxes, kitchen, books.

**EX-021-082** — *The list of names is on the teacher's desk.*
- Head: **list** (singular) → **is**. ❌ *are* (don't follow *names*).
- Nouns: list, names, teacher's (possessive — [L-037](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-037-possessive-s.md)), desk.

**EX-021-083** — *My best friend from primary school now lives in Canada with her husband and two children.*
- Head of subject: **friend** → **lives**.
- Nouns: friend, school, Canada, husband, children.
- Note: *primary school* = school for young children (ages ~5–11).

**EX-021-084** — *The noise from the street outside my window keeps me awake at night.*
- Head: **noise** → **keeps**.
- Nouns: noise, street, window, night.
- Note: *keeps me awake* = doesn't let me sleep.

**EX-021-085** — *A bottle of water, a sandwich and an apple are in your lunch box.*
- Three head nouns → **are**.
- Nouns: bottle, water, sandwich, apple, lunch box.

**EX-021-086** — *The price of fruit and vegetables at this market is very low.*
- Head: **price** → **is**.
- Nouns: price, fruit, vegetables, market.

## Step 9 — Nouns in short conversations (EX-021-087 to 090)

**EX-021-087**
> **A:** What's your **job**?
> **B:** I'm a **nurse**. I work in a **hospital**.
- Nouns: job (💭 S), nurse (👩 B), hospital (📍 P).
- Note: *What's your job?* = *What do you do?* ([D-001](../../06-spoken-english/daily-conversations/dialogue-001-meeting-someone-new.md)).

**EX-021-088**
> **A:** Can I have a **coffee**, please?
> **B:** Sure. With **milk** and **sugar**?
> **A:** Just **milk**, thanks.
- Nouns: coffee, milk, sugar.
- Note: *a coffee* = **a cup of** coffee (common in cafés). *milk, sugar* — no *a*.

**EX-021-089**
> **A:** Where's the **bathroom**?
> **B:** It's next to the **kitchen**, on the **left**.
- Nouns: bathroom (S), kitchen (P), left (P).
- Note: *left* here is a noun (a direction: *on the left*).

**EX-021-090**
> **A:** What's in the **box**?
> **B:** A **present** for my **sister**. It's her **birthday** tomorrow.
- Nouns: box, present (= gift 🎁), sister, birthday.
- Note: *A present for my sister.* — a short answer without a verb is normal in speech ([L-009](../../00-foundations/04-what-is-a-sentence/lesson-009-what-is-a-sentence.md)).

## Step 10 — Nouns in a short story (EX-021-091 to 093)

> *(1) Sam is a **baker**. (2) Every **morning**, he makes **bread**, **cakes** and **cookies** in his small **shop**. (3) The **smell** of fresh **bread** brings many **customers** to his **door**.*

**EX-021-091** — Sentence (1)
- Nouns: **Sam** 👩 S · **baker** 👩 B
- Note: *baker* = a person who makes bread. *bake* (verb) + **-er** = person (word family).

**EX-021-092** — Sentence (2)
- Nouns: **morning** 💭 (time) · **bread**, **cakes**, **cookies** 📦 O · **shop** 📍 P
- Note: *bread* (uncountable) vs *cakes, cookies* (countable, plural -s).

**EX-021-093** — Sentence (3)
- Head of subject: **smell** → **brings** (not *bring*).
- Nouns: smell 💭, bread 📦, customers 👩 (people who buy), door 📦.

---

## Step 11 — Nouns in real writing: a short message (EX-021-094 to 096)

> *Hi Jo! Thanks for the **flowers**. They are beautiful! Can you come for **dinner** on **Friday**? Bring your **sister** too. See you, **Mia***

**EX-021-094** — *Thanks for the flowers.*
- Nouns: **flowers** 📦 P
- Note: *Thanks for* + noun ✅ (EX-021-008).

**EX-021-095** — *Can you come for dinner on Friday?*
- Nouns: **dinner** 📦 P · **Friday** 💭 P
- Note: *come for dinner* = come to eat dinner. *on* + day ([L-050](../../01-beginner-grammar/prepositions/lesson-050-prepositions-of-time-at-on-in.md)).

**EX-021-096** — *Bring your sister too.*
- Nouns: **sister** 👩 O
- Note: instruction; *too* = also. Names (*Jo, Mia*) are also nouns.

---

## Step 12 — Noun or verb? Telling similar words apart (EX-021-097 to 100)

**EX-021-097** — *I **email** my boss every Monday. / I got an **email** from my boss.*
- 1st *email* = **verb** (after *I*). 2nd *email* = **noun** (after *an*).
- Other nouns: boss, Monday.

**EX-021-098** — *We **walk** to work. / Let's go for a **walk**.*
- 1st *walk* = **verb**. 2nd *walk* = **noun** (after *a*).
- Note: *work* (after *to*) is a noun in sentence 1.

**EX-021-099** — *Please **answer** the question. / I don't know the **answer**.*
- 1st *answer* = **verb** (instruction). 2nd = **noun** (after *the*).
- *question* = noun (O).

**EX-021-100** — *The **dream** was strange. / I **dream** about flying.*
- 1st *dream* = **noun** (after *The*, subject). 2nd = **verb** (after *I*).
- Note: *about flying* — *flying* here is an **-ing** form used like a noun (a gerund — later: [L-109](../../02-intermediate-grammar/gerunds-and-infinitives/lesson-109-verb-plus-ing.md)). Advanced, but notice it!
