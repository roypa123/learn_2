# Example Bank 022 — Common and Proper Nouns (EX-022-013 to EX-022-100)

**For lesson:** [L-022 — Common Nouns and Proper Nouns](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-022-common-and-proper-nouns.md) · EX-022-001 to 012 are in the lesson.

## How to use this bank

- Go in order (easy → hard).
- **Cover the explanation**, then: (1) find the **proper nouns** (P), (2) find the **common nouns** (C), (3) check capitals and *the*.
- **Key:** **P** = proper noun (capital) · **C** = common noun (small)

> 🔁 **Remember:** one particular name → **Capital**. General word → small. Most names: no *the*. Rivers, seas, ranges, *the USA/UK*, famous buildings, *the + family-s*: **the** ([L-022 §7.4](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-022-common-and-proper-nouns.md#74-proper-nouns-and-the)).

## Step 1 — Common or proper? Short sentences (EX-022-013 to 022)

**EX-022-013** — *Ana is a student.*
- P: **Ana** · C: *student*
- Note: a person's name vs a general word for a type of person.

**EX-022-014** — *Tokyo is a big city.*
- P: **Tokyo** · C: *city*
- Note: no *the* before *Tokyo*.

**EX-022-015** — *Rex is my dog.*
- P: **Rex** (a pet's name) · C: *dog*
- Note: pets' names are proper nouns.

**EX-022-016** — *Today is Friday.*
- P: **Friday** · C: — (*today* = time word)
- Note: days → capital.

**EX-022-017** — *I speak Hindi.*
- P: **Hindi**
- Note: languages → capital.

**EX-022-018** — *Peru is a country.*
- P: **Peru** · C: *country*

**EX-022-019** — *The baby's name is Noor.*
- P: **Noor** · C: *baby, name*
- Note: *baby's* = belonging to the baby ([L-037](../../01-beginner-grammar/nouns/lesson-037-possessive-s.md)).

**EX-022-020** — *October is cold.*
- P: **October**
- Note: months → capital.

**EX-022-021** — *My friend is Brazilian.*
- P: **Brazilian** (nationality) · C: *friend*
- Note: nationality words → capital, even as describing words.

**EX-022-022** — *Mount Fuji is beautiful.*
- P: **Mount Fuji**
- Note: a single mountain → **no** *the*. *Mount* is part of the name → capital.

## Step 2 — Many kinds of proper nouns (EX-022-023 to 032)

**EX-022-023** — *Dr Patel works at City Hospital.*
- P: **Dr Patel**, **City Hospital**
- Note: *Dr* = Doctor (title). Hospital name → each word capital.

**EX-022-024** — *We celebrate Eid with our family.*
- P: **Eid** (a holiday) · C: *family*
- Note: holidays → capital: *Eid, Christmas, Diwali, New Year*.

**EX-022-025** — *My phone is a Samsung.*
- P: **Samsung** (brand) · C: *phone*
- Note: *a Samsung* = a phone made by Samsung.

**EX-022-026** — *She lives on Elm Road.*
- P: **Elm Road** · Note: road/street names → capitals; no *the*.

**EX-022-027** — *Australia is a continent and a country.*
- P: **Australia** · C: *continent, country*

**EX-022-028** — *The Pacific is the biggest ocean.*
- P: **the Pacific** · C: *ocean*
- Note: oceans take **the**.

**EX-022-029** — *My sister studies at Cairo University.*
- P: **Cairo University** · C: *sister*

**EX-022-030** — *We watched Frozen last night.*
- P: **Frozen** (film title) · C: *night*

**EX-022-031** — *Ramadan is in March this year.*
- P: **Ramadan**, **March** · C: *year*
- Note: the dates of Ramadan change each year.

**EX-022-032** — *I love Italian food.*
- P: **Italian** (nationality adjective) · C: *food*
- Note: ❌ *italian food*. Adjectives from country names keep the capital.

## Step 3 — Proper nouns and pronouns (EX-022-033 to 042)

> 🔁 After we say a name once, we often use a pronoun: *he, she, it, they* ([L-011](../../00-foundations/04-what-is-a-sentence/lesson-011-the-subject.md)).

**EX-022-033** — *Omar is my cousin. He lives in Jordan.*
- P: **Omar**, **Jordan** · C: *cousin*
- Note: **He** = Omar.

**EX-022-034** — *Lisbon is in Portugal. It is near the sea.*
- P: **Lisbon**, **Portugal** · C: *sea*
- Note: **It** = Lisbon.

**EX-022-035** — *The Garcias are our neighbours. They have three dogs.*
- P: **the Garcias** (family) · C: *neighbours, dogs*
- Note: **They** = the Garcia family.

**EX-022-036** — *I like Mrs Wong. She is a great teacher.*
- P: **Mrs Wong** · C: *teacher*
- Note: **She** = Mrs Wong.

**EX-022-037** — *Luna is our cat. She sleeps all day.*
- P: **Luna** · C: *cat, day*

**EX-022-038** — *We use Zoom for meetings. It is easy.*
- P: **Zoom** (app/company) · C: *meetings*

**EX-022-039** — *Toni and Marco are from Naples. They are brothers.*
- P: **Toni**, **Marco**, **Naples** · C: *brothers*

**EX-022-040** — *The Amazon is in South America. It is very long.*
- P: **the Amazon**, **South America** · Note: **It** = the river.

**EX-022-041** — *My best friend is Yuki. I call her every Sunday.*
- P: **Yuki**, **Sunday** · C: *friend* · Note: **her** = Yuki (object).

**EX-022-042** — *Spanish is a beautiful language. Millions of people speak it.*
- P: **Spanish** · C: *language, people* · Note: **it** = Spanish.

## Step 4 — Questions and negatives (EX-022-043 to 052)

**EX-022-043** — *Is Canada bigger than the USA?*
- P: **Canada**, **the USA**
- Note: *Canada* (no *the*) vs *the USA* (with *the*) in the same sentence!

**EX-022-044** — *Paris isn't the capital of Italy. Rome is.*
- P: **Paris**, **Italy**, **Rome** · C: *capital* (= the main city of a country)

**EX-022-045** — *Do you know Dr Brown?*
- P: **Dr Brown**

**EX-022-046** — *We don't have school on Saturday.*
- P: **Saturday** · C: *school*

**EX-022-047** — *Where is Lake Victoria?*
- P: **Lake Victoria** · Note: lakes → **no** *the*.

**EX-022-048** — *Does your brother speak German?*
- P: **German** · C: *brother*

**EX-022-049** — *My birthday isn't in June. It's in July.*
- P: **June**, **July** · C: *birthday*

**EX-022-050** — *Is the Sahara in Africa?*
- P: **the Sahara**, **Africa** · Note: deserts usually take **the**.

**EX-022-051** — *They don't live in London. They live in Manchester.*
- P: **London**, **Manchester**

**EX-022-052** — *Which language do people speak in Brazil?*
- P: **Brazil** · C: *language, people* · Answer: *Portuguese* (P).

## Step 5 — Different times (EX-022-053 to 060)

**EX-022-053** — *Last Christmas, we visited my grandparents in Scotland.*
- P: **Christmas**, **Scotland** · C: *grandparents*
- Note: *visited* = past (-ed).

**EX-022-054** — *Next Monday, Mr Lee will start his new job.*
- P: **Monday**, **Mr Lee** · C: *job* · Note: *will start* = future.

**EX-022-055** — *Shakespeare was a famous English writer.*
- P: **Shakespeare**, **English** · C: *writer* · Note: *was* = past.

**EX-022-056** — *In 2010, my family moved to Toronto.*
- P: **Toronto** · C: *family* · Note: year numbers are written as numerals.

**EX-022-057** — *The meeting will be on Thursday at Green Hotel.*
- P: **Thursday**, **Green Hotel** · C: *meeting*

**EX-022-058** — *My grandmother was born in India in May 1950.*
- P: **India**, **May** · C: *grandmother*

**EX-022-059** — *We went to the Louvre in Paris last summer.*
- P: **the Louvre** (a museum), **Paris** · C: *summer* (small)
- Note: *went* = past of *go*.

**EX-022-060** — *Tomorrow is New Year's Day.*
- P: **New Year's Day** · Note: all important words capital.

## Step 6 — Two topics: proper nouns + adjectives (EX-022-061 to 070)

**EX-022-061** — *Venice is a small, beautiful city in Italy.*
- P: **Venice**, **Italy** · C: *city* · Adjectives: *small, beautiful*.

**EX-022-062** — *I love Japanese films and Korean music.*
- P: **Japanese**, **Korean** (adjectives from proper nouns) · C: *films, music*

**EX-022-063** — *Ali has an old Toyota.*
- P: **Ali**, **Toyota** · Note: **an** old — *old* starts with a vowel sound.

**EX-022-064** — *Busy Oxford Street is full of shops.*
- P: **Oxford Street** · C: *shops* · Note: adjective *busy* before a proper noun — possible, a little literary.

**EX-022-065** — *Our new teacher, Ms Diaz, is from Mexico.*
- P: **Ms Diaz**, **Mexico** · C: *teacher*
- Note: commas around *Ms Diaz* — it gives the name of the teacher (extra information).

**EX-022-066** — *The Thai restaurant on Hill Street is very good.*
- P: **Thai**, **Hill Street** · C: *restaurant*

**EX-022-067** — *Cold Canadian winters are long.*
- P: **Canadian** · C: *winters* (small — a season)

**EX-022-068** — *Little Sami is three years old.*
- P: **Sami** · Note: *little* before a name = young/small (friendly).

**EX-022-069** — *The French language has many silent letters.*
- P: **French** · C: *language, letters*
- Note: silent letters! ([L-006](../../00-foundations/02-letters-and-sounds/lesson-006-letters-and-sounds.md))

**EX-022-070** — *My favourite city is sunny Barcelona.*
- P: **Barcelona** · C: *city*

## Step 7 — Three topics: names + *the* / no *the* + prepositions (EX-022-071 to 078)

**EX-022-071** — *We swam in the Mediterranean Sea near Nice.*
- P: **the Mediterranean Sea** (*the* — a sea), **Nice** (city, no *the*)
- Note: ⚠️ *Nice* (a city in France) vs *nice* (adjective = pleasant).

**EX-022-072** — *They climbed in the Alps in Switzerland.*
- P: **the Alps** (range → *the*), **Switzerland** (no *the*)

**EX-022-073** — *My uncle lives in the Netherlands, in Amsterdam.*
- P: **the Netherlands** (plural-looking name → *the*), **Amsterdam**

**EX-022-074** — *The bus goes from Central Station to the airport.*
- P: **Central Station** · C: *bus, airport*
- Note: *the airport* (no name → common noun + *the*). With a name: *Heathrow Airport* (no *the*).

**EX-022-075** — *I work for the United Nations in Geneva.*
- P: **the United Nations**, **Geneva**

**EX-022-076** — *She studied at Harvard and then moved to the UK.*
- P: **Harvard**, **the UK**

**EX-022-077** — *We took photos of the Eiffel Tower from the River Seine.*
- P: **the Eiffel Tower**, **the River Seine** · C: *photos*

**EX-022-078** — *The Philippines has more than 7,000 islands in the Pacific Ocean.*
- P: **the Philippines**, **the Pacific Ocean** · C: *islands*
- Note: *the Philippines* is one country → **has** (singular verb).

## Step 8 — Long sentences with many names (EX-022-079 to 086)

**EX-022-079** — *Every August, my aunt Rosa drives from Madrid to the south of Spain with her two children.*
- P: **August**, **Rosa**, **Madrid**, **Spain** · C: *aunt, south, children*
- Note: *the south* — a direction → small. (*South Africa* — part of a name → capital.)

**EX-022-080** — *Dr Okafor, the new doctor at Lagos General Hospital, speaks English, Yoruba and French.*
- P: **Dr Okafor**, **Lagos General Hospital**, **English**, **Yoruba**, **French** · C: *doctor*

**EX-022-081** — *My favourite book, The Little Prince, was written by Antoine de Saint-Exupéry.*
- P: **The Little Prince** (title), **Antoine de Saint-Exupéry** · C: *book*
- Note: *de* stays small in some names. *was written by* = passive (later — [L-164](../../02-intermediate-grammar/passive-voice/lesson-164-introduction-to-passive.md)).

**EX-022-082** — *On Tuesday, the Kims flew from Seoul to New York on Korean Air.*
- P: **Tuesday**, **the Kims**, **Seoul**, **New York**, **Korean Air**

**EX-022-083** — *The students in Mr Hassan's class are reading Romeo and Juliet this term.*
- P: **Mr Hassan**, **Romeo and Juliet** · C: *students, class, term*
- Note: *and* is small inside the title.

**EX-022-084** — *Our hotel in Rome was near the Colosseum, so we walked there on our first morning.*
- P: **Rome**, **the Colosseum** · C: *hotel, morning*

**EX-022-085** — *In winter, many people from Northern Europe travel to the Canary Islands for the sun.*
- P: **Northern Europe**, **the Canary Islands** · C: *winter, people, sun*
- Note: *Northern* is capital here because *Northern Europe* is a region name.

**EX-022-086** — *My cousin Leila works for a small company in Dubai, but her husband works for Emirates.*
- P: **Leila**, **Dubai**, **Emirates** (an airline) · C: *cousin, company, husband*

## Step 9 — Conversations (EX-022-087 to 090)

**EX-022-087**
> **A:** Where are you from?
> **B:** **Kenya**. From **Nairobi**.
> **A:** Oh! Do you speak **Swahili**?
> **B:** Yes, and **English**.
- P: Kenya, Nairobi, Swahili, English.

**EX-022-088**
> **A:** When is your exam?
> **B:** On **Wednesday**, the 5th of **June**.
- P: Wednesday, June · C: *exam*
- Note: dates: *the 5th of June* (UK) / *June 5th* (US) ([L-092](../../01-beginner-grammar/basic-sentences/lesson-092-numbers-ordinals-dates-time.md)).

**EX-022-089**
> **A:** What's your dog's name?
> **B:** **Biscuit**!
> **A:** Ha ha, that's a funny name for a dog.
- P: **Biscuit** (the dog's name) · C: *dog, name*
- Note: *biscuit* (small) = a sweet dry food 🍪. As a pet's name → **B**iscuit.

**EX-022-090**
> **A:** Excuse me, is this **Park Lane**?
> **B:** No, this is **Hill Road**. **Park Lane** is the next street on the left.
- P: Park Lane, Hill Road · C: *street, left*

## Step 10 — In a short story (EX-022-091 to 093)

> *(1) **Ravi** is a cook from **Mumbai**. (2) Every **Friday**, he cooks **Indian** food at **Spice House**, a small restaurant on **Market Street**. (3) In **December**, people come from **London** and **Bristol** to eat his famous curry.*

**EX-022-091** — Sentence (1): P **Ravi**, **Mumbai** · C *cook*.
**EX-022-092** — Sentence (2): P **Friday**, **Indian**, **Spice House**, **Market Street** · C *food, restaurant*. Note: *a small restaurant on Market Street* explains what *Spice House* is.
**EX-022-093** — Sentence (3): P **December**, **London**, **Bristol** · C *people, curry*.

## Step 11 — Real writing: an email (EX-022-094 to 096)

> *Dear **Mr Evans**,*
> *My name is **Sara Lopez**. I am a student at **Westfield College**. I would like to visit your office on **Monday**, 3 **March**.*
> *Best wishes,*
> ***Sara***

**EX-022-094** — *Dear Mr Evans,*
- P: **Mr Evans** · Note: formal emails start with *Dear* + title + family name + comma ([L-017](../../00-foundations/06-first-words/lesson-017-greetings-and-classroom-english.md)).

**EX-022-095** — *I am a student at Westfield College.*
- P: **Westfield College** · C: *student* · Note: no *the* before the college name.

**EX-022-096** — *I would like to visit your office on Monday, 3 March.*
- P: **Monday**, **March** · C: *office*
- Note: *would like to* = a polite *want to* ([L-082](../../01-beginner-grammar/verbs/lesson-082-want-to-and-would-like.md)).

## Step 12 — Same word, common or proper? (EX-022-097 to 100)

**EX-022-097** — *I eat an **apple** every day. / My laptop is an **Apple**.*
- *apple* = fruit (C). *Apple* = the company/brand (P).

**EX-022-098** — ***May** I come in? / My exam is in **May**.*
- 1st *May* = helping verb (asking permission) — capital only because it's the first word. 2nd *May* = the month (P).

**EX-022-099** — *We bought new **china** for the kitchen. / My friend is from **China**.*
- *china* (C) = plates and cups made of fine white clay. *China* (P) = the country.

**EX-022-100** — *We eat **turkey** at Thanksgiving. / **Turkey** is between Europe and Asia.*
- *turkey* (C) = a large bird / its meat 🦃. *Turkey* (P) = the country (now also officially *Türkiye*).
- *Thanksgiving* (P) = a holiday in the USA and Canada.
