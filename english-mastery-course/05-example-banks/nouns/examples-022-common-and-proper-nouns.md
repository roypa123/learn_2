### Old draft step 4 — Questions and negatives (EX-022-043 to 052)

**EX-022-043** — *Is Canada bigger than the USA?*
- P: **Canada**, **the USA**
- Note: *Canada* (no *the*) vs *the USA* (with *the*) in the same sentence!

**EX-022-044** — *Paris isn't the capital of Italy. Rome is.*
- P: **Paris**, **Italy**, **Rome** · C: *capital* (= the main city of a country)

**EX-022-045** — *Do you know Dr Brown?*
- P: **Dr Brown**

**EX-022-046** — *We don't have school on Saturday.*
- P: **Saturday** · C: *school*

**EX-022-047** — *Where is Lake Victoria?*
- P: **Lake Victoria** · Note: lakes → **no** *the*.

**EX-022-048** — *Does your brother speak German?*
- P: **German** · C: *brother*

**EX-022-049** — *My birthday isn't in June. It's in July.*
- P: **June**, **July** · C: *birthday*

**EX-022-050** — *Is the Sahara in Africa?*
- P: **the Sahara**, **Africa** · Note: deserts usually take **the**.

**EX-022-051** — *They don't live in London. They live in Manchester.*
- P: **London**, **Manchester**

**EX-022-052** — *Which language do people speak in Brazil?*
- P: **Brazil** · C: *language, people* · Answer: *Portuguese* (P).

### Old draft step 6 — Two topics: proper nouns + adjectives (EX-022-061 to 070)

**EX-022-061** — *Venice is a small, beautiful city in Italy.*
- P: **Venice**, **Italy** · C: *city* · Adjectives: *small, beautiful*.

**EX-022-062** — *I love Japanese films and Korean music.*
- P: **Japanese**, **Korean** (adjectives from proper nouns) · C: *films, music*

**EX-022-063** — *Ali has an old Toyota.*

**EX-022-064** — *Busy Oxford Street is full of shops.*

**EX-022-065** — *Our new teacher, Ms Diaz, is from Mexico.*

- Note: commas around *Ms Diaz* — it gives the name of the teacher (extra information).

- P: **Thai**, **Hill Street** · C: *restaurant*

**EX-022-067** — *Cold Canadian winters are long.*
- P: **Canadian** · C: *winters* (small — a season)

**EX-022-068** — *Little Sami is three years old.*

**EX-022-069** — *The French language has many silent letters.*

### Old draft step 7 — Three topics: names + *the* / no *the* + prepositions (EX-022-071 to 078)

**EX-022-071** — *We swam in the Mediterranean Sea near Nice.*
- P: **the Mediterranean Sea** (*the* — a sea), **Nice** (city, no *the*)
- Note: ⚠️ *Nice* (a city in France) vs *nice* (adjective = pleasant).

**EX-022-072** — *They climbed in the Alps in Switzerland.*
- P: **the Alps** (range → *the*), **Switzerland** (no *the*)

**EX-022-073** — *My uncle lives in the Netherlands, in Amsterdam.*
- P: **the Netherlands** (plural-looking name → *the*), **Amsterdam**

**EX-022-074** — *The bus goes from Central Station to the airport.*
- P: **Central Station** · C: *bus, airport*
- Note: *the airport* (no name → common noun + *the*). With a name: *Heathrow Airport* (no *the*).

**EX-022-075** — *I work for the United Nations in Geneva.*
- P: **the United Nations**, **Geneva**

**EX-022-076** — *She studied at Harvard and then moved to the UK.*
- P: **Harvard**, **the UK**

**EX-022-077** — *We took photos of the Eiffel Tower from the River Seine.*
- P: **the Eiffel Tower**, **the River Seine** · C: *photos*

**EX-022-078** — *The Philippines has more than 7,000 islands in the Pacific Ocean.*
- P: **the Philippines**, **the Pacific Ocean** · C: *islands*
- Note: *the Philippines* is one country → **has** (singular verb).

### Old draft step 8 — Long sentences with many names (EX-022-079 to 086)

**EX-022-079** — *Every August, my aunt Rosa drives from Madrid to the south of Spain with her two children.*
- P: **August**, **Rosa**, **Madrid**, **Spain** · C: *aunt, south, children*
- Note: *the south* — a direction → small. (*South Africa* — part of a name → capital.)

**EX-022-080** — *Dr Okafor, the new doctor at Lagos General Hospital, speaks English, Yoruba and French.*
- P: **Dr Okafor**, **Lagos General Hospital**, **English**, **Yoruba**, **French** · C: *doctor*

**EX-022-081** — *My favourite book, The Little Prince, was written by Antoine de Saint-Exupéry.*
- P: **The Little Prince** (title), **Antoine de Saint-Exupéry** · C: *book*
- Note: *de* stays small in some names. *was written by* = passive (later — [L-164](../../02-intermediate-grammar/passive-voice/lesson-164-introduction-to-passive.md)).

**EX-022-082** — *On Tuesday, the Kims flew from Seoul to New York on Korean Air.*
- P: **Tuesday**, **the Kims**, **Seoul**, **New York**, **Korean Air**

**EX-022-083** — *The students in Mr Hassan's class are reading Romeo and Juliet this term.*
- P: **Mr Hassan**, **Romeo and Juliet** · C: *students, class, term*
- Note: *and* is small inside the title.

**EX-022-084** — *Our hotel in Rome was near the Colosseum, so we walked there on our first morning.*
- P: **Rome**, **the Colosseum** · C: *hotel, morning*

**EX-022-085** — *In winter, many people from Northern Europe travel to the Canary Islands for the sun.*
- P: **Northern Europe**, **the Canary Islands** · C: *winter, people, sun*
- Note: *Northern* is capital here because *Northern Europe* is a region name.

**EX-022-086** — *My cousin Leila works for a small company in Dubai, but her husband works for Emirates.*
- P: **Leila**, **Dubai**, **Emirates** (an airline) · C: *cousin, company, husband*

### Old draft step 12 — Same word, common or proper? (EX-022-097 to 100)

**EX-022-097** — *I eat an **apple** every day. / My laptop is an **Apple**.*
- *apple* = fruit (C). *Apple* = the company/brand (P).

**EX-022-098** — ***May** I come in? / My exam is in **May**.*
- 1st *May* = helping verb (asking permission) — capital only because it's the first word. 2nd *May* = the month (P).

**EX-022-099** — *We bought new **china** for the kitchen. / My friend is from **China**.*
- *china* (C) = plates and cups made of fine white clay. *China* (P) = the country.

**EX-022-100** — *We eat **turkey** at Thanksgiving. / **Turkey** is between Europe and Asia.*
- *turkey* (C) = a large bird / its meat 🦃. *Turkey* (P) = the country (now also officially *Türkiye*).
- *Thanksgiving* (P) = a holiday in the USA and Canada.
