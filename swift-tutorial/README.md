# Learn Swift, SwiftUI and Xcode — From Zero

Welcome! 👋

This tutorial teaches you how to make **iPhone apps**.
You do **not** need to know anything before you start.
We go **slowly**, **step by step**, with **small examples**.

---

## Who is this for?

- People who have **never written code** before.
- People who **do not have a Mac** (yet).
- People whose **English is not perfect**. We use short and simple sentences.

---

## What do the signs mean?

At the top of every chapter you will see one of these signs:

| Sign | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Works in browser** | You can do this chapter on **any computer** (Windows is fine). Just open a website. |
| 🍎 **Needs a Mac** | You need a Mac (real, rented in the cloud, or an iPad with Swift Playgrounds). |

Inside chapters you will also see:

| Sign | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 💡 **Tip** | A helpful idea. |
| ⚠️ **Careful** | A common mistake. Read this! |
| 🧠 **Remember** | The most important thing in this section. |
| 🏋️ **Practice** | A small task for you to try. |
| 📖 **New word** | A word you may not know, explained simply. |

---

## How to use this tutorial

1. Read the chapters **in order**. Every chapter uses things from the chapters before it.
2. **Type** every example yourself. Do not copy and paste. Typing helps your brain remember.
3. **Run** every example and look at the result.
4. Do every 🏋️ **Practice** task.
5. If you do not understand something, read it again slowly. That is normal! Everyone does this.
