# Chapter 01 — What Is Programming?

✅ **Works in browser** — no install needed.

---

## What you will learn

- What a computer program is.
- What programming (coding) means.
- Why computers need **exact** instructions.

---

## 1. A computer is very fast, but not smart

A computer can do **millions** of things in one second.

But a computer **does not think**.
It only does **exactly** what you tell it.
Nothing more. Nothing less.

> 📖 **New word: Instruction**
> An instruction is one small order. Example: "Open the door."

---

## 2. Example: making a sandwich 🥪

Imagine you have a robot.
You want the robot to make a sandwich.

You say: **"Make a sandwich."**

The robot does nothing. It does not know what a sandwich is!

So you must give **small, exact steps**:

1. Take two pieces of bread.
2. Put the bread on the plate.
3. Open the butter.
4. Take the knife.
5. Put butter on one piece of bread.
6. Put the other piece of bread on top.

Now the robot can make the sandwich. 🎉

🧠 **Remember:** A computer is like this robot. You must give it small, exact steps.

---

## 3. What is a program?

> 📖 **New word: Program**
> A program is a list of instructions for the computer.

Every app on your phone is a program:

- The camera app is a program.
- The calculator app is a program.
- A game is a program.

---

## 4. What is programming?

> 📖 **New word: Programming (also called Coding)**
> Programming means **writing** the instructions for a program.

A person who writes programs is called a **programmer** or a **developer**.

Soon, that will be **you**! 😊

---

## 5. Why a special language?

Computers do not understand English, Hindi, Spanish or any human language.

Human language is often **not clear**.

Example: *"I saw a man with a telescope."*

- Did **I** have the telescope?
- Or did the **man** have the telescope?

We don't know! A computer would be confused.

So we use a **programming language**.
A programming language is **very clear**. Every word has only **one** meaning.

> 📖 **New word: Programming language**
> A special language for writing instructions that a computer can follow.

There are many programming languages:

| Language | Often used for |
|---|---|
| **Swift** | iPhone, iPad and Mac apps |
| Kotlin | Android apps |
| Python | Data, science, AI |
| JavaScript | Websites |

In this tutorial, we learn **Swift**.

---

## 6. Order matters

The computer reads your instructions **from top to bottom**, one by one.

Look at these two lists:

**List A**
1. Put on socks.
2. Put on shoes.

**List B**
1. Put on shoes.
2. Put on socks.

Same steps, but **different order**.
List B is silly — socks over shoes! 🧦👟

🧠 **Remember:** In code, the **order** of lines is very important.

---

## 7. Mistakes are called "bugs"

> 📖 **New word: Bug**
> A bug is a mistake in a program.

> 📖 **New word: Debugging**
> Debugging means finding and fixing bugs.

Every programmer makes bugs. Every day. Even experts.
Making bugs is **normal**. Fixing them is how you learn.

---

## 🏋️ Practice

Take a paper. Write exact steps for a robot to **brush its teeth**.

Try to write at least **8 steps**.

Then read your steps. Ask yourself:
*"If the robot does exactly this, and nothing else, will it work?"*

Did you forget "open the toothpaste"? 😄

---

## 🧠 Remember

- A **program** is a list of instructions.
- **Programming** means writing those instructions.
- Computers need **exact** and **ordered** steps.
- We use **Swift** to make iPhone apps.
- Mistakes are called **bugs**, and they are normal.
